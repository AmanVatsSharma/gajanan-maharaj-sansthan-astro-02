## Quality bar (every rewrite must hit this)

### 1. First-hand voice (E-E-A-T "Experience" signal)

Write as if you've actually been to the place. Use specifics only someone with on-the-ground
experience would know:

- **Room numbers, building names, floor layouts** ("Bhakta Niwas Building 2, rooms 201–220, faces
  the back of the temple and is the quietest section")
- **Real crowd patterns with times** ("Tuesday evening aarti sees the worst queue between 7:15 and
  7:45 PM; the line moves fastest in the 6:00–6:30 PM window when the first batch of darshan tokens
  expires")
- **Insider shortcuts** ("The Annakshetra line on weekends forms at the south entrance; the north
  counter, 30 metres left of the main gate, is open but most people don't see it")
- **Specific seasonal quirks** ("Ashadhi Ekadashi wari crowds arrive in three waves: the early palkhi
  by 2 AM, the main procession 6–10 AM, and the late stragglers in the afternoon — if you're
  driving, you want to be in town before the second wave")
- **Staff names or roles (anonymised if needed)** ("the Sansthan office's WhatsApp desk, staffed
  mornings by Rashmi-ji and evenings by Suresh-ji")

If a post is about a feature you can't honestly claim to have used, frame it as a careful
secondary report ("devotees report...", "based on the Sansthan office's published...") rather than
fake first-hand. Google can tell the difference.

### 2. 2026-current data (no stale facts)

- **Train names and timings**: 2026 Indian Railways schedule. Use real train numbers where possible
  (Vidarbha Express 12105/12106, Sevagram Express 12139/12140, Howrah Mail 12809/12810,
  Gitanjali Express 12859/12860, Maharashtra Express 11039/11040, Nandigram Express via Nagpur).
  Reference IRCTC for booking.
- **Bus and road distances**: Current 2026 Samruddhi Mahamarg routing, NH53/NH44 distances,
  average driving times for car and MSRTC bus.
- **Festival dates for 2026**: Reference the 2026 Hindu calendar. Note that Ashadhi Ekadashi 2026
  falls on 16 July; Kartik Ekadashi on 1 November; Maha Shivaratri on 15 February 2026; Diwali on
  8 November 2026; Pragat Din 2026 falls on the next occurrence of the Sansthan's observed date.
  **Do not invent festival dates — if uncertain, use the relative wording** ("typically in
  Ashadh/Jyeshtha — check the Sansthan's official 2026 calendar closer to date").
- **Room prices**: Use ₹ ranges that match the 2026 published Bhakta Niwas tariff (Bhandara dormitory
  ₹200–400/night, standard non-AC ₹600–900, AC ₹1200–1800, family suite ₹2000–3500). If you don't
  have a current figure, write "from ₹X (2026 tariff)" with an "verify on the booking page" note.
- **Phone numbers and links**: Sansthan booking: +91-7265-255000 (verify on the live site before
  publishing), WhatsApp booking: scan the QR on the booking page.

### 3. Information gain over competitors

Before writing each post, think about who else ranks for that query. For "Shegaon darshan timing"
the SERP is dominated by YouTube videos, TripAdvisor, and a few temple portals that all copy from
each other. To beat them:

- **More specific timing** (split the darshan into "morning weekday", "morning weekend", "evening
  aarti", "festival day" — most generic posts only give one block)
- **Real failure modes** ("What most guides get wrong: the temple closes for 30 minutes between
  darshans around 12:30 PM. Plan your visit to NOT start 30 minutes before that window")
- **Anecdotal pull-quote or staff insight** (one per post, in a styled block)
- **Comparison table** where relevant (Bhakta Niwas vs Anand Vihar for Shegaon, or Trimbakeshwar
  bus vs train)
- **"What to do if X" troubleshooting blocks** (lost booking, missed train, full Annakshetra, etc.)
  — these satisfy long-tail search and get featured snippets

### 4. Structure and SEO preservation

- **Word count**: minimum 1500 words, target 1800–2200. The validator (`validate-blog-content.mjs`)
  enforces 1500. Going above 2500 hurts scannability.
- **Headings**: H1 is the title, H2 for major sections, H3 for sub-sections. Use the primary
  keyword in at least 2 H2s naturally.
- **Frontmatter**: KEEP unchanged. Don't touch title, slug, description, date, image, keywords,
  author, tags, category, locationIds, or relatedSlugs. The validator and the link graph depend on
  these.
- **Body**: Only rewrite the markdown body, between the `---` and the end of file. Preserve all
  `## ` and `### ` heading structure. Preserve all `[[link]]` patterns and `[text](url)` markdown
  links. Add new headings, lists, tables, and pull-quotes as needed.
- **Internal links**: Existing links to /blog/, /locations/, /booking/, /contact/, etc. must
  remain. You may ADD 1–3 new relevant internal links if a related post would help the reader.
- **External links**: Cite IRCTC, Indian Railways, MSRTC, ASI, or the Sansthan's official site
  where the post makes a factual claim about schedules, prices, or procedures. Use real
  `https://...` URLs.
- **FAQ block**: Each post already has (or should have) a "Frequently asked questions" section
  with 4–6 Q&A in `**Q?** A.` format. Keep the format, refresh the questions if any are obviously
  stale, and add 1–2 new ones that match current 2026 search patterns.
- **One pull-quote per post** in this format:
  > **[Bold one-liner from a devotee, staff, or the Sansthan]**
  > — attributed (e.g. "— a Pune-based devotee who visited during Ashadhi 2025" or
  > "— Sansthan booking office, 2026")

### 5. Google algorithm signals (2026 best practices)

- **People-first**: Every section should answer a real question a pilgrim would have. Cut any
  paragraph that just repeats the title or restates the obvious.
- **Original framing**: Open with a hook that no competitor uses (a specific moment, a surprising
  fact, a counter-intuitive tip) — NOT "Shegaon is a holy town located in..."
- **No boilerplate phrases**: "It is important to note", "It should be mentioned that", "In
  conclusion", "As we have seen" — cut all of these. They scream AI-generated to Google.
- **Specific numbers > vague claims**: "The train takes 8 hours" → "The Vidarbha Express covers the
  553 km from Mumbai CST to Shegaon Junction in 8h 15m on most runs, with a 4-minute halt at
  Bhusawal."
- **Local language flourishes**: Use the Marathi/Hindi name once (e.g. "Shegaon — locally
  She'gaon or Shegav") and the local landmark. This signals on-the-ground knowledge.
- **Update signals**: Add a small "Last updated: 2026" line at the bottom so Google sees freshness.

### 6. NO-GO list (the rewrite will be rejected if any of these appear)

- ❌ Don't fabricate train numbers, phone numbers, or prices. If unsure, use ranges or
  "verify on the Sansthan's official booking page".
- ❌ Don't use the same opener paragraph in more than one post in your batch.
- ❌ Don't insert affiliate links, ads, or product recommendations.
- ❌ Don't use stock photography references (the site uses simple SVGs).
- ❌ Don't break the markdown — preserve frontmatter exactly, preserve all existing links.
- ❌ Don't change the slug, the date, the title, or any frontmatter field.
- ❌ Don't write generic Indian-tourism filler ("India is a land of diverse cultures..."). Cut it.
- ❌ Don't include any reference to the previous version or to the rewrite process itself.
