# Bhakta Niwas Frequently Asked Questions | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

The Sansthan digital desk fields about 200 booking and planning queries a week, and roughly 60% of them are the same 15–20 questions. This FAQ is the canonical 2026 reference, written from the actual questions the desk has answered in the past year, with the answers verified against the current 2026 Bhakta Niwas tariff and the 2026 Sansthan calendar. If you have read this and still have a question, the WhatsApp booking desk is the fastest channel — typically a 2–4 hour response during working hours.

## The 2026 Bhakta Niwas tariff

**What are the typical charges for Bhakta Niwas rooms?** The 2026 Bhakta Niwas tariff (all-inclusive) is:

- Bhandara dormitory: ₹200–400 per bed per night
- Standard non-AC double: ₹600–900 per night
- Standard AC double: ₹1,250–1,800 per night
- Family suite: ₹2,000–3,500 per night
- Deluxe four-bed: ₹3,500–4,150 per night (Shegaon, Omkareshwar, Pandharpur only)

The Sansthan office confirms the live rate at booking. The same rate applies at all four locations (Shegaon, Omkareshwar, Pandharpur, Trimbakeshwar), with minor local variations.

## Booking and check-in

**What is the check-in and check-out policy at Shegaon?** Standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Most Sansthan accommodations offer a 24-hour check-out cycle from the time of entry, allowing flexibility based on your arrival. Late check-out (up to 6:00 PM) is available on request, subject to availability, and may incur a half-day charge.

**Is there an online booking portal for Sansthan accommodation?** The Sansthan's official [booking request page](/booking) is the only legitimate online channel. Be wary of third-party sites claiming to offer "online booking" of Bhakta Niwas rooms — they are not authorised and frequently overcharge by 20–40%.

**Can I book a room for just one person?** Yes. Bhandara dormitory facilities are available for solo devotees at all four locations, and standard non-AC single-occupancy rooms are also bookable. ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence) is mandatory regardless of group size.

**How far in advance should I book?** Standard non-festival dates need 7–14 days lead time. Festival windows (Pragat Din, Maha Shivaratri, Ashadhi Ekadashi, Kartik Ekadashi, Diwali) need 4–8 weeks. Verify exact festival dates on the Sansthan's 2026 calendar.

**Do I need to pay a deposit?** Most standard non-festival bookings do not require a deposit. AC rooms, family suites, and festival-window bookings may require a 25% deposit via UPI to the official Sansthan account. The deposit is refundable per the cancellation policy.

## Meals and food

**Are meals provided at the temple accommodation?** The rooms themselves do not have room service, but the Sansthan operates the Annakshetra (community kitchen) at all four locations, serving free Satvik meals — breakfast, lunch, and dinner — regardless of where you stay. The Mahaprasad Hall at Shegaon, the Bhakta Niwas canteens at Omkareshwar and Trimbakeshwar, and the Pundalik canteen at Pandharpur offer additional paid options.

**Is the food strictly Satvik?** Yes. The Annakshetra serves Satvik vegetarian food (no onion, no garlic) at Shegaon, Trimbakeshwar, and Pandharpur. The Omkareshwar Annakshetra serves a slightly more flexible Satvik menu that includes onion and garlic on Sundays and festivals — verify on arrival.

**Are Jain meals available?** Yes, at all four locations, but they must be requested at least 24 hours in advance via the WhatsApp booking desk or the duty manager.

## Family and children

**Can my children stay free?** Children under 5 stay free. Children 5–12 are charged 50% of the adult rate. Children 12 and above are charged the full rate. An extra mattress for a child is provided on request at no charge.

**Are cots or extra beds available?** Yes, extra mattresses (folded beds with bedding) are available at all four properties at no extra charge for children or for adults sharing a room. Request at booking.

**Is the Bhakta Niwas suitable for senior citizens?** Yes. The properties have lift access (except Visawa at Shegaon and the older Trimbakeshwar block), ramps, and ground-floor rooms are available on request. The Annakshetra is wheelchair-accessible at all four locations.

## Festival and special visits

**When is the cheapest time to visit?** April through mid-July is the off-season. Bhakta Niwas rates are at their lowest (no festival surcharge), walk-ins are easy, and the darshan queue is the shortest of the year. The trade-off is the heat in April–May and the monsoon in June–July.

**Can I attend a festival darshan as a non-resident guest?** Yes. Festival darshan is open to all visitors, regardless of where you stay. The Sansthan darshan tokens are free and issued at the temple gate.

**What is the 2026 Maha Shivaratri date?** Maha Shivaratri falls on 15 February 2026. The Shegaon and Trimbakeshwar Sansthan properties see the heaviest bookings 5 days on either side. Book 4–6 weeks ahead for this window.

**What is the 2026 Ashadhi Ekadashi date?** Ashadhi Ekadashi falls on 16 July 2026. Pandharpur's Bhakta Niwas books out 6–8 weeks ahead for this window. The Varkari Wari processions arrive in three waves — early palkhi by 2:00 AM, main procession 6:00–10:00 AM, late stragglers in the afternoon.

## Logistics and connectivity

**Is parking available?** Yes, at all four properties. Shegaon has a marked 60-vehicle lot, Anand Vihar has space for 30 cars, Omkareshwar has a 20-vehicle lot, Pandharpur has a 50-vehicle lot, and Trimbakeshwar has a 25-vehicle lot. During festival days, parking is coordinated by Sansthan volunteers, and arriving before 9:00 AM helps.

**Is Wi-Fi available?** Free Wi-Fi is available at the Bhakta Niwas reception areas and in the Anand Vihar dining hall. Signal strength varies by room, especially at the Omkareshwar property's lake-facing side. Mobile data (Jio, Airtel, Vi) works at all four locations.

**Are power backup facilities in place?** Yes. All four properties have generator backup for common areas and emergency lighting in rooms. The Annakshetra operates on backup power during outages to ensure meal service is uninterrupted.

## Multi-city yatra

**Can I book all four properties in a single request?** Yes. Submit a single [booking request](/booking) with your multi-city itinerary, and the Sansthan office will allocate rooms at each stop. Allow 5–7 working days for multi-property confirmations during peak season.

**What is the best multi-city circuit?** The Shegaon + Trimbakeshwar + Omkareshwar circuit is the most popular 7–9 day pattern. For Wari season, the Pandharpur-centric 3–4 day plan is the most common. For a comprehensive plan, see the [Week-Long Devotional Circuit Planner](/blog/week-long-devotional-circuit-planner).

**Can I get a single invoice for a multi-city stay?** Yes. The Sansthan office can issue a single consolidated invoice covering all four properties, useful for tax purposes or for NRI devotees who want a single receipt.

## Refunds and cancellations

**What is the cancellation policy?** Standard non-festival bookings can be cancelled up to 48 hours before check-in with a full deposit refund. Festival-window bookings have a stricter 7-day cancellation window. No-shows are charged the full first-night rate. See the [Bhakta Niwas Refund and Cancellation Policy](/blog/bhakta-niwas-refund-and-cancellation-policy) for the current terms.

**What if I need to extend my stay mid-visit?** Walk to the front desk and ask for the duty manager. Extensions are usually possible, especially at Shegaon and Pandharpur, and the same tariff applies. At Trimbakeshwar, the smaller property may not have extension availability during peak season.

## For NRI and international devotees

**Is there a help-desk for English-speaking devotees?** Yes, at Shegaon year-round, and at the other three properties during the December–January peak. The Shegaon help-desk is staffed by volunteers who can assist with translation, transport, and medical coordination.

**Can I pay in foreign currency?** Yes, at Shegaon. The Bhakta Niwas reception accepts USD, EUR, and GBP at the prevailing bank rate, but UPI is the preferred method.

**Is the food suitable for international visitors?** The Satvik menu is mild and vegetarian. International visitors occasionally find it repetitive for stays longer than 3 days; the Omkareshwar and Pandharpur canteens have a slightly more varied menu. The Anand Vihar property at Shegaon has a paid South-Indian breakfast counter that adds variety.
