# Complete Transport Guide for Devotees | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

There is a moment every pilgrim knows well: you have decided to visit a holy place, your heart is set on the darshan, and then logistics sets in. How do you actually get there? The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan operates four locations across Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, and each one demands a different transport strategy. Shegaon, the Samadhi Mandir in Buldhana district, has rail but no commercial airport. Omkareshwar on the Narmada requires a train to Khandwa and a road connection. Pandharpur on the Chandrabhaga River sits on a branch line with limited direct access. Trimbakeshwar near Nashik is the most accessible from Mumbai but still needs a final taxi leg. This guide covers every realistic route with specific train numbers, bus operators, taxi costs, driving distances, and practical tips drawn from what the Sansthan office regularly shares with devotees calling for transport assistance.

## Reaching Shegaon

Shegaon sits in the Buldhana district of Vidarbha, roughly 580 km northeast of Mumbai on NH 53, 240 km west of Nagpur, and about 45 km south of Akola town. The town has its own railway station on the Howrah-Mumbai main line, which is the single most important piece of transport infrastructure for devotees. Shegaon does not have a commercial airport; the nearest one is at Akola, about 45 km away, reachable in roughly an hour by taxi.

**From Mumbai by train.** The 12139 Sewagram Express is the workhorse route for Mumbai devotees. It departs Mumbai CST at 8:55 PM and reaches Shegaon at 8:00 AM the next morning — an overnight journey of about 11 hours covering 580 km. Second Sitting, Sleeper Class, AC 3-Tier, and AC 2-Tier are all available. The 12105 Vidarbha Express, departing Mumbai CST at 6:50 PM, arrives Shegaon around 10:00 PM. The 12809 Howrah Mail is another option, departing at 8:40 PM. Book tickets 60 days in advance on IRCTC, and book earlier if you are travelling during Pragat Din or Samadhi Utsav, when demand surges dramatically.

**From Nagpur.** The road distance is about 240 km and takes 4 to 5 hours by car. The same 12139 Sewagram Express runs from Nagpur to Shegaon in about 4 to 5 hours. A private taxi from Nagpur costs approximately INR 2,000 to 4,000 depending on the vehicle type. Shared cars are a practical budget option for solo travellers.

**From Pune.** There is no direct train. The most practical route is to take a train from Pune to Khamgaon or Jalna — about 5 to 6 hours — and then a local bus or taxi from Khamgaon to Shegaon, roughly 50 km and an hour. An overnight MSRTC bus from Pune to Akola or Khamgaon is another option.

**From Nashik.** The road distance is about 280 km via Igatpuri, Bhusawal, and Jalgaon, taking 5 to 6 hours by car. Bus connections exist from Nashik to Khamgaon but with limited frequency. A private taxi is the most reliable option from Nashik.

**By bus from Mumbai.** Private operators including VRL Travels, Paulo Travels, and Orange Tours run overnight AC sleeper buses from Mumbai to Shegaon, typically departing around 8:00 PM on Fridays and arriving by 7:00 AM on Saturdays. Return buses leave Shegaon on Sundays. Tickets are available on the operator websites and apps, and booking 2 to 3 weeks ahead is advisable during festival seasons.

## Reaching Omkareshwar

Omkareshwar lies on the Mandhata island in the Narmada River, in Khandwa district of Madhya Pradesh. It is approximately 340 km from Shegaon via Khandwa. The nearest railway station is Khandwa Junction, about 30 km from Omkareshwar (roughly 45 minutes by taxi). The nearest commercial airport is at Indore, about 80 km away and roughly 2 hours by taxi.

**From Mumbai.** Mumbai to Khandwa is approximately 600 km. The 12961 Avantika Express from Mumbai Central to Indore stops at Khandwa, with the journey taking approximately 8 to 9 hours. From Khandwa, a taxi to Omkareshwar is 30 km and 45 minutes. Alternatively, driving directly from Mumbai to Omkareshwar via Nashik and Khandwa covers about 620 km and takes 10 to 11 hours.

**From Nagpur.** The distance is about 450 km, with trains and cars both taking 7 to 8 hours. The 12659 Gitanjali Express stops at Khandwa, making it a viable train option for Nagpur devotees.

**From Indore.** This is the most convenient route for devotees flying into India. Indore to Omkareshwar is 80 km and about 2 hours by taxi or bus. Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport in Indore has flights from Mumbai, Delhi, and most major Indian cities.

**By bus from Indore.** MP Tourism runs AC and non-AC buses throughout the day, with a journey time of 2 to 2.5 hours. Private operators offer similar services. Tickets are available on the MP Tourism website and through RedBus.

## Reaching Pandharpur

Pandharpur sits on the Chandrabhaga River in Solapur district, Maharashtra. It is approximately 320 km from Shegaon and 200 km from Pune. The nearest railway station is Pandharpur (PBN), on the Miraj-Solapur branch line. The nearest commercial airport is at Solapur, approximately 80 km away, or Pune, about 200 km away.

**From Mumbai.** The distance is about 400 km and 7 to 8 hours by car. The most practical train route is Mumbai CST to Miraj Junction (by the 11023 Mumbai-Shatabdi Express or 11025 Deccan Queen), then a taxi or local bus from Miraj to Pandharpur, which is 60 km and about 1.5 hours. There is also a direct Mumbai CST to Pandharpur Express that runs a few days a week. Always check the current schedule on IRCTC before planning.

**From Pune.** The distance is 200 km and 4 to 5 hours by car. Direct MSRTC and private buses run from Pune to Pandharpur, taking approximately 5 hours. This is the most convenient route for Pune devotees.

**From Solapur.** Just 80 km and 1.5 to 2 hours by car or taxi. Multiple MSRTC buses run daily, making this the shortest and most practical route for devotees in the Solapur region.

**From Shegaon.** The road distance is about 320 km and 6 to 7 hours via Khamgaon, Mehkar, Karanja, and Wainganga Road. There is no direct train or bus; a private taxi or car is the only practical option. This leg is a significant drive, so plan for a full day of travel and an overnight stop if possible.

Pandharpur comes alive during Ashadhi Ekadashi, when hundreds of thousands of Warkaris walk the Wari route to the temple. If you are visiting during that period, see our [Ashadhi Ekadashi Pandharpur Wari guide](/blog/ashadhi-ekadashi-pandharpur-wari-guide) for the full picture of what to expect.

## Reaching Trimbakeshwar

Trimbakeshwar is near Nashik in Maharashtra, about 180 km from Mumbai via NH 160. The nearest railway station is Nasik Road, roughly 30 km from Trimbakeshwar (about 45 minutes by taxi). Nashik Airport is about 40 km away and roughly an hour by road.

**From Mumbai.** The distance is about 180 km and 4 hours by car via Igatpuri, Nashik, and Trimbakeshwar Road. Frequent MSRTC and private buses run from Mumbai (Thane, Dadar) to Nashik. From Nashik, a taxi to Trimbakeshwar covers the final 30 km in about 45 minutes. The train from Mumbai CST to Nasik Road takes approximately 3 hours on the 12115/12116 Godavari Superfast Express or the 11057/11058 Mumbai CSMT-Amravati Express.

**From Nashik.** Trimbakeshwar is 30 km and 45 minutes by taxi or local bus. MSRTC runs regular buses from Nashik CBS to Trimbakeshwar at intervals of roughly 30 to 45 minutes during peak hours.

**From Pune.** The distance is about 360 km and 6 to 7 hours by car via the Mumbai-Nashik highway. Alternatively, take a train from Pune to Nasik Road (approximately 4 hours) and then a taxi to Trimbakeshwar.

## Practical Transport Tips

**Book trains early.** IRCTC opens bookings 60 days in advance. During Pragat Din (February) and Samadhi Utsav (August-September), Sleeper and AC tickets can sell out within hours of the booking window opening. Set a calendar reminder for the exact date when your travel window opens for booking.

**Confirm bus schedules before relying on them.** Some of the less frequent routes, particularly Pune to Khamgaon and Nashik to Khamgaon, have limited services that may not align with your preferred travel time. Have a backup plan, such as a taxi, if the bus does not fit your schedule.

**Avoid app-based cabs for long-distance inter-city routes.** Services like Ola and Uber work well between Mumbai and Nashik or Mumbai and Pune, but do not have dependable coverage to Shegaon, Omkareshwar, or Pandharpur. Book through a local taxi operator or a registered travel agency. If you need help with transport coordination, reach out via the [contact page](/contact).

**Allow buffer time on festival days.** During Pragat Din and Samadhi Utsav, the Sansthan arranges special trains and buses, but they are crowded and timetables can shift. Arrive at the station or bus stop at least 30 minutes earlier than you normally would. See our [festival rush booking strategies](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) for a complete preparation checklist.

**Book accommodation before finalising transport.** There is no point in booking a train to Shegaon for Pragat Din if the Bhakta Niwas is already full. Check availability on the [booking page](/booking) first, then lock in your transport. Room allocations are typically confirmed 30 to 60 days before the festival, and the best rooms go first.

**Carry the Sansthan contact number.** Save +91 80531 90691 in your phone before you travel. The Sansthan office can provide real-time guidance on transport disruptions, special festival arrangements, and last-minute booking options. You can also message the same number on WhatsApp for quick responses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which is the most reliable train from Mumbai to Shegaon?**

A: The 12139 Sewagram Express is the most consistent option, departing Mumbai CST at 8:55 PM and arriving Shegaon at 8:00 AM. It runs daily and has all major seating classes. The 12105 Vidarbha Express is a solid alternative.

**Q: Can I drive from Mumbai to all four locations in one trip?**

A: Yes, but the full circuit is roughly 1,820 km and requires at least 7 to 8 days. Plan for 230 to 260 km of driving per day, which is comfortable. The [week-long devotional circuit planner](/blog/week-long-devotional-circuit-planner) covers the full route with daily stops.

**Q: Are there direct buses from Mumbai to Omkareshwar?**

A: No direct Mumbai to Omkareshwar bus. Take the bus or train to Indore or Khandwa, then transfer to a local bus or taxi for the final 30 to 80 km.

**Q: How far in advance should I book a taxi for the full circuit?**

A: Book 3 to 4 weeks ahead for the full circuit. For individual city-to-city transfers, 1 to 2 weeks is sufficient. During festival periods, book 6 to 8 weeks in advance, as taxi operators get booked up early.

**Q: Is there a self-drive option?**

A: Self-drive cars from operators like Zoomcar and Revv are available in Mumbai, Pune, and Nashik. The road conditions on the Shegaon-Omkareshwar and Omkareshwar-Pandharpur stretches are mostly good, though some rural sections have narrow lanes. Self-drive gives maximum flexibility, but ensure the driver is comfortable with long inter-state drives.

**Q: What about parking at the temple and Bhakta Niwas?**

A: All Bhakta Niwas campuses have parking for guests. At the temple itself, parking is available but fills up quickly during festivals. Arrive early and keep your room booking confirmation handy if you are staying at the Bhakta Niwas.
