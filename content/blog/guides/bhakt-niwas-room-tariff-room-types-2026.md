# Bhakt Niwas Room Tariff 2026: Room Types, Prices & How to Book

If you are planning a visit to Shegaon for darshan, staying at Bhakt Niwas — the Sansthan's own pilgrim accommodation — is the most convenient option. The rooms are located within walking distance of the Samadhi Mandir, the pricing is subsidized by the Sansthan, and the atmosphere is quiet and devotional. This guide covers the 2026 room types, the tariff (room charges), how to book, and practical tips for peak periods.

## Why stay at Bhakt Niwas?

Most devotees who visit Shegaon for a one- or two-day visit do not need a hotel — a day trip from Nagpur (about 3 hours) or Akola (about 2 hours) is feasible. But if you are arriving in the evening, planning to attend the morning darshan, or staying for a festival like Pragat Din or Samadhi Utsav, on-site accommodation at Bhakt Niwas removes the need to commute from a distant hotel.

The advantages over nearby hotels are straightforward: proximity to the mandir, meals included, the devotional atmosphere, and the subsidized rates. The disadvantages are also real: rooms are basic, hot water may be limited, and the demand far exceeds supply during peak festivals.

## Room types and 2026 tariffs

The Sansthan publishes room categories that change occasionally. The following rates are current as of early 2026 and reflect the subsidized pricing for devotees. Prices may be revised — confirm with the Sansthan office or the online booking portal before finalizing.

### Dormitory / hall accommodation

**Type:** Shared hall with bunk beds or cots for 4–8 people. Shared bathroom.
**Tariff:** Around ₹200–₹300 per person per night (not a private room — suitable for budget groups and solo travelers who are comfortable with shared space).

### Basic room (non-AC, shared bathroom)

**Type:** Simple non-AC room, typically twin or triple sharing. Common bathroom on the corridor.
**Tariff:** Around ₹500–₹800 per room per night, depending on occupancy and whether the booking is made during a festival period.

### Non-AC room (private bathroom)

**Type:** Non-AC room with attached bathroom. Single or double occupancy.
**Tariff:** Around ₹800–₹1,200 per room per night. This is the most popular category for families.

### Deluxe non-AC room

**Type:** Spacious non-AC room with attached bathroom, TV, and basic furnishings. Suitable for small families (2 adults + 1–2 children).
**Tariff:** Around ₹1,200–₹1,800 per room per night.

### AC room

**Type:** Air-conditioned room with attached bathroom. Single or double occupancy. The AC room category is in highest demand during summer months.
**Tariff:** Around ₹1,500–₹2,500 per room per night.

### Suite / family room

**Type:** Larger suite suitable for extended families. Typically includes a sitting area. Subject to availability — only a small number of suites exist.
**Tariff:** Around ₹2,000–₹3,500 per room per night. Confirm availability before planning around this category.

> **Note on accuracy:** Room tariffs are set by the Sansthan and subject to periodic revision. The ranges above are based on recent booking data and the Sansthan's published rates. Always confirm current rates at the booking counter or the official portal before confirming.

## How to book

### Online booking

The Sansthan operates an online booking portal for Bhakt Niwas. Booking opens 30 to 45 days in advance for major festivals and 15 to 30 days for regular dates. The online booking process is straightforward:

1. Visit the Sansthan booking page.
2. Select room type, date, and number of guests.
3. Provide mobile number and ID.
4. Pay the advance amount (typically 20–30% of the tariff).
5. Receive a confirmation SMS. Carry this and a photo ID to the check-in counter.

### Offline booking

The Sansthan booking office at Shegaon accepts walk-in reservations on a first-come, first-served basis. Walk-in availability is unpredictable during festival periods — the rooms are typically sold out before the festival begins. Offline booking is most reliable for ordinary weekday visits.

### Phone booking

For inquiries and last-minute availability, call the Sansthan's booking helpline. This is often the fastest way to check if rooms are available during a festival period when the online portal is sold out.

## Booking tips for peak periods

**Pragat Din (February 12):** The most popular single day at Shegaon. Rooms are in demand from early January. Book 45 days in advance if possible. The Sansthan increases room allocation for this period but demand still exceeds supply.

**Samadhi Utsav (Rishi Panchami, August–September):** The second peak period. Book at least 30 days before the utsav starts. The ten-day festival period draws devotees from across Maharashtra and neighboring states.

**Weekends:** Even ordinary weekends are busy. If you are traveling on a Friday evening or Saturday morning, book at least two weeks in advance.

**Summer (April–June):** AC rooms sell out quickly in summer. Non-AC rooms remain available but the heat is significant. Consider visiting between October and February if you have flexibility.

## What to bring

The rooms are furnished with the basics — bed, table, chair, bathroom. Bring your own toiletries, any medications, and a small torch if you prefer not to use the corridor lighting at night. Mobile signal is generally good inside the Bhakta Niwas campus. Wi-Fi is not reliably available.

## Checking in and out

Check-in time is typically from 12 noon or 2 pm. Check-out is by 10 am. Arrive during office hours (the booking counter is open from 8 am to 8 pm). After check-in, a short walk to the Samadhi Mandir for evening darshan is the traditional first act for many devotees.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can I book a room for just a few hours for darshan?**
A: The Sansthan does not offer hourly booking. Minimum booking is typically one night. If you are on a day trip, consider commuting from Nagpur or Akola rather than booking a room.

**Q: Is the tariff refundable if I cancel?**
A: Cancellation and refund policies vary. Generally, cancellations made 15+ days before the check-in date receive a full refund of the advance, and cancellations within 5 days are non-refundable. Confirm the current policy at the time of booking.

**Q: Is food included in the room tariff?**
A: The tariff typically includes breakfast and dinner (simple sattvic meals). Lunch may be extra. During festival periods, annadan (free community meals) is available to all visitors.

**Q: Can unmarried or solo travelers book rooms?**
A: Yes. The Sansthan accommodates solo travelers. The dormitory option is the most affordable for solo visitors. Some family rooms can be shared — enquire at the booking counter.

**Q: How far is Bhakta Niwas from the Samadhi Mandir?**
A: The rooms are within the Sansthan campus, approximately 200–500 meters from the Samadhi Mandir. It is a 5 to 8 minute walk along paved paths within the complex.
