# Omkareshwar To Trimbakeshwar Pilgrimage Checklist | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

The journey from Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar is the central link in the four-location temple circuit. It connects the Narmada River Jyotirlinga in Madhya Pradesh with the Godavari River Jyotirlinga in Maharashtra — a transition from the Mandhata island's contemplative pace to the Sahyadri foothills' devotional intensity. The route covers approximately 420 kilometres, takes 8 to 9 hours by car, and passes through some of the most interesting terrain in the Maharashtra-Madhya Pradesh circuit: the Nimar plains, the Satmala hills, and the Godavari valley. This checklist covers everything you need to plan, prepare for, and execute this leg of the pilgrimage — from transport options and mid-journey stops to accommodation at Trimbakeshwar and the specific considerations that apply when you are travelling between two Jyotirlingas.

## Route overview

**Distance:** Approximately 420 kilometres

**Driving time:** 8 to 9 hours by car or taxi

**Best direction:** Omkareshwar to Khandwa to Burhanpur to Jalgaon Jamod to Aurangabad to Nashik to Trimbakeshwar

**Alternative direction:** Omkareshwar to Khandwa to Indore to Dhule to Nashik to Trimbakeshwar (approximately 500 km, 10 hours)

The recommended route follows the Khandwa-Burhanpur-Aurangabad-Nashik corridor via NH 753C, NH 753F, and NH 160. This route is well-maintained and passes through major towns with good facilities. The alternative via Indore is longer but offers the convenience of Indore's airport if you need to fly out from there.

## Pre-journey checklist

Before leaving Omkareshwar, make sure you have:

- [ ] **Booking confirmation for Trimbakeshwar accommodation.** Confirm your Bhakta Niwas or hotel reservation before departing Omkareshwar. If the Trimbakeshwar Bhakta Niwas is full, have a backup hotel in Nashik booked as well.
- [ ] **Transport arranged for the Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar leg.** If you are using a hired car or taxi for the full circuit, confirm that your driver is available for this leg and knows the pickup point at Omkareshwar.
- [ ] **Fuel the vehicle.** Fill up at Khandwa (the last major fuel station before Burhanpur) if you are driving yourself.
- [ ] **Cash for the journey.** ATMs are available at Khandwa and Burhanpur, but the rural stretches between Burhanpur and Nashik have limited ATM access. Carry enough cash for meals, tolls, and any emergency expenses.
- [ ] **Documents accessible.** Keep your driving licence, vehicle documents, and ID in the car's dashboard compartment for easy access at state border crossings.
- [ ] **Phone charged and power bank ready.** The hilly stretches between Burhanpur and Aurangabad have patchy mobile coverage. Keep your phone charged for navigation and emergency calls.
- [ ] **Snacks and water.** The 8 to 9 hour journey requires provisions for the road. Pack water bottles, fruits, and light snacks for the journey.

## The journey in segments

### Omkareshwar to Khandwa (30 km, 45 minutes)

Start from the mainland end of the Narmada bridge (where your taxi dropped you when you arrived at Omkareshwar). The 30-kilometre drive to Khandwa takes about 45 minutes on MP SH 27. Khandwa is a major railway junction and a good stop for fuel, breakfast, or last-minute supplies before the longer drive ahead.

### Khandwa to Burhanpur (140 km, 3 hours)

From Khandwa, the road heads northwest toward Burhanpur on NH 753C. This is a well-maintained stretch through the Nimar plains. Burhanpur is a historically significant town with Mughal architecture. It is a useful stopping point for lunch — several restaurants near the bus stand serve clean vegetarian food.

### Burhanpur to Jalgaon Jamod to Aurangabad (200 km, 4 hours)

From Burhanpur, the road continues west on NH 753F toward Aurangabad. The terrain becomes hillier as you approach the Ajanta range, and the road quality remains good. Aurangabad is a major city with extensive facilities and is the best place to stop for a proper meal break, a rest, or an overnight stay if you want to divide the journey into two days.

### Aurangabad to Nashik (220 km, 4 hours)

From Aurangabad, the road continues northwest on NH 160 toward Nashik. This is the longest single stretch of the Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar journey. The landscape transitions from the semi-arid Marathwada region to the greener Nashik valley. Stop at a dhaba near Sinnar for a meal break.

### Nashik to Trimbakeshwar (30 km, 45 minutes)

The final stretch from Nashik to Trimbakeshwar takes you through the Godavari valley and up to the Sahyadri foothills. The Brahmagiri hill becomes visible from the road in the final kilometres. Trimbakeshwar town is compact, and the Jyotirlinga temple is a short walk from the main road.

## Mid-journey stopping options

**One-day journey (recommended):** Start at 6:00 AM from Omkareshwar, stop for lunch at Burhanpur or Aurangabad, and arrive at Trimbakeshwar by 3:00 or 4:00 PM — giving you time for the evening darshan.

**Two-day journey:** Stop at Aurangabad on Day 1. Aurangabad has excellent hotels, restaurants, and the added option of visiting the Ellora or Ajanta caves. From Aurangabad, Trimbakeshwar is 4 hours away on Day 2.

**Two-day journey via Khandwa:** Stop at Khandwa on Day 1 (after a brief visit to Omkareshwar), and continue from Khandwa to Trimbakeshwar on Day 2. This divides the journey more evenly but requires a Khandwa hotel booking.

## Transport options

**By private taxi:** The most practical option. A one-way AC car from Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar costs approximately INR 4,500 to 5,500. An SUV costs approximately INR 5,500 to 6,500. If you are doing the full circuit (Shegaon-Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar), book a multi-leg taxi that covers all three legs — this is significantly more economical than booking each leg separately.

**By train and taxi:** There is no direct train from Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar. The train route would be Khandwa to Nasik Road (approximately 8 hours) and then a taxi from Nasik Road to Trimbakeshwar (45 minutes). This is longer and less convenient than a direct taxi.

**By bus:** No direct bus. You would need a bus from Khandwa to Aurangabad or Nashik, and then a local bus or taxi to Trimbakeshwar. This is a budget option but takes 12 to 14 hours total.

## Accommodation at Trimbakeshwar

**Bhakta Niwas (Sansthan):** Simple Non-AC accommodation near the Jyotirlinga temple. Book through +91 80531 90691 or the [booking form](/booking). The [accommodation comparison guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-across-locations-comparison) shows room types and tariffs.

**Hotels in Nashik:** A wide range of options from budget to luxury. The 30-kilometre commute to Trimbakeshwar takes 45 minutes. MSRTC buses run regularly from Nashik to Trimbakeshwar. This is the best option if Bhakta Niwas is full or if you prefer more comfortable accommodation.

**Budget hotels near Trimbakeshwar:** A handful of small hotels are available near the temple approach road. Ask at the temple or the Bhakta Niwas for recommendations.

## What to expect at Trimbakeshwar

Trimbakeshwar is the most compact of the four Sansthan locations. The Jyotirlinga temple is at the centre of the town, and the Brahmagiri hill rises immediately behind it. The darshan experience is intimate compared to Shegaon — the temple is smaller, the queues are shorter, and the spiritual atmosphere is more contemplative.

**Darshan timing:** The temple opens at approximately 4:00 AM and closes around 9:30 PM, with a break in the afternoon (typically 12:30 PM to 4:00 PM). The [darshan timing guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-darshan-timing-guide) covers the complete daily schedule.

**Godavari ritual:** Many devotees visit the Godavari spring at the base of Brahmagiri hill for the ritual dip. The spring water flows from the hill and is considered sacred. Wear clothes that you do not mind getting wet, and carry a separate set of dry clothes to change into after the ritual.

**Brahmagiri walk:** The walk from the temple to the Godavari spring and back takes approximately 1 hour. The path is well-maintained but involves some uphill walking. Wear comfortable shoes.

## Pre-departure from Omkareshwar

Before leaving Omkareshwar, complete these tasks:

- [ ] **Return any library or borrowed items** to the Bhakta Niwas or local contacts.
- [ ] **Settle any outstanding bills** at your accommodation.
- [ ] **Collect your luggage** from storage (if you used it during the Omkareshwar stay).
- [ ] **Confirm your onward transport** — taxi, bus, or train — with the driver or operator.
- [ ] **Check the Trimbakeshwar accommodation status** — call +91 80531 90691 to confirm that your booking is still active.
- [ ] **Leave feedback** with the Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas staff if your stay was particularly good or if there were issues.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Which is the better route — via Burhanpur or via Indore?**

A: Via Burhanpur (Khandwa-Burhanpur-Aurangabad-Nashik) is the shorter route at approximately 420 km and 8 to 9 hours. Via Indore is approximately 500 km and 10 hours but offers the convenience of Indore Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport for devotees flying in or out. Choose based on whether you need the airport connection.

**Q: Can I visit Ellora or Ajanta caves on the way?**

A: Yes. The Ellora caves are 30 km from Aurangabad, and the Ajanta caves are approximately 100 km. Both are accessible from the Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar route via Aurangabad. Plan for 4 to 6 additional hours for the cave visits. The [Shegaon and Ajanta Ellora combined trip guide](/blog/shegaon-and-ajanta-ellora-combined-trip) covers this option.

**Q: Is the Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar leg the longest in the circuit?**

A: The Omkareshwar to Pandharpur leg (approximately 480 km) is slightly longer than the Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar leg (approximately 420 km). However, the Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar leg takes longer because of the hilly terrain and narrower roads in some sections.

**Q: Should I stop overnight between Omkareshwar and Trimbakeshwar?**

A: If you want a relaxed pace, stopping at Aurangabad for the night is the best option. Aurangabad has excellent hotels and restaurants, and the Ellora and Ajanta caves are nearby if you want to extend your spiritual itinerary. If you prefer to complete the journey in one day, start at 6:00 AM and plan for an 8-hour drive with meal breaks.

**Q: What is the road quality like?**

A: The road quality is good throughout. The NH stretches (NH 753C, NH 753F, and NH 160) are well-maintained. Some rural sections near Burhanpur and Jalna have narrower lanes, but they are serviceable for standard cars and taxis. The Nashik to Trimbakeshwar stretch is in excellent condition.

**Q: Can I do the Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar leg by train?**

A: There is no direct train. The train route would be Khandwa to Nasik Road (approximately 8 hours) and then a taxi to Trimbakeshwar (45 minutes). This is longer and less convenient than the direct taxi option. The train option is only worth considering if you are specifically interested in the train journey experience.
