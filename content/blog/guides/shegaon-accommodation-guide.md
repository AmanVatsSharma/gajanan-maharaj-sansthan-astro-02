# Shegaon Accommodation Guide

Shegaon offers a wide range of Sansthan-managed accommodation options, from low-cost dharamshala rooms ideal for solo yatris to A/C family suites suitable for multi-generational groups. The three principal Sansthan properties are **Bhakt Niwas** (the main pilgrim stay near the Mandir), **Anand Vihar** (a quieter garden-style property), and **Visawa** (a small dharamshala for short stays and overflow). This guide compares the three, walks you through the booking process, and addresses the most common accommodation questions from devotees.

## Bhakt Niwas — the main pilgrim stay

Bhakt Niwas is the largest Sansthan-managed accommodation block, located within a 5-minute walk of the Samadhi Mandir. The property offers:

- **Bhandara rooms** (dormitory-style, low cost, ideal for solo yatris and small groups)
- **Standard rooms** (twin or triple occupancy, attached bath, family-friendly)
- **A/C suites** (limited inventory, advance booking essential during festival season)
- **Common dining area** with tea/coffee and a small snack counter
- **Lockers** for valuables near the front desk
- **Wheelchair access** to all floors via lift

Bhakt Niwas is the right choice for most first-time family visitors and for devotees who want to be within walking distance of the Mandir for early-morning darshan.

## Anand Vihar — a quieter garden-style property

Anand Vihar is a more spacious Sansthan property set in a large garden complex, around 2 km from the Mandir. It is a good fit for:

- **Families with young children** who want outdoor space
- **Longer-stay visitors** (3+ days) who appreciate a calmer environment
- **Groups** who want shared dining and small meeting spaces
- **Devotees who plan a day at Anand Sagar** — the property is adjacent

Anand Vihar has a small on-site kitchen, a meditation hall, and a path-walk that many senior citizens find restorative. The trade-off is that it is 2 km from the Mandir, so a morning auto-rickshaw is recommended for the early aarti.

## Visawa — a small dharamshala for short stays

Visawa is a small Sansthan dharamshala that primarily serves as overflow accommodation during festival periods. It is suitable for:

- **Short overnight stays** (1–2 nights)
- **Festival overflow** when Bhakt Niwas and Anand Vihar are fully booked
- **Group bookings** (10+ people) on prior arrangement

Visawa is the most basic of the three but is also the lowest-cost, and the volunteer staff are known for being particularly welcoming to first-time visitors who arrive without a reservation.

## How to book

The accommodation is coordinated centrally by the Sansthan office, regardless of which property you choose. The recommended booking flow is:

1. **Submit a booking request** via the [booking page](/booking) with your travel dates, group size, and any specific needs (senior citizens, children, dietary preferences, accessibility).
2. **Confirm via WhatsApp or phone** — the Sansthan office typically responds within a few hours with availability and pricing. For step-by-step support, see the [Bhakta Niwas Complete Booking Guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) and the [Phone and WhatsApp Booking Best Practices](/blog/phone-and-whatsapp-booking-best-practices) articles.
3. **Carry a printed or mobile copy** of the booking confirmation on arrival. The front desk will request it at check-in.
4. **Arrive within the check-in window** (typically 12:00 to 20:00). For very early or very late arrivals, notify the office in advance so the front desk can prepare.

## Pricing and payment

Sansthan accommodation is offered on a no-profit basis, with rates significantly below comparable commercial hotels. The exact pricing varies by room type, season, and festival window. The current indicative pricing (as of 2026) is:

- **Bhandara rooms:** ₹200–400 per night per person
- **Standard rooms:** ₹800–1500 per night (double occupancy)
- **A/C suites:** ₹1800–3500 per night (double occupancy)

These are indicative; the Sansthan office will confirm the exact rate at the time of booking. Payment is accepted at the property (cash, UPI, or card) and a receipt is provided. Pilgrims who cannot afford the standard rates can request a subsidized stay via the front desk — the Sansthan does not turn away devotees for inability to pay.

## Best time to book

The Bhakt Niwas fills up weeks in advance during major festival periods. If your visit coincides with:

- **Pragat Din** — book 6–8 weeks ahead
- **Punyatithi Aradhana** — book 6–8 weeks ahead
- **Maha Shivaratri** — book 4–6 weeks ahead
- **Ganesh Chaturthi** — book 3–4 weeks ahead
- **Regular season (October–March)** — book 1–2 weeks ahead
- **Off-season (April–June, monsoon)** — walk-ins are usually possible, but booking ahead is still recommended

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Bhakt Niwas suitable for senior citizens?** Yes. The property has lift access, ramps, and a 5-minute walking path to the Mandir. Wheelchair users can navigate the corridors; the inner sanctum path is partially stepped.

**Are meals included in the room rate?** No. The Annakshetra serves free meals to all visitors, and a small paid snack counter operates at the Bhakt Niwas. Most families find this arrangement works well.

**Is there parking on site?** Limited parking is available at all three properties. During peak festival days, parking is coordinated by volunteers, and arriving early helps.

**Can we book a partial-day stay for a quick darshan?** Yes, day-use rooms are sometimes available on request. The Sansthan office can advise on availability.

**Are the rooms air-conditioned?** A/C rooms are available at Bhakt Niwas (limited inventory) and Anand Vihar. Standard rooms have fans and good ventilation. The Vidarbha winter is mild, so non-A/C rooms are comfortable from November to February.

For direct assistance with a Shegaon stay, use the [booking page](/booking) or the [contact page](/contact). The Sansthan office responds within one working day during regular season, and within a few hours during festival windows.

## What to expect on the ground

The Sansthan-managed properties are designed for pilgrims, not for luxury travelers. Expect clean rooms, hot water in the mornings, simple but wholesome meals at the Annakshetra, and an atmosphere that prioritises the devotee experience over frills. The volunteers and front-desk staff are experienced in handling families, large groups, and first-time visitors, and they will guide you through check-in, darshan token collection, and any local logistics. Most families find that a Shegaon stay, while modest in physical comfort, is among the most spiritually fulfilling of their yatra — and that the simple structure of the Sansthan accommodation is part of what makes the experience so grounding.

If you have a specific need (extra bed for a child, early check-in, late check-out, dietary preference), mention it in your booking request. The Sansthan office is used to accommodating most reasonable requests, particularly for senior citizens and families with young children.
