# Mumbai To Shegaon Weekend Guide | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

For Mumbai devotees, Shegaon is the most accessible major pilgrimage destination in the Sansthan's portfolio. The 580-kilometre distance from Mumbai CST is covered overnight by the 12139 Sewagram Express, which departs at 8:55 PM and arrives at Shegaon at 8:00 AM — arriving at the temple in time for morning darshan with no daytime travel needed. This overnight train connection is what makes a Shegaon weekend yatra practical for Mumbai devotees: Friday evening departure, Saturday darshan and Anand Sagar visit, Saturday night or Sunday morning departure back to Mumbai, and a full weekend of spiritual experience with minimal travel hassle. The Sewagram Express is reliable, affordable, and consistently on schedule, which is why it has become the default transport for Mumbai-Shegaon weekend pilgrims.

This guide provides the complete logistics for a Mumbai-to-Shegaon weekend trip — from booking the train and the Bhakta Niwas to the darshan schedule, what to pack, and how to make the most of a 48-hour visit. If you have more time available, the [Shegaon and Ajanta Ellora combined trip guide](/blog/shegaon-and-ajanta-ellora-combined-trip) covers a 3-day itinerary that adds the UNESCO caves to your Shegaon visit.

## The basic framework

A Mumbai-to-Shegaon weekend yatra follows this pattern:

- **Friday evening:** Depart Mumbai CST on the 12139 Sewagram Express (8:55 PM departure).
- **Saturday morning:** Arrive Shegaon at 8:00 AM. Check in at Bhakta Niwas. Attend morning darshan at the Samadhi Mandir.
- **Saturday full day:** Anand Sagar visit, afternoon darshan, evening aarti, Mahaprasad meals, rest at Bhakta Niwas.
- **Saturday night or Sunday morning:** Return to Mumbai by the same Sewagram Express (departs Shegaon at 8:00 PM, arrives Mumbai CST at 7:00 AM Sunday) or by an overnight bus.

This framework works for solo devotees, couples, families, and small groups. The key to making it work smoothly is advance booking — train tickets, Bhakta Niwas rooms, and the return transport all need to be confirmed before you leave Mumbai.

## Friday evening: Departure from Mumbai

**Train booking:** The 12139 Sewagram Express is the primary overnight train from Mumbai CST to Shegaon. It departs Mumbai CST at 8:55 PM and arrives Shegaon at 8:00 AM the next morning. The journey takes approximately 11 hours. Book tickets at least 30 days in advance on IRCTC — during Pragat Din season (February) and Samadhi Utsav (August-September), tickets sell out quickly.

**Seat class:** Second Sitting is the most economical option (INR 200–300). Sleeper Class is more comfortable for overnight travel (INR 400–600). AC 3-Tier is the most comfortable (INR 800–1,200). For a weekend trip, Sleeper Class is the best balance of comfort and cost.

**What to bring on the train:** A small overnight bag with a change of clothes, toiletries, a water bottle, and any medications. You do not need a large suitcase for a 2-day trip — a small backpack is sufficient. The [luggage management guide](/blog/how-to-manage-luggage-during-yatra) covers what to pack for short trips.

**At Mumbai CST:** Arrive at CST at least 30 minutes before the 8:55 PM departure. The platform for the Sewagram Express is typically Platform 4 or 5. Carry your ticket (digital or printed), a water bottle, and some snacks for the journey. The train has a pantry car, but the food options are limited.

## Saturday morning: Arrival and darshan

**Arrival at Shegaon (8:00 AM):** The train arrives at Shegaon station at 8:00 AM. Collect your bags and proceed to the Bhakta Niwas.

**Transport from station to Bhakta Niwas:** The Sansthan runs a free shuttle bus service from Shegaon station to the Bhakta Niwas on request. Call the Bhakta Niwas from the platform and someone will direct you to the pickup point. If the shuttle is not available, a shared auto-rickshaw from the station to the Bhakta Niwas costs approximately INR 20–30 per person.

**Check-in at Bhakta Niwas:** Official check-in time is typically 10:00 AM. If you arrive earlier, the reception will store your luggage and allow you to freshen up in designated areas. Use the early morning for darshan at the Samadhi Mandir, which is a short walk from the Bhakta Niwas.

**Morning darshan (8:30 AM):** Attend the morning darshan at the Samadhi Mandir. The 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM slot is ideal for a Saturday visit — the queue is manageable, and the temple atmosphere is peaceful. Offer your prayers at the Samadhi, take the prasad, and spend some time in the temple courtyard. The [darshan timing guide](/blog/shegaon-darshan-timing-guide) covers the complete daily schedule.

**Breakfast (10:00 AM):** Check in to your room, freshen up, and have breakfast at the Mahaprasad canteen. The morning meal is typically poha or upma with chai.

## Saturday full day: Anand Sagar, darshan, and aarti

**Anand Sagar visit (10:30 AM to 12:30 PM):** Walk to the Anand Sagar complex, which is adjacent to the Samadhi Mandir. Visit the musical fountain (if operational), the gardens, the Anandanand museum, and the temple exhibition. Anand Sagar is most peaceful in the morning before the afternoon crowds arrive. The museum provides valuable context about Shri Gajanan Maharaj's life and teachings. The [Anand Sagar guide](/blog/anand-sagar-visiting-guide) covers the complex in detail.

**Lunch (12:30 PM):** Lunch at the Mahaprasad canteen. The canteen serves Satvik vegetarian food on a thali system. Meals are included in the Bhakta Niwas tariff.

**Afternoon rest (1:00 PM to 4:00 PM):** Rest at the Bhakta Niwas during the hottest part of the day. Use this time for reading, journaling, or resting.

**Afternoon darshan (4:00 PM):** Attend the afternoon darshan at the Samadhi Mandir. The 4:00 PM slot is the best time for a second darshan — short queue, relaxed atmosphere, and the temple is at its most accessible.

**Evening aarti (7:00 PM):** Attend the evening Mangala Aarti at 7:00 PM. This is the largest gathering of the day and is deeply moving for devotees. The aarti at Shegaon is more structured and devotional than many other temple aartis, and the experience of singing along with hundreds of fellow devotees is powerful.

**Dinner (8:00 PM):** Dinner at the Mahaprasad canteen. After dinner, attend the musical fountain show at Anand Sagar if it is operating (typically on weekends and festival days).

## Sunday morning: Final darshan and departure

**Morning darshan (5:00 AM or 6:00 AM):** Wake up for a final darshan at the Samadhi Mandir before departure. The pre-dawn darshan is the most peaceful experience of the visit, and many devotees make it a point to attend even on the day of departure.

**Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Breakfast at the Mahaprasad canteen.

**Check-out (8:00 AM):** Check out of the Bhakta Niwas. Store your luggage at the reception if your return train is in the evening.

**Final Anand Sagar walk (8:30 AM to 10:00 AM):** Take a final walk around the Anand Sagar complex before departure. The morning light on the gardens and the Samadhi Mandir dome is beautiful, and the quiet morning atmosphere is a fitting conclusion to the weekend yatra.

**Return journey:** Board the 12139 Sewagram Express from Shegaon at 8:00 PM, arriving Mumbai CST at 7:00 AM Monday morning — back in time for the work week. Alternatively, take an overnight bus from Shegaon to Mumbai (departs around 8:00 PM, arrives around 7:00 AM). Bus options include VRL Travels, Paulo Travels, and Orange Tours.

## Accommodation booking

Book the Shegaon Bhakta Niwas at least 2 to 4 weeks in advance for a weekend visit. During Pragat Din and Samadhi Utsav seasons, book 6 to 8 weeks in advance. The Bhakta Niwas offers 2-bed and 3-bed rooms (Non-AC and AC). For a solo devotee or couple, a 2-bed room is ideal. For a family, a 3-bed or 4-bed room is more appropriate.

Book through +91 80531 90691 (WhatsApp and phone) or the [booking form](/booking). The [Bhakta Niwas complete booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the booking process in detail.

## Alternative transport options

While the Sewagram Express is the most popular option for Mumbai-to-Shegaon weekend travel, there are alternatives worth considering.

**Overnight bus:** VRL Travels, Paulo Travels, and Orange Tours operate AC sleeper buses from Mumbai (Thane, Dadar, Borivali) to Shegaon. The journey takes 8 to 9 hours, departing around 8:00 PM and arriving around 5:00 to 6:00 AM. Bus tickets cost INR 800 to 1,500, which is comparable to a Sleeper Class train ticket. Buses are more flexible than trains — they do not require advance booking as far ahead, and you can cancel more easily. The downside is less comfort than a train and more frequent delays due to traffic and road conditions.

**Self-drive car:** The drive from Mumbai to Shegaon takes 8 to 9 hours via the Mumbai-Nagpur Expressway (NH 53) or the older Mumbai-Aurangabad-Buldhana route. A self-drive car gives maximum flexibility — you can leave whenever you want, stop for meals at dhabas, and return at a time that suits you. However, the long drive is tiring, and navigating the Shegaon town traffic on arrival and departure adds to the hassle. Carpooling with 3 to 4 devotees splits the fuel cost and makes the drive more comfortable.

**Private taxi:** Hired cars from Mumbai to Shegaon cost INR 8,000 to 12,000 one way. This is the most comfortable option but the most expensive. It works well for families with young children or elderly members who find the train or bus uncomfortable.

For a weekend trip, the Sewagram Express remains the best balance of cost, comfort, and convenience.

## What to pack for a weekend yatra

**Darshan clothes:** One set of modest clothing for darshan (long pants or dhoti for men, sari or salwar kameez for women). One set of comfortable casual wear for the Bhakta Niwas.

**Comfortable shoes:** Closed-toe shoes for the temple steps, and slip-ons for the Bhakta Niwas.

**Light jacket or shawl:** For the early morning darshan, particularly in winter (November to January) when the pre-dawn temperature can be surprisingly cool.

**Basic medications:** Any regular medications, plus paracetamol and adhesive bandages for minor injuries.

**Phone charger and power bank:** Long days at the temple and Anand Sagar drain batteries quickly.

**ID proof:** Aadhaar card or driving licence for check-in at the Bhakta Niwas.

**Cash:** For temple donations, canteen purchases, and any emergency expenses. ATMs in Shegaon may have limited cash during peak periods.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is a weekend enough time for a meaningful visit to Shegaon?**

A: Yes. A 48-hour visit allows for morning and evening darshans, an Anand Sagar visit, and the evening aarti — all the core experiences of a Shegaon pilgrimage. The overnight train makes it practical without sacrificing work days.

**Q: Should I book the Bhakta Niwas or a nearby hotel?**

A: The Bhakta Niwas is the better option for a weekend visit. It is walking distance from the temple, meals are included, and the devotional atmosphere enhances the spiritual experience. Private hotels near the temple are a backup option if the Bhakta Niwas is full.

**Q: Can I visit Shegaon on a Sunday-Monday weekend instead of Friday-Saturday?**

A: Yes, but the train timing is less convenient. The 12139 Sewagram Express departs Mumbai CST at 8:55 PM on Fridays and arrives Shegaon at 8:00 AM on Saturdays. The return train departs Shegaon at 8:00 PM on Saturdays and arrives Mumbai at 7:00 AM on Sundays. For a Sunday-Monday trip, you would need an alternative transport arrangement, such as an overnight bus.

**Q: What if the Sewagram Express is cancelled or delayed?**

A: Train cancellations and delays are rare but do happen, particularly during monsoon season. Have a backup plan: the overnight bus from Mumbai to Shegaon (departs around 8:00 PM, arrives around 7:00 AM). Bus operators include VRL Travels, Paulo Travels, and Orange Tours. Book the bus ticket as a backup when you book your train ticket.

**Q: Is Shegaon safe for solo female devotees?**

A: Yes. Shegaon is a small, peaceful town with a strong devotional community. The Bhakta Niwas is secure, the temple is well-managed, and the local population is respectful toward female devotees. Solo female devotees should request a room on a lower floor at the Bhakta Niwas and avoid walking alone after dark outside the campus.

**Q: Can I extend the weekend to include a visit to Ajanta or Ellora caves?**

A: Yes, but 3 days is the minimum for adding the caves. Shegaon to Ajanta is approximately 200 km and 4 hours by car, and Shegaon to Ellora (near Aurangabad) is approximately 200 km and 4 hours. You would need to stay overnight near the caves and return to Shegaon or Mumbai the next day. The [Shegaon and Ajanta Ellora combined trip guide](/blog/shegaon-and-ajanta-ellora-combined-trip) covers this option.
