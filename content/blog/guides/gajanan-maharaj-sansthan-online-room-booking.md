# Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan Online Room Booking

Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan online room booking works as a request-and-confirm flow: you submit your stay details through the Sansthan's [online booking page](/booking), the office reviews availability, and a written confirmation from the office completes the reservation — after which most rooms run on a 24-hour check-out cycle counted from your time of entry. There is no instant-pay cart and no third-party site; the request page plus the office together are the whole system. This guide walks through every step of it, the room types and sanctioned tariffs at each of the four centres — Shegaon, Pandharpur, Omkareshwar and Trimbakeshwar — and the follow-on questions devotees ask most, so your family's rooms are secured the right way, the first time.

We wrote this hub because online booking queries arrive at the digital desk from every direction — families comparing centres, first-time pilgrims unsure what happens after the form, devotees worried they have landed on an impersonator site. Everything below reflects one principle: the official channel is the only channel.

## The request flow, from form to key

Understanding the sequence removes most of the anxiety first-time users feel. The Sansthan's system is deliberately simple — a devotee-facing request page, an office that holds the allocation, and a confirmation that puts the room beyond doubt.

1. **Gather the party details.** Exact headcount including children, preferred centre — Shegaon, Pandharpur, Omkareshwar or Trimbakeshwar — arrival and departure dates, room type if you have a preference, and any special need such as a ground-floor room or wheelchair-friendly access.
2. **Submit through the booking page.** Complete the form on the [online booking page](/booking) with those details. One submission per party and per stay keeps the office's records clean; duplicate submissions for the same dates slow everyone's file down.
3. **Await the office's acknowledgement.** The office reviews requests in the order received. During festival windows this takes longer — that is volume, not neglect.
4. **Receive and keep the written confirmation.** The confirmation from the office is your reservation. Save it, carry a printed copy, and bring it with photo ID for each adult to the desk.
5. **Settle the balance as guided and check in.** At the centre, complete any balance payment per the office's guidance against your receipt, and your 24-hour cycle begins from entry.

Two habits protect this flow. First, keep every message and receipt in one folder on your phone — the paper trail is your friend if any detail needs correcting. Second, if anything about a confirmation looks off, verify it with the office through the [contact page](/contact) before travelling, not after. ## Rooms and tariffs across the four centres

The Sansthan publishes one tariff structure across its accommodation network. The sanctioned rows every devotee should know:

| Room type | Capacity | Tariff per night | Naturally suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Bed AC | 2 | ₹1,650 | Couples, elderly pairs, a parent and child |
| 3 Bed Non-AC | 3 | ₹1,750 | Small families travelling in the cool season |
| 3 Bed AC | 3 | ₹2,050 | Small families in the hot months |
| 4 Bed Non-AC | 4 | ₹2,250 | The standard family room |
| 4 Bed AC | 4 | ₹2,550 | Larger families, April to June travel |

Read the arithmetic before you choose, because it often decides the room. The AC premium on the same bed count is ₹300 per night — that is the entire jump from 3 Bed Non-AC at ₹1,750 to 3 Bed AC at ₹2,050, and likewise from 4 Bed Non-AC at ₹2,250 to 4 Bed AC at ₹2,550. Moving up a bed count without AC — 3 Bed Non-AC to 4 Bed Non-AC — adds ₹500 per night. And a family graduating from 2 Bed AC at ₹1,650 straight to 4 Bed AC at ₹2,550 pays ₹900 per night more for the two extra beds and the space. Every room carries an attached bathroom with hot water, capacity equals the bed count, and the rooms are priced as service rather than commerce — modest by hotel standards, spotless by any.

## Choosing your centre: what the online request should say

An online request succeeds fastest when it matches the party to the right centre and the right town rhythm. The four centres differ more than newcomers expect.

| Centre | The town's character | Book early around |
|---|---|---|
| Shegaon | The Samadhi Mandir, Anand Sagar and the annadan at regional scale; the tradition's heart | Ekadashis, Pragat Din, jayanti, punyatithi, Shravan Mondays |
| Pandharpur | The Vitthal Rukmini temples and the Bhima; the wari's destination | Ashadhi and Kartik ekadashi windows |
| Omkareshwar | The island jyotirlinga in the Narmada; boat-and-parikrama pilgrimage | Mahashivratri, Sawan Mondays, winter weekends |
| Trimbakeshwar | The hill-town jyotirlinga near Nashik; pooja-centred travel | Pooja dates, monsoon weekends, festival clusters |

The request itself should state your centre plainly, keep the headcount exact, and note flexibility — "any floor", "non-AC acceptable if AC is full" — because flexibility is often what lets the office confirm a room rather than waitlist it. Groups above eight or ten should say so in the first line; group allocation runs on a separate sheet at every centre. ## What "online booking" is — and what it is not

Because the phrase attracts imitators, precision matters. The Sansthan's online room booking is a request system on the Sansthan's own booking page, answered and confirmed by the Sansthan's own office. It is not a third-party travel portal listing, not an instant-payment widget, and not a numbered hotline run by an agency. Anyone offering "instant confirmed Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan rooms" through any other route is not the Sansthan, however convincing the website or the WhatsApp display picture.

Practically, three habits keep a family safe. Book only through the [booking page](/booking) itself; treat any confirmation as genuine only when it comes from the office; and when in doubt, verify through the [contact page](/contact) — the canonical source for every current office detail, a point our [contact numbers guide](/blog/all-sansthan-contact-numbers-complete-list) develops further. Devotees who fell for lookalike services report the same story: a slick form, a payment, and then no room at the desk. The office-confirmed request has never worked that way.

## Timing the request: seasons and festival windows

The online request rewards the calendar sense every pilgrim eventually acquires. Ordinary weekdays outside school holidays are usually confirmable close to travel — even a few days ahead at quieter centres. The exceptions worth planning around:

- **Ekadashi at every centre**, when observant travellers converge and rooms tighten from the previous evening.
- **The Shegaon utsav year** — jayanti, Pragat Din and punyatithi — for which experienced families request weeks to months ahead.
- **Ashadhi ekadashi at Pandharpur**, the wari's culmination, when the town's capacity fills earliest of all.
- **Mahashivratri and the Sawan Mondays at the two jyotirlinga centres**, plus the winter pooja season at Trimbakeshwar, when pooja-bound families arrive the night before.

The request page takes a date range; if your dates are locked to a festival, submit early and say so. If your dates are flexible, moving a stay two days off the peak often converts a waitlist into a confirmation — a trick frequent devotees use without shame. And once the confirmation arrives, guard it: the 24-hour cycle from entry means an evening arrival holds the room to the following evening, which pairs naturally with an afternoon darshan and a rested departure. ## After confirmation: arrival day at the desk

The online work is done; the pilgrim's work continues. Arrival runs smoothly for parties that arrive prepared.

1. **Carry the confirmation and photo IDs.** The booking reference plus one original ID per adult is the desk's first ask at every centre.
2. **Arrive as a party, not in fragments.** Allocation is simplest when the whole group presents together; if elders are travelling separately, note it in the request so the desk expects two arrivals under one file.
3. **Check the room against the confirmation.** Bed count, AC or non-AC, ground floor if requested — raise any mismatch at the desk immediately, while the allocation chart can still be adjusted.
4. **Note the cycle.** Your 24-hour clock starts at entry; plan the departure darshan, luggage and transport inside it rather than against it.
5. **Keep every receipt to the last.** Balance payments, extensions, extras — the receipt folder that flew with you should fly home fuller.

Should anything on arrival day need repair — a delayed train, a wrong date, a sudden extra traveller — the office desk is the place, politely and early. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan online room booking an instant confirmation?

No. The [booking page](/booking) submits a request; the office then sends the written confirmation that actually reserves the room. The deliberate pace keeps allocation fair and human — plan for the office's reply rather than an on-screen confirmation, and submit early around festivals.

### Can I book rooms at all four centres through the same booking page?

Yes. The request page serves the Sansthan's accommodation network — Shegaon, Pandharpur, Omkareshwar and Trimbakeshwar. State the centre clearly in the request, and use separate requests for separate stays so each centre's file stays clean.

### What are the current room tariffs for online booking?

The sanctioned published rows are: 2 Bed AC at ₹1,650; 3 Bed Non-AC at ₹1,750; 3 Bed AC at ₹2,050; 4 Bed Non-AC at ₹2,250; and 4 Bed AC at ₹2,550 per night, with attached bathroom and hot water in every room. The same structure applies across the centres; confirm any detail of your specific stay with the office at confirmation.

### Which payment happens online and which at the desk?

The office states any advance due when it confirms your request, and the balance is settled at the centre as guided, with receipts for both. The [advance amount guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-booking-advance-amount-guide) explains how the advance adjusts against your tariff, and the [payment methods guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-booking-payment-methods-guide) covers accepted modes across locations.

### Is there a check-out time I should plan around?

Most rooms follow a 24-hour check-out cycle counted from the time of entry rather than a fixed hotel noon deadline. An evening arrival keeps the room to the following evening — confirm the cycle for your specific room with the office when the booking is confirmed.

### A website other than the Sansthan's offered me instant Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan rooms. Should I take it?

No. Verify every such offer against the office through the [contact page](/contact) before paying anyone.
