# Bhakta Niwas Frequently Asked Questions | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

Every devotee who has ever stared at the Bhakta Niwas booking page for the first time has had the same set of questions. How far in advance should I book? What room type do I actually need? Are meals really included? What happens if my plans change? The answers to these questions are straightforward, but they are scattered across different guides and WhatsApp conversations, which makes the booking process feel more complicated than it needs to be. This FAQ pulls together the most common questions from devotees across all four Sansthan locations — Shegaon, Omkareshwar, Pandharpur, and Trimbakeshwar — and answers them with verified details from the Sansthan's own booking system. If you cannot find what you are looking for here, the [complete booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the full process in detail, and the [accommodation comparison guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-across-locations-comparison) breaks down the differences between each location.

## Booking and reservation questions

**Q: How far in advance should I book Bhakta Niwas?**

A: The booking window depends on the season and the location. For regular seasons at Shegaon, booking 2 to 3 weeks in advance is usually sufficient. During festival periods — Pragat Din, Samadhi Utsav, Ashadhi Ekadashi — book at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance. At Omkareshwar and Trimbakeshwar, where the Bhakta Niwas properties are smaller, add an extra week to these timelines. Pandharpur during the Wari season (Ashadhi Ekadashi) requires 60 to 90 days of advance booking because Dindi participants are allocated rooms first. The safest rule is: book as early as you know your dates, regardless of the season.

**Q: Can I book online, or do I have to call or WhatsApp?**

A: The Sansthan accepts booking requests through multiple channels: WhatsApp on +91 80531 90691, phone calls to the same number, and the [booking request form](/booking) on this website. WhatsApp is the fastest channel for most devotees — the booking desk typically responds within 2 to 4 hours during regular days. The web form is useful when you are sending a detailed request for a large group or a multi-location itinerary. The [phone and WhatsApp booking best practices guide](/blog/phone-and-whatsapp-booking-best-practices) explains how to format your request for the fastest response.

**Q: What information do I need to provide when booking?**

A: A complete booking request should include: your full name and contact number, the names and ages of all guests, your intended check-in and check-out dates, the room type you prefer, and any special requests (ground-floor room, adjacent rooms, early check-in). Government-issued photo ID (Aadhar, Voter ID, or passport) is mandatory at check-in for every adult guest. The [booking process guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-booking-process) walks through each step.

**Q: Can I hold a booking without confirming?**

A: No. The Sansthan does not offer provisional or tentative bookings. A booking is confirmed only when the staff respond with written confirmation specifying the room type, dates, and tariff. Do not assume a room is booked until you receive this confirmation in writing — either on WhatsApp, over the phone, or via email.

## Room types and facilities

**Q: What room types are available at Shegaon Bhakta Niwas?**

A: The Shegaon complex offers the widest range of room types across all locations. The available options are: 2 Bed Non-AC, 2 Bed AC, 3 Bed Non-AC, 3 Bed AC, 4 Bed Non-AC, 4 Bed AC, Deluxe Suite, Luxury Suite, and Family Room (6-person capacity, AC). All prices include accommodation, all meals, applicable taxes, and hot water. The [room types and facilities guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-room-types-and-facilities) provides detailed descriptions and pricing for each category.

**Q: Is there a dormitory or common hall option?**

A: Yes. Shegaon Bhakta Niwas has a common hall that accommodates up to 50 guests in a dormitory-style arrangement. This is the most economical option and is well-suited for young devotees and large groups. The common hall has shared bathroom facilities, 24/7 security, and access to all meals at the Mahaprasad canteen. Many long-stay devotees find the common hall experience spiritually meaningful — sharing space with fellow pilgrims from different regions creates a sense of community.

**Q: Are AC rooms available at all locations?**

A: AC rooms are available at Shegaon and Omkareshwar. Pandharpur and Trimbakeshwar Bhakta Niwas properties are smaller and currently offer Non-AC rooms only. During summer months (March to June), the Non-AC rooms at these locations can be warm, so plan accordingly — carry light cotton clothing and a small fan if needed. The [accommodation comparison guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-across-locations-comparison) shows which room types are available at each location.

**Q: What facilities are included with the room?**

A: All rooms include accommodation, all three daily meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) at the Mahaprasad canteen, hot water, and applicable taxes. There are no hidden charges. Rooms have attached bathrooms with 24-hour water supply. The Bhakta Niwas campus has 24/7 security, and the temple is within walking distance. Wi-Fi availability varies by location — ask at reception when you check in.

## Check-in, check-out, and stays

**Q: What are the check-in and check-out times?**

A: Standard check-in time is typically around 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, and check-out is by 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Early check-in requests are often accommodated, especially on weekdays and during non-festival periods. If you arrive before the standard check-in time, you can usually leave your luggage at the reception, freshen up at the washrooms, and head to the temple for darshan while waiting for your room to be prepared. Late check-out requests should be made at the reception on the previous evening — availability depends on the next day's bookings.

**Q: Can I extend my stay or renew my room for another night?**

A: Yes, subject to availability. Room renewal should be confirmed with the reception staff at least a few hours before the standard check-out time. During regular seasons, renewal is straightforward. During festival seasons, renewal is not guaranteed because new guests are arriving and your room may be needed for the next booking. If you are planning a stay longer than 3 nights, discuss renewal logistics at the time of check-in. The [extended stay planning guide](/blog/extended-stay-planning-for-devotees) covers long-stay logistics in detail.

**Q: What is the maximum number of guests allowed per room?**

A: Each room type has a specified maximum capacity. Family Rooms accommodate up to 6 guests, 4-bed rooms accommodate up to 4 guests, 3-bed rooms up to 3 guests, and 2-bed rooms up to 2 guests. Exceeding the room capacity is not permitted for safety and comfort reasons. For large groups, the [best accommodation for large groups guide](/blog/best-accommodation-for-large-groups) provides a framework for selecting the right room mix.

## Meals and dietary requirements

**Q: Are meals really included in the room tariff?**

A: Yes, all three daily meals — breakfast, lunch, and dinner — are included in the accommodation tariff at all Bhakta Niwas locations. The Mahaprasad canteen serves Satvik vegetarian food. Breakfast typically includes poha, upma, or similar items with chai. Lunch and dinner are full thalis with dal, sabzi, roti, rice, and sweet. The food is simple, hygienic, and included in the price you pay for the room — there is no separate meal charge.

**Q: Can I get Jain food or special dietary meals?**

A: The Mahaprasad canteen serves Satvik vegetarian food that is generally suitable for Jain devotees, as it avoids onion and garlic in most preparations. However, it is best to confirm your specific dietary requirements at the time of booking or at the reception when you check in. The canteen staff are accustomed to dietary requests and will do their best to accommodate them. If you have a severe food allergy or a very specific dietary requirement, carry some self-sufficient snacks as a backup.

## Cancellation and modifications

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**

A: The Sansthan's cancellation policy allows modifications and cancellations with reasonable notice. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the scheduled check-in are typically eligible for a full refund or rescheduling. Cancellations within 24 hours of check-in are typically non-refundable. These timelines can vary during festival periods, so confirm the specific policy at the time of booking. For detailed timelines and refund procedures, see the [refund and cancellation policy guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-refund-and-cancellation-policy).

**Q: Can I modify my booking dates after confirmation?**

A: Yes. To modify a confirmed booking, contact the same staff member who confirmed it, or send a message to the booking number (+91 80531 90691) referencing your original confirmation. State clearly what you want to change — the new dates, the same room type, and the same duration. Modifications are easier to process when there is good availability. During peak festival periods, modifications may be limited if the Bhakta Niwas is fully booked for your new dates.

## Payment and pricing

**Q: How do I pay for my booking?**

A: Payment methods vary by location and staff preference. The Sansthan typically accepts cash at the time of check-in. Some locations also accept UPI and bank transfers. Confirm the payment method with the staff member who confirms your booking. Do not make payments to unofficial numbers — always verify through the [official contact page](/contact) or the WhatsApp number +91 80531 90691.

**Q: Are there any hidden charges?**

A: No. The Bhakta Niwas tariff is all-inclusive — it covers accommodation, all meals, hot water, and applicable taxes. There are no separate charges for amenities, parking, or temple access. The only additional expenses you might incur are for optional services like special darshan, guide fees at nearby attractions, or personal purchases.
