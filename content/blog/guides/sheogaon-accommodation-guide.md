# Shegaon Accommodation Guide | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

Choosing where to stay in Shegaon is one of the most consequential decisions of any yatra. The Samadhi Mandir is the centre of everything — all queues start and end here, all aarti times radiate out from here, and the daily rhythm of the town itself is set by the temple's schedule. Staying within walking distance of the temple matters far more than having a "nicer" hotel room in a different part of town, because the tired moments of any pilgrimage happen at unpredictable times, and the difference between a five-minute walk back to your room and a forty-minute auto-rickshaw ride can decide whether the trip feels gentle or exhausting. This guide covers every realistic accommodation option in Shegaon — from the Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas rooms to private dharamshalas and small commercial hotels — with current pricing, booking procedures, and the specific situations where each type of property is the right call.

## The Bhakta Niwas Network at Shegaon

The Sansthan operates several Bhakta Niwas properties within a short walk of the Samadhi Mandir, and these are the primary recommendation for most devotees. There are three main Bhakta Niwas campuses near the temple: the original Bhakta Niwas closest to the main gate, the newer building with more modern rooms slightly behind the temple, and a third smaller property favoured by elderly devotees because of its quiet location. The total number of rooms across all three properties is roughly 250, distributed across various categories.

The room categories are typically: Standard Non-AC (the most affordable and most commonly available), Standard AC, Family Room (sleeps four to six), Dormitory (per-bed pricing, available for groups of single devotees), and a small number of VIP rooms for senior citizens and registered NRIs who book well in advance. The Standard Non-AC rooms are typically INR 300-500 per night, AC rooms INR 600-900, and Family Rooms INR 800-1,500. Dormitory beds are INR 100-200 per person per night. Pricing varies slightly based on season — festival periods see rate increases of about 30-50 percent on some categories, and the Sansthan reserves the right to revise pricing annually.

The biggest practical advantage of Bhakta Niwas over private accommodation is the morning queue. Devotees staying on the Bhakta Niwas campus can join the queue at 3:30 AM for the Mangala Aarti without needing to leave their building. Devotees staying in private hotels in other parts of town must arrive by auto-rickshaw or on foot, which adds thirty to forty-five minutes of uncertainty. The [shegaon darshan timing guide](/blog/sheogaon-darshan-timing-guide) breaks down each darshan slot and how the Bhakta Niwas location affects queue timing. The [shegaon accommodation near temple guide](/blog/shegaon-accommodation-near-temple) has the full property list with room-by-room details.

## Private Dharamshalas in Shegaon

Several other dharamshalas operate within walking distance of the temple, run by other trusts and families associated with the original Maharaj tradition. These dharamshalas typically offer similar pricing to Bhakta Niwas, and some have specific community ties — Marwari dharamshalas, Telugu dharamshalas, and the like — that devotees belonging to those communities sometimes prefer. The rooms at these dharamshalas vary in maintenance quality: some are spotless and well-managed, others are basic and under-resourced. Visiting the property before booking is recommended if you have the time.

The most common reason to choose a private dharamshala over Bhakta Niwas is availability. During peak festival periods, Bhakta Niwas can be fully booked weeks in advance, and a private dharamshala may have last-minute availability when Bhakta Niwas does not. The Sansthan does not have jurisdiction over private dharamshalas, so the booking process is directly with the dharamshala manager. Most accept walk-in bookings, though calling ahead is always wise.

## Small Commercial Hotels

Shegaon has a small commercial hotel sector catering primarily to business travellers passing through the town, and these properties are a useful third option when Bhakta Niwas is fully booked. The hotel rooms are typically more modern than Bhakta Niwas rooms — they may have televisions, room service, and Western-style attached bathrooms — but they are a 10-20 minute auto-rickshaw ride from the temple, and they do not have the devotional atmosphere of the Bhakta Niwas campus. Rates range from INR 800 to INR 2,500 per night depending on the property and the season.

For devotees travelling with elderly parents who are used to a certain standard of room comfort, a small commercial hotel can be the right call. The Sansthan has no relationship with these commercial hotels, so any booking or complaint is handled directly with the property. Many devotees use commercial hotels as a backup option if they arrive without a Bhakta Niwas reservation and cannot find one on the day.

## Anand Sagar Complex and Surrounding Area

The Anand Sagar entertainment complex is on the eastern edge of the temple campus, and the area around it has a few accommodation options including a few premium hotels that cater to visiting dignitaries and wealthy devotees. These properties have air-conditioned rooms with attached restaurants, and they sit about a 10-minute walk from the main temple gate. Rates range from INR 1,500 to INR 4,500 per night. The Anand Sagar area is the right call for a devotee who wants a quieter setting with access to the Anand Sagar gardens, the Guruprasad restaurant, and the musical fountain complex.

## When to Book and How

Bhakta Niwas rooms are most available during the lean seasons of August and September, less available during the festival rush of Ashadhi Ekadashi (July), Kartik Ekadashi (November), Pragat Din (February), and the year-end winter holidays (December-January). For festival periods, book at least four to six weeks in advance. For lean periods, two to three weeks is usually sufficient. The [festival rush booking strategies](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) guide has detailed festival-specific advice.

The booking procedure is straightforward. The primary method is WhatsApp or phone, with the contact numbers listed on the [booking page](/booking). You can also email the Sansthan office or use the contact form on [contact](/contact). When you book, you will be asked for your name, contact number, the date of arrival and departure, the number of people in your party, and your preferred room type. The Sansthan typically confirms within 24 to 48 hours during regular seasons and within 12 hours during peak seasons. After confirmation, payment is made on arrival at the Bhakta Niwas reception — no advance payment is required, and no deposit is taken for normal bookings. The [complete booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the procedure step by step.

For NRI devotees booking from abroad, the WhatsApp process is still the most reliable. The phone number works internationally and you can communicate in English, Hindi, or Marathi. Some NRI devotees use the WhatsApp number of a family friend in India to facilitate the booking in the local language, which can be useful for elderly family members who are not as comfortable with the technology. The [NRI booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-booking-for-nris) covers international travel and booking specifics.

## Special Considerations for Elderly and Family Devotees

Elderly devotees often need specific room features: a ground-floor room, a Western-style bathroom, easy access to the canteen, and proximity to the medical facility. When you book, mention these requirements explicitly. The Sansthan will try to allocate a ground-floor room with an attached bathroom, but availability is not guaranteed, so book early. The medical facility on the Bhakta Niwas campus handles routine issues like blood pressure, dehydration, and minor injuries. For more serious medical needs, the government hospital in Shegaon is the closest full-service facility.

Family devotees with young children should request a Family Room or two adjacent Standard Rooms. The Family Rooms are typically on the higher floors of the newer building and sleep up to six. The canteen serves simple, mild food suitable for children. The campus has open spaces where children can move safely during downtime. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far is Bhakta Niwas from the temple?** The closest Bhakta Niwas property is roughly 100-150 metres from the temple's main entrance — about a two-minute walk. The other Bhakta Niwas buildings are within 300 metres. All are walkable even for elderly devotees, though a wheelchair can be requested for those who need it.

**Are meals included in the room rate?** No. Bhakta Niwas rooms are room-only. The Mahaprasad canteen and the Guruprasad restaurant are separate expenses, with the Mahaprasad thali at INR 40-60 per meal and the Guruprasad restaurant serving à la carte items at slightly higher rates.

**Are lockers available for valuables?** Yes. The Bhakta Niwas reception has lockers for valuables like passports, jewellery, and excess cash. There is no charge for the lockers, and they are accessible during reception hours.

**Is there parking at Bhakta Niwas?** Yes. Each Bhakta Niwas campus has a parking area for cars, two-wheelers, and bicycles. Parking is free for registered Bhakta Niwas guests. During peak festival periods, the parking areas can fill up quickly, so arrive early.

**Can I cancel my booking if plans change?** Yes, with at least 72 hours' notice. Same-day cancellations may forfeit the first night's deposit if any was taken. The Sansthan is generally flexible on cancellations during off-peak periods, so contact the office as soon as your plans change.

**What is the difference between Bhakta Niwas and a private dharamshala?** The Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas is professionally managed with consistent maintenance, clean restrooms, and standard procedures. Private dharamshalas vary widely in quality — some are excellent, others are basic. The Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas is the more reliable choice for first-time visitors.
