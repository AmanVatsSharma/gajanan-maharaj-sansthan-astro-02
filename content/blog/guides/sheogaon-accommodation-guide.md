# Shegaon Accommodation Guide | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

Shegaon is a small town in Maharashtra's Buldhana district, and it is not the kind of place with a large hotel industry. Most visitors come for the Samadhi Mandir of Shri Gajanan Maharaj, and the accommodation ecosystem has grown around that single purpose. Your main choices are straightforward: stay at the Sansthan-run Bhakta Niwas, stay at one of the small private lodges near the temple, or stay at a mid-range hotel in the Shegaon market area about 2 km away. Each option has clear trade-offs in terms of convenience, cost, and experience. This guide covers everything you need to make the right choice.

## Bhakta Niwas: The Official Devotee Accommodation

The Bhakta Niwas at Shegaon is the flagship accommodation of Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan. It is located approximately 300 metres from the Samadhi Mandir entrance, within easy walking distance. The campus consists of multiple blocks ranging from basic dormitory halls to private AC rooms. The entire facility is run by the Sansthan, which means prices are kept deliberately low and the atmosphere is devotional rather than commercial.

**Room types:** The dormitory halls are shared facilities with bunk beds accommodating 4 to 8 people per room. Shared bathrooms with hot water are located along each hallway. Dormitory rates are typically INR 100-200 per bed per night. These are ideal for solo devotees, young groups, or families who are comfortable with shared sleeping arrangements. The rooms are clean, well-ventilated, and maintained by the Sansthan housekeeping staff.

Private rooms at Bhakta Niwas range from non-AC double rooms (INR 300-500 per night) to AC double rooms (INR 600-900 per night) to larger family rooms that can accommodate 4-6 people (INR 800-1,500 per night). Room allocation is not guaranteed until you receive a confirmation message from the booking desk, and the Sansthan does not offer online booking through a website. All bookings are handled via phone or WhatsApp.

**Facilities:** The Bhakta Niwas campus has a large dining hall that serves three meals daily. Breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM, lunch from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, and dinner from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM. The meals are simple, vegetarian, and sattvic. During major festivals, the canteen operates extended hours and serves free or subsidised meals to thousands of devotees. Hot water is available from 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Drinking water ATMs are available on campus. A small medical clinic with a resident doctor operates during major festivals; during normal days, a nurse is available on call.

**Check-in and check-out:** The standard check-in time is 10:00 AM, and check-out is 10:00 AM the following day, operating on a 24-hour cycle from the time of entry. This means if you arrive at 6:00 PM, you can check out at 6:00 PM the next day, which is flexible for devotees arriving by late-night trains. The Shegaon railway station is about 2 km from Bhakta Niwas, and shared auto-rickshaws are available outside the station for INR 20-30 per person.

**Booking process:** Call or WhatsApp the Sansthan booking desk with your name, number of people, dates, and preferred room type. The [complete booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the exact phone numbers, message templates, and what to do if you do not receive a confirmation. During peak periods, the booking desk can be very busy, and it is worth calling multiple times if you do not get through on the first attempt.

## Private Lodges Near the Temple

Several small private lodges operate within 500 metres of the Samadhi Mandir entrance. These are family-run establishments with 10 to 20 rooms each. Rooms are typically basic non-AC with attached bathrooms. Prices range from INR 400-800 per night, which is slightly more expensive than Bhakta Niwas dormitories but comparable to or cheaper than private rooms at the Sansthan. The advantage of a private lodge is availability — they accept walk-ins and do not require advance booking, which is useful if you arrive unexpectedly or if Bhakta Niwas is fully booked.

The quality of private lodges varies. Some are well-maintained with clean rooms and friendly owners who have been serving devotees for decades. Others are more basic, with thin walls and shared bathrooms. If you are considering a private lodge, arrive during the day, inspect the room before paying, and ask other devotees at the temple for recommendations. The lodges near the temple entrance are generally better maintained than those further away.

Private lodges do not offer meals, but almost all of them are within walking distance of small restaurants and tea stalls. A basic vegetarian thali costs INR 80-150 at these restaurants. For devotees who prefer to eat at the Sansthan Mahaprasad hall and stay in a private room, the private lodge plus Mahaprasad combination is a popular middle ground.

## Mid-Range Hotels in Shegaon

For devotees who want more amenities — air conditioning throughout, room service, Wi-Fi, a restaurant on-site, and more privacy — there are several mid-range hotels in Shegaon's market area, about 2 km from the temple. These hotels cater to business travellers as well as pilgrims and offer a more conventional hotel experience. Prices range from INR 1,500-4,000 per night depending on the hotel and season.

The main trade-off is distance from the temple. A 2 km walk in the pre-dawn cool is manageable, but during the heat of the day, you will need to arrange an auto-rickshaw (INR 30-50 per trip) to reach the temple. For devotees attending multiple darshans per day, this adds both cost and logistical complexity. The [mumbai to shegaon weekend guide](/blog/mumbai-to-shegaon-weekend-guide) covers how to manage this efficiently if you are arriving from Mumbai for a short trip.

Mid-range hotels are also the most reliable option during major festivals when Bhakta Niwas and private lodges are fully booked. If you are visiting during Pragat Din Utsav or another major festival, consider booking a hotel room at least 6-8 weeks in advance. The [festival rush booking strategies](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) guide has detailed advice for securing accommodation during peak periods.

## What to Expect on Arrival

When you arrive at Shegaon by train, the station is small and easy to navigate. Exit through the main gate and you will find auto-rickshaw drivers waiting. Tell them you are going to Bhakta Niwas or the Samadhi Mandir, and the fare is typically INR 30-50. The journey takes 10 minutes. If you are staying at a mid-range hotel, confirm the address with your driver, as some hotels are on the outskirts of town.

If you are arriving by bus, the MSRTC bus stand is about 1.5 km from the temple. Auto-rickshaws from the bus stand to Bhakta Niwas cost INR 20-40.

At Bhakta Niwas check-in, you will need to present a government-issued photo ID for every adult in your group. Children should be accompanied by their parent's ID. The check-in process takes 5-10 minutes. You will receive a room key and a brief orientation about dining hall timings, hot water availability, and the location of the nearest medical facility. If you have booked a private room and it is not ready when you arrive, the reception will typically offer you a waiting area or suggest a nearby private lodge while your room is prepared.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it better to stay at Bhakta Niwas or a private hotel?** For first-time visitors and families, Bhakta Niwas is the better choice. The proximity to the temple, the Mahaprasad canteen, the devotional atmosphere, and the support from Sansthan staff make it the most practical option. Private hotels are better suited to repeat visitors who want more privacy or are visiting during a festival when Bhakta Niwas is fully booked.

**How far in advance should I book Bhakta Niwas?** During normal months, 1-2 weeks notice is sufficient. During festival periods (Pragat Din, Guru Purnima, Ashadhi Ekadashi), book at least 4-6 weeks in advance. The [booking strategies guide](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) provides timelines and tips for peak periods.

**Can I extend my stay at Bhakta Niwas?** Extensions are possible if rooms are available. During peak periods, extensions are rarely possible due to waiting lists. Always have a backup accommodation plan. Ask about extension options at check-in rather than waiting until the last day.

**Are there accommodation options for NRIs and foreign nationals?** Yes. Bhakta Niwas accepts all visitors regardless of nationality. Foreign nationals must carry a valid passport and Indian visa. The [NRI booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-booking-for-nris) covers the specific requirements for international devotees.

**Is there parking available at Bhakta Niwas?** Yes, there is a designated parking area for cars and two-wheelers near the campus entrance. The parking is free for devotees staying at Bhakta Niwas. During festival days, the parking area reaches capacity early, so arrive before 10:00 AM if you are driving.

**What happens if I lose my room key?** Report to the reception desk immediately. A replacement key will be issued, and if the original is found later, it will be deactivated. There is typically no charge for a lost key unless it is a deliberate case of misuse.
