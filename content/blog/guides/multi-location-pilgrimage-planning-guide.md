# Multi Location Pilgrimage Planning Guide | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan maintains four sacred locations across Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, each with its own temple, accommodation, and devotional character. For many devotees, visiting just one location feels like touching only one corner of a much larger spiritual landscape. Shegaon holds the Samadhi Mandir and the Anand Sagar complex. Omkareshwar guards a Jyotirlinga on the Narmada. Pandharpur is the heart of the Warkari tradition. Trimbakeshwar holds the Godavari's source and another Jyotirlinga. Each one rewards extended attention, but the most memorable pilgrimages connect two or more of these sites in a single journey.

The most common question from devotees planning a multi-location yatra is straightforward: where do I start, and in what order? The answer depends on your origin city, your available leave, the festival calendar, and whether you are travelling by train, bus, or car. A devotee coming from Mumbai has different routing logic than someone starting from Indore or Nagpur. A family with three days of leave has very different options from someone with ten days. The festival you want to attend — Pragat Din in February, Ashadhi Ekadashi in July, Mahashivratri in February or March — changes the routing, the booking timelines, and even the transport mode. This guide provides a comprehensive framework for planning multi-location pilgrimages — from the two-location combinations that work for a long weekend to the full four-location circuit that fills a week or more. It covers route sequencing logic, accommodation booking strategies for multiple locations, transport arrangements, budget planning, and the specific considerations that apply when you are coordinating bookings, travel, and darshan across four different Sansthan properties simultaneously.

## Choosing your starting point and origin city

Before selecting a route, it helps to think about where you are travelling from. The Sansthan's locations are spread across three states — Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh — and the nearest major city changes which location is most accessible.

- **From Mumbai or Pune:** Shegaon is reachable in 8 to 10 hours by train or 8 to 9 hours by car. Pandharpur is about 4 to 5 hours from Pune. Trimbakeshwar is about 4 hours from Mumbai. Omkareshwar requires a stop in Indore or Khandwa. The typical starting point for Mumbai and Pune devotees is Shegaon, then moving west or south depending on the circuit.
- **From Nagpur:** Shegaon is about 4 to 5 hours away by train or car. Omkareshwar is about 7 to 8 hours. Starting from Shegaon or Omkareshwar is natural for Nagpur-based devotees.
- **From Indore:** Omkareshwar is just 2 hours away, making it the natural starting point. From Omkareshwar, you can continue to Shegaon (6 to 7 hours) or extend the yatra further.
- **From Solapur:** Pandharpur is 1.5 hours away. Solapur-based devotees can start with Pandharpur and continue to Shegaon (6 to 7 hours).

The [Indore-Omkareshwar-Shegaon yatra guide](/blog/indore-omkareshwar-shegaon-yatra) provides a detailed route plan for devotees entering from the Madhya Pradesh side. The [Mumbai to Shegaon weekend guide](/blog/mumbai-to-shegaon-weekend-guide) covers the starting leg for Mumbai-based devotees doing a shorter circuit.

## Route sequencing logic

The key principle of multi-location pilgrimage planning is geographical efficiency: minimise backtracking, group locations that are close to each other, and sequence the journey to flow naturally from your origin city.

### Option A: The Maharashtra Circuit (Shegaon-Omkareshwar-Pandharpur or Shegaon-Pandharpur-Trimbakeshwar)

**Best for:** Devotees starting from Mumbai, Pune, or Nagpur.

**Route:** Shegaon to Omkareshwar (340 km, 6 to 7 hours) to Pandharpur (480 km, 10 hours) or Trimbakeshwar (600 km, 12 hours).

**Duration:** 7 to 10 days for the full circuit.

## The four locations in brief

**Shegaon (Buldhana district, Maharashtra):** The primary location — the Samadhi Mandir where Shri Gajanan Maharaj attained samadhi, the Anand Sagar complex, and the Mahaprasad canteen. This is the spiritual centre of the Sansthan and the starting point for most multi-location yatras.

**Omkareshwar (Khandwa district, Madhya Pradesh):** One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, located on the Mandhata island in the Narmada River. The temple is on an island connected by a 500-metre bridge, and the Narmada aarti at sunset is one of the most beautiful spiritual experiences in the circuit.

**Pandharpur (Solapur district, Maharashtra):** The Vitthal temple on the Chandrabhaga River, the centre of the Warkari Bhakti tradition. Ashadhi Ekadashi transforms the town into the largest devotional gathering in Maharashtra. For non-festival visits, the atmosphere is peaceful and devotional.

**Trimbakeshwar (Nashik district, Maharashtra):** Another of the twelve Jyotirlingas, located at the source of the Godavari River at the base of Brahmagiri hill. The temple is compact, the darshan is intimate, and the Godavari spring ritual is a unique experience.

## Route sequencing logic

The key principle of multi-location pilgrimage planning is geographical efficiency: minimise backtracking, group locations that are close to each other, and sequence the journey to flow naturally from your origin city.

### Option A: The Maharashtra Circuit (Shegaon-Omkareshwar-Pandharpur or Shegaon-Pandharpur-Trimbakeshwar)

**Best for:** Devotees starting from Mumbai, Pune, or Nagpur.

**Route:** Shegaon to Omkareshwar (340 km, 6 to 7 hours) to Pandharpur (480 km, 10 hours) or Trimbakeshwar (600 km, 12 hours).

**Duration:** 7 to 10 days for the full circuit.

**The Maharashtra Circuit** is the most popular multi-location yatra format. It connects three locations within Maharashtra (plus Omkareshwar in Madhya Pradesh) in a logical east-to-west flow. The route follows NH 53 from Shegaon to the Madhya Pradesh border, then NH 753F and NH 160 through Aurangabad and Nashik to Trimbakeshwar, with a southern branch to Pandharpur via Solapur.

### Option B: The Vidarbha-Madhya Pradesh Circuit (Shegaon-Omkareshwar)

**Best for:** Devotees with 3 to 5 days available, or those entering from Indore.

**Route:** Shegaon to Omkareshwar (340 km, 6 to 7 hours).

**Duration:** 3 to 5 days.

**The Shegaon-Omkareshwar circuit** is the simplest and most accessible multi-location yatra. It connects the Samadhi Mandir with the Narmada Jyotirlinga and requires only one inter-city leg. The [Shegaon-Omkareshwar 3-day itinerary](/blog/shegaon-omkareshwar-3-day-itinerary) provides a complete day-by-day plan.

### Option C: The Three Jyotirlinga Circuit (Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar with Shegaon)

**Best for:** Devotees who want to experience all three Jyotirlingas in the Sansthan's portfolio (Omkareshwar, Trimbakeshwar) alongside the Samadhi Mandir.

**Route:** Shegaon to Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar, or the reverse.

**Duration:** 5 to 7 days.

**The Three Jyotirlinga Circuit** is the most spiritually comprehensive option. It covers the Narmada Jyotirlinga (Omkareshwar), the Godavari Jyotirlinga (Trimbakeshwar), and the Samadhi Mandir (Shegaon). The Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar leg is approximately 420 km and 8 to 9 hours. The [Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar pilgrimage checklist](/blog/omkareshwar-to-trimbakeshwar-pilgrimage-checklist) covers this leg in detail.

### Option D: The Full Four-Location Circuit

**Best for:** Devotees with 10 to 14 days available, or those on a dedicated pilgrimage tour.

**Route:** Shegaon-Omkareshwar-Pandharpur-Trimbakeshwar-Shegaon (or the reverse).

**Duration:** 7 to 10 days minimum.

**The Full Circuit** covers all four Sansthan locations in a complete loop. The total driving distance is approximately 1,820 km, which works out to 230–260 km per driving day over 7 days — very manageable. The [planning a one-week Maharashtra yatra guide](/blog/planning-a-one-week-maharashtra-yatra) provides a day-by-day itinerary for the full circuit.

## Accommodation booking for multiple locations

The most important logistical consideration for a multi-location yatra is coordinating accommodation bookings across all properties. Each Bhakta Niwas operates independently, and you need to make separate booking requests for each location.

**Booking sequence:**

1. **Book all locations simultaneously.** Do not wait to book the second location until you have confirmed the first. Send all booking requests together (in a single WhatsApp message if possible) so that the Sansthan team can see your full itinerary and coordinate availability across properties.

2. **Specify the sequence and dates clearly.** In your booking request, state: "We are doing a multi-location yatra: Shegaon for X nights (dates), Omkareshwar for Y nights (dates), Pandharpur for Z nights (dates)." The booking desk will then process all requests together.

3. **Confirm each booking separately.** Wait for individual confirmations from each property. Do not assume that a single confirmation covers all locations.

4. **Keep a booking summary.** Maintain a single document (on your phone or printed) that shows: location, dates, room type, tariff, booking reference, and contact number for each property. This document is invaluable when you arrive at each location and need to check in quickly.

**Booking through the website form:** The [booking form](/booking) on this website accepts multi-location booking requests. Fill out the form for each location separately, or send a single WhatsApp message to +91 80531 90691 with all details together. The [Bhakta Niwas complete booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the booking process in detail.

**Booking lead time:** For multi-location yatras, book at least 6 to 8 weeks in advance during regular seasons and 10 to 12 weeks in advance during festival periods. The [festival rush booking strategies guide](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) covers festival-period booking in detail.

## Transport arrangements for multi-location yatras

**Private taxi (recommended):** A hired car with a driver is the most practical transport option for multi-location yatras. A 7-day AC car (Maruti Ertiga) costs approximately INR 17,500 to 24,500, including the driver's stay and meals. Sharing among three devotees brings the per-person cost to INR 5,800 to 8,200. Book 3 to 4 weeks in advance through a local operator. The [complete transport guide](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) covers taxi booking details.

**Multi-leg taxi booking:** If you are not doing the full circuit in one continuous drive, book each leg separately through local operators. For example: Shegaon to Omkareshwar (book through Shegaon operator), Omkareshwar to Trimbakeshwar (book through Khandwa operator), Trimbakeshwar to Pandharpur (book through Nashik operator). This approach gives you more flexibility but costs more than a single multi-leg booking.

**Train and bus:** For devotees who prefer public transport, the train and bus network connects the major cities near each Sansthan location. However, the indirect routes and limited frequency make public transport impractical for the full circuit. It works well for individual legs — for example, train from Mumbai to Shegaon, taxi from Shegaon to Omkareshwar, and train from Khandwa back to Mumbai.

## Budget planning for multi-location yatras

The budget for a multi-location yatra depends on the number of locations, the duration, the transport mode, and the accommodation standard. Here is a budget estimate for the full four-location circuit:

| Item | Cost per person (INR) |
|------|-----------------------|
| Taxi share (7 days, 3 people) | 6,000–8,200 |
| Accommodation (6 nights, shared rooms) | 3,600–6,000 |
| Meals (7 days, Mahaprasad + restaurants) | 2,000–3,500 |
| Train/bus to/from origin city | 1,500–5,000 |
| Temple donations and miscellaneous | 500–1,500 |
| **Total (excluding origin transport)** | **13,600–24,200** |

For a two-location yatra (Shegaon-Omkareshwar), the budget is approximately INR 4,800 to 9,900 per person. For a three-location yatra (Shegaon-Omkareshwar-Trimbakeshwar), the budget is approximately INR 8,000 to 15,000 per person.

## Festival calendar integration

The best time to plan a multi-location yatra is around the festival calendar. Each Sansthan location has major festivals at different times of the year, and visiting multiple locations during their festival periods creates a rich devotional experience:

- **February:** Pragat Din at Shegaon + Mahashivratri at Trimbakeshwar (both fall in the same month)
- **June-July:** Ashadhi Ekadashi at Pandharpur + Samadhi Utsav at Shegaon (both in the monsoon season)
- **October-November:** Kartik Ekadashi at Pandharpur + Diwali at all locations

The [festival dates and booking windows guide](/blog/festival-dates-and-booking-windows) provides the complete festival calendar with booking timelines.

## Special considerations for multi-location yatras

**Document management:** Keep all booking confirmations, transport tickets, and ID documents organised in a single folder on your phone or in a printed envelope. When you are moving between four locations, the risk of misplacing documents increases. The [document checklist guide](/blog/devotee-document-checklist-for-all-locations) covers what to carry.

**Health management:** Different locations have different medical facilities. Know the nearest hospital at each stop before you arrive. Carry a sufficient supply of regular medications for the full duration of the yatra. The [medical emergencies guide](/blog/handling-medical-emergencies-on-yatra) covers health infrastructure at each location.

**Luggage management:** A multi-location yatra generates more luggage handling than a single-location visit. Pack light — one small bag per person is sufficient for a 7-day circuit. The [luggage management guide](/blog/how-to-manage-luggage-during-yatra) covers packing and transport tips.

**Emergency contacts:** Save the Sansthan booking number (+91 80531 90691) and the [emergency contact guide](/blog/emergency-contact-during-pilgrimage) covers local emergency numbers at each location.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Which multi-location yatra is the best for a first-time pilgrim?**

A: The Shegaon-Omkareshwar 3-day itinerary is the best starting point. It covers two major sacred sites, requires only one long drive, and delivers a complete devotional experience without being overwhelming. The [3-day itinerary guide](/blog/shegaon-omkareshwar-3-day-itinerary) provides the complete plan.

**Q: How far in advance should I book accommodation for a multi-location yatra?**

A: At least 6 to 8 weeks in advance for regular seasons, and 10 to 12 weeks for festival periods. Book all locations simultaneously to ensure availability across the full itinerary.

**Q: Is it better to do the full circuit in one continuous trip or to visit locations separately?**

A: One continuous trip is more efficient and provides a better sense of pilgrimage continuity — the spiritual experience of moving from one sacred site to another is more meaningful when the visits are connected. Separate visits work well for devotees who prefer to spend more time at each location or who cannot take extended leave from work.

**Q: Can I book a taxi for the entire circuit from one operator?**

A: Yes. Many taxi operators in Shegaon, Khamgaon, and Aurangabad offer multi-location circuit packages. A 7-day AC car for the full circuit costs approximately INR 17,500 to 24,500. Book 3 to 4 weeks in advance and confirm the driver's availability for all legs.

**Q: What is the best order to visit the four locations?**

A: Shegaon to Omkareshwar to Pandharpur to Trimbakeshwar is the recommended order. This flows from east to west, minimises backtracking, and connects the Samadhi Mandir (the spiritual starting point) with the three Jyotirlingas in a logical sequence.

**Q: Should I visit during a festival period?**

A: Festival visits are more spiritually intense but also more crowded and logistically challenging. For a first multi-location yatra, a non-festival period (October to February, excluding major festival weeks) is more comfortable. For experienced devotees, festival visits add a layer of communal devotion that is unique and memorable. The [festival dates guide](/blog/festival-dates-and-booking-windows) covers the festival calendar.
