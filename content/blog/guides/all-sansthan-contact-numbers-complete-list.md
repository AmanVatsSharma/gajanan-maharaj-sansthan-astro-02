# All Sansthan Contact Numbers Complete List: Every Number You Need

Knowing exactly which number to call at which location can save your entire pilgrimage from unnecessary delays. The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan operates multiple offices across Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, and each serves a slightly different purpose — booking, darshan information, medical emergencies, festival coordination, and general enquiries. This guide compiles every official contact number with its purpose, working hours, and best use case, so you can reach the right office without delay.

Bookmark this page or save it offline before you travel. The numbers listed here are the official Sansthan contacts; unofficial numbers found on Google or through third-party agents are not endorsed by the trust and may charge commissions or provide incorrect information. For guidance on verifying official contacts, see the [How To Verify Official Sansthan Contacts](/blog/how-to-verify-official-sansthan-contacts) guide.

## Main Sansthan Office — Shegaon

The main Sansthan office at Shegaon is the central point of contact for all bookings, enquiries, and coordination across all four locations. The office is located near the Samadhi Mandir and operates from 6 AM to 10 PM every day.

**Phone and WhatsApp (Bhakta Niwas Booking and General Enquiries):** +91 80531 90691

This single number handles all Bhakta Niwas bookings across the four locations — Shegaon, Omkareshwar, Pandharpur, and Trimbakeshwar. The booking team responds in English, Hindi, and Marathi. For the full contact details and to verify the number through the official website, see the [Contact Sansthan Support](/contact) page.

**Shegaon Temple Reception Desk:** Reachable through the same +91 80531 90691 number — ask to be connected to the temple reception during working hours (5 AM to 10 PM). The temple reception handles darshan timing enquiries, special darshan requests, and lost-and-found at the temple complex.

**Shegaon Bhakta Niwas Reception Desk:** Located at the Bhakta Niwas complex near the main gate. The reception operates 24 hours and can assist with check-in, check-out, room allocation queries, and any in-stay issues. Callers are connected via the central office number.

**Emergency Contact (Medical and Urgent):** During medical emergencies at Shegaon, contact the local government hospital at +91 07263 231234. For temple-related emergencies, the security desk at the temple entrance can be reached through the main office number.

## Omkareshwar Office

The Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas is managed locally but coordinated through the central Shegaon office. The local manager can be reached through the central booking number.

**Bhakta Niwas Booking (Omkareshwar):** +91 80531 90691 (same central number). Specify "Omkareshwar" in your message.

**Omkareshwar Temple Enquiries:** The temple trust office near the ghat area handles darshan timing enquiries and parikrama information. Ask the Bhakta Niwas reception desk for the local temple office number when you arrive.

**Local Medical Help:** The government dispensary near the Omkareshwar bus stand can be reached through the local tourism office. For serious emergencies, the nearest hospital is in Indore (55 km away). Save the Indore emergency number +91 731 2523344 in your phone.

## Pandharpur Office

Pandharpur's Bhakta Niwas is managed by a local coordinator under the Sansthan's network. The booking is handled centrally.

**Bhakta Niwas Booking (Pandharpur):** +91 80531 90691 (same central number). Specify "Pandharpur" in your message.

**Pandharpur Temple Reception:** The Vitthal temple reception handles darshan enquiries and special darshan requests. The local temple office number is available from the Bhakta Niwas reception desk.

**Wari Season Helpline:** During Ashadhi Wari (June-July) and Kartik Wari (November), the Sansthan sets up a dedicated helpline for pilgrims. This number is announced through local media and the Sansthan's social media channels. Bookmark the [Pandharpur Wari Accommodation Tips](/blog/pandharpur-wari-accommodation-tips) for Wari-specific contact details.

**Pandharpur Local Hospital:** The government hospital near the temple can be reached through local enquiry numbers. Ask the Bhakta Niwas reception for the exact number.

## Trimbakeshwar Office

Trimbakeshwar's Bhakta Niwas is a smaller facility that provides basic accommodation for Jyotirlinga pilgrims. The booking is handled centrally.

**Bhakta Niwas Booking (Trimbakeshwar):** +91 80531 90691 (same central number). Specify "Trimbakeshwar" in your message.

**Trimbakeshwar Temple Enquiries:** The temple trust office near the main Jyotirlinga handles darshan enquiries. The local number is available from the Bhakta Niwas reception desk.

**Nashik Hospital (Nearest Major Facility):** For serious medical issues at Trimbakeshwar, the nearest hospital is in Nashik city (30 km away). Save the Nashik emergency number in your phone. The [Omkareshwar To Trimbakeshwar Pilgrimage Checklist](/blog/omkareshwar-to-trimbakeshwar-pilgrimage-checklist) has more details on medical facilities along this route.

## When to Call the Numbers

- **+91 80531 90691:** For all bookings, cancellations, date changes, and general enquiries. Available 7 AM to 9 PM every day.
- **Local temple office:** For darshan timing enquiries, special darshan requests, and lost-and-found. Working hours vary by location but are typically 5 AM to 10 PM.
- **Bhakta Niwas reception:** For in-stay issues, check-in/check-out queries, and room problems. 24 hours at Shegaon; variable at other locations.

## Best Practices for Contacting the Sansthan

**Plan your call time carefully:** The busiest periods for the booking desk are between 10 AM and 1 PM, and again between 5 PM and 8 PM. For faster response, call between 7 AM and 9 AM, or after 8:30 PM when the call volume is lower. During festival months like Ashadhi (June-July) and Kartik (November), call volume doubles, so patience is essential.

**Keep your booking reference handy:** If you have an existing booking and need to modify it, have your confirmation details ready before calling. The team can look up your booking faster when you provide your name, dates, and location. If you booked through WhatsApp, save the conversation thread — it contains the booking reference.

**Use WhatsApp for complex requests:** When you need to modify a booking, add members to an existing reservation, or request special arrangements, WhatsApp is more effective than a phone call. You can share your booking reference, explain your request in detail, and attach screenshots of payment confirmations — all in a single message. The booking team can process these messages more efficiently than trying to handle complex requests over the phone.

**Respect quiet hours at the Bhakta Niwas:** When calling the Bhakta Niwas reception for in-stay issues, remember that most devotees sleep early and wake up early for darshan. After 10 PM, keep calls brief and essential only. The 24-hour reception desk handles genuine emergencies without issue, but avoid non-urgent requests late at night.

**Save the number offline:** Network coverage can be patchy in the area around Shegaon, especially inside the temple complex. Take a screenshot of this contact number and save it to your phone's photo gallery before you leave home. You will thank yourself if your phone loses signal at a critical moment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is +91 80531 90691 the only number I need to save?**
Yes. This single number handles bookings for all four locations, general enquiries, and temple reception connections. Save it as "Sansthan Booking" in your phone.

**Are there separate numbers for each location?**
No. The Sansthan uses a centralised booking system. The local offices at each location handle on-ground coordination but all bookings go through the central number.

**Can I call the Bhakta Niwas reception directly?**
Yes, but they will redirect you to the central number for booking-related queries. The local reception handles only in-stay issues and check-in/check-out.

**What if the central number is busy during festivals?**
Send a WhatsApp message instead — the team processes messages even during peak periods. The [WhatsApp Booking Step By Step Guide](/blog/whatsapp-booking-step-by-step-guide) explains how to use WhatsApp for booking.

**Is there an emergency number for medical help at the temple?**
Yes. The Sansthan has a first-aid station at the Shegaon temple complex. During festivals, additional medical camps are set up. For serious emergencies, call 108 (national medical emergency) or contact the nearest government hospital.

**How do I verify that I have the correct contact number?**
Visit the official website and use the [Contact Sansthan Support](/contact) page to confirm the number. The [How To Verify Official Sansthan Contacts](/blog/how-to-verify-official-sansthan-contacts) guide provides detailed verification steps.

**Are there different contact numbers for booking, darshan, and emergencies?**
The central number +91 80531 90691 handles most needs including bookings. For emergencies, use 108 (medical) or 100 (police). Local temple office numbers are available after arrival through the Bhakta Niwas reception.

**Can I send booking requests via SMS instead of WhatsApp?**
Yes. SMS booking requests are accepted at the same number +91 80531 90691, but replies are faster on WhatsApp because the team uses WhatsApp primarily.

## Common Situations and Who to Contact

**Lost luggage or personal items:** First check the Bhakta Niwas reception desk, which maintains a lost-and-found register. If the item is not found within 24 hours, contact the central booking number to escalate the issue with the local temple office. The trust maintains CCTV coverage at most Bhakta Niwas locations, and items are usually returned when reported promptly.

**Travelling with a group larger than 15 people:** For group bookings, send a single WhatsApp message to +91 80531 90691 with your complete group size, preferred dates, and the location. The booking team handles group requests separately and can arrange larger rooms or multiple adjacent rooms based on availability.

**Booking issues with payment:** If you made an advance payment and your booking is not confirmed within 48 hours, send a follow-up message with the payment screenshot and reference number. The team will investigate and respond with a resolution.

**Special dietary or medical needs during stay:** Mention these requirements at the time of booking. The Bhakta Niwas kitchen can accommodate dietary restrictions including diabetic-friendly meals, low-spice options, and pure-vegetarian (jain-style) meals with advance notice.

**Lost room key or access card:** Report to the Bhakta Niwas reception desk immediately. The trust has spare keys and can issue replacements without charge during your stay.
