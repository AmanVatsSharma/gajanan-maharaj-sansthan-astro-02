# Hyderabad to Omkareshwar: Route Guide for Devotees

The journey from Hyderabad to Omkareshwar covers roughly 750 kilometres of central Indian terrain, crossing from Telangana into Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh before reaching the sacred island of Mandhata in the Narmada River. For devotees in and around Hyderabad who wish to visit the Jyotirlinga at Omkareshwar, several transport options exist — each with different trade-offs in cost, duration, and comfort. The good news is that the final approach to the temple via Indore is well-serviced by road, rail, and air, making Omkareshwar more accessible than many pilgrims assume. The route is popular among Telugu-speaking devotees who combine it with visits to nearby Jyotirlingas, and the Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas at Omkareshwar offers a comfortable resting point for travellers on the Hyderabad-Indore corridor.

This route guide covers the three main ways to get from Hyderabad to Omkareshwar: by flight, by train, and by road, with specific recommendations for families, senior citizens, and budget-conscious travellers. The information here is current as of 2026 and reflects prevailing flight schedules, train timings, and road conditions. If you are coming from a different city, the [Complete Transport Guide for Devotees](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) covers broader multi-city routes.

## Option 1: Hyderabad to Indore by Flight

The fastest route is a direct flight from Hyderabad's Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA) to Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport in Indore. Multiple airlines operate daily flights, and the journey takes approximately 1 hour and 40 minutes. Flight prices vary from INR 2,500 to INR 8,000 depending on how far in advance you book and whether you catch a sale period. Weekday morning flights are typically cheaper than weekend evening flights. Travelling with elderly parents makes the flight option especially valuable because it eliminates the physical strain of a 14-hour train journey.

From Indore airport, hire a pre-paid taxi to Omkareshwar. The distance is about 130 kilometres, and the drive takes 3 to 3.5 hours through the Malwa plateau. Taxis are available at the airport counter and typically charge INR 1,800 to INR 2,500 for a one-way trip to Omkareshwar. You can also pre-book through Ola or Uber, although availability drops dramatically once you leave Indore city limits. For families of four to five, sharing a taxi works out cheaper than bus fare per person.

Shared cabs on the Indore-Omkareshwar route cost less — around INR 300 to INR 500 per person — but they wait for multiple passengers to fill up, which can add an hour or more to your journey. These cabs depart from designated points in Indore rather than from the airport, so you would need to first reach the departure point from the airport.

Book your Omkareshwar accommodation before you fly. The [Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas Accommodation Guide](/blog/locations/omkareshwar/omkareshwar-bhakta-niwas-accommodation-guide) explains how to reserve a room at the Sansthan's accommodation near the temple. During festival periods, rooms can fill up weeks in advance, and arriving without a booking is risky.

## Option 2: Hyderabad to Indore by Train

For devotees who prefer rail travel, several trains connect Hyderabad to Indore. The most convenient option is the Indore Rajkot Express or the Ahilya Nagari Express, both of which run regularly. The journey takes approximately 14 to 16 hours, and you will want to book an AC 2-tier or AC 3-tier berth for comfort. Sleeper class is available at lower fares but can be tiring for a full-night journey. Lower berths should be requested at the time of booking through IRCTC — they are not automatically assigned even to senior citizens.

From Indore Junction, the approach to Omkareshwar is straightforward. State transport buses leave from Sarwate Bus Stand every 30 minutes from 6 AM to 9 PM. The bus fare is INR 100 to INR 150 per person, and the journey takes about 2.5 hours. The buses drop you near the Omkareshwar temple entrance, from where it is a short walk to the Bhakta Niwas. The buses are reasonably comfortable but can be crowded during festival periods.

Alternatively, hire a taxi from Indore railway station. The cost is INR 1,200 to INR 1,800 for a one-way trip. This is the better option if you are travelling with elderly family members or carrying heavy luggage, as the bus can be crowded and the bus stand is some distance from the temple. Pre-paid taxi counters are available at Indore Junction — avoid drivers who approach you directly inside the station premises.

## Option 3: Hyderabad to Indore by Road

Driving from Hyderabad to Indore covers approximately 800 kilometres and takes 14 to 16 hours, depending on traffic and stops. The route passes through Nanded, Akola, and Burhanpur — a culturally rich corridor with several temples worth a brief stop if you have the time. National Highway 52 forms the majority of the route, and road conditions are generally good. The drive is best broken into two legs with an overnight stop. A good halfway point is Burhanpur, which has decent hotels and is historically significant. From Burhanpur, the remaining 350 kilometres to Indore take about 6 hours.

If you are driving yourself, fill up at major towns — fuel stations can be sparse in the rural stretches between Burhanpur and Khandwa. Carry water and snacks for the journey, and plan to drive during daylight hours only. The highway has heavy truck traffic at night, and the rural stretches are poorly lit, making night driving risky. A co-driver who can share the wheel is highly recommended for this route.

## From Indore to Omkareshwar: The Final Leg

Regardless of which transport option you choose, all routes converge at Indore, and from Indore to Omkareshwar is a consistent 130-kilometre stretch. The road descends from the Malwa plateau into the Narmada valley, and the scenery becomes more dramatic as you approach the river. The temple on Mandhata island comes into view about 20 kilometres before you reach the bridge. The final descent into the Narmada valley is steep, and motion-sensitive travellers should keep medication handy.

State transport buses are the most economical option at INR 100 to INR 150 per person. Private taxis cost INR 1,200 to INR 1,800. If you are travelling in a group of four or more, sharing a taxi works out cheaper per person than the bus, and it is far more comfortable. The buses stop frequently along the route to pick up passengers, which can extend the journey time, while taxis take you directly to the temple ghat.

Once you arrive at Omkareshwar, walk across the bridge to the temple island. The [Omkareshwar Darshan Timing Guide](/blog/omkareshwar-darshan-timings) has everything you need to plan your visit — including the best time for aarti, the parikrama route around the island, and tips for first-time visitors.

## Practical Tips for Hyderabad Devotees

Plan your travel around the monsoon season with caution. The roads from Indore to Omkareshwar can become slippery during July and August, and the Narmada River levels rise significantly. If you are travelling during this period, check weather forecasts and road conditions before departing. The Khandwa-Omkareshwar stretch is particularly prone to flooding during heavy rain.

Carry sufficient water and snacks for the Indore-Omkareshwar leg. There are limited food options along the route, especially between Khandwa and the temple. Highway dhabas at Khandwa offer basic thalis at INR 80 to INR 120, but the cleanliness varies.

Book return transport in advance if you are catching a flight or train. During festival seasons, taxis at Omkareshwar are in high demand and fares can increase by 30 to 40 percent. The Bhakta Niwas reception can help you arrange a return taxi, often at more reasonable rates than the open-market taxis outside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far is Omkareshwar from Hyderabad by road?**
Approximately 750 kilometres. The journey takes 14 to 16 hours by car and can be broken into two legs with an overnight stop in Burhanpur.

**What is the cheapest way to travel from Hyderabad to Omkareshwar?**
The train from Hyderabad to Indore, followed by a state transport bus from Indore to Omkareshwar, is the most economical option. Total cost per person is approximately INR 1,500 to INR 2,500 depending on train class.

**Are there direct trains from Hyderabad to Omkareshwar?**
No. The nearest railway station to Omkareshwar is Khandwa Junction, about 60 kilometres away. Most devotees find it more convenient to reach Indore and then travel by road to the temple.

**How much does a taxi from Indore to Omkareshwar cost?**
INR 1,200 to INR 1,800 for a one-way trip in a sedan. Shared cabs cost INR 300 to INR 500 per person but may wait for other passengers.

**Is it safe to drive from Hyderabad to Omkareshwar at night?**
No. National Highway 52 has heavy truck traffic at night, and the rural stretches between Burhanpur and Khandwa are poorly lit. Plan to drive during daylight hours and break the journey into manageable legs.

**Can I visit Omkareshwar as a day trip from Indore?**
Yes. Many devotees stay in Indore and make a day trip to Omkareshwar. The bus journey takes about 5 hours round trip, which is tight for a comfortable darshan experience. An overnight stay at the [Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas](/locations/omkareshwar-bhakt-niwas) is strongly recommended.
