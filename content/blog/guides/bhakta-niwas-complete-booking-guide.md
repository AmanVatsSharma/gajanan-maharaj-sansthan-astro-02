When the Sharmas from Nagpur arrived at Shegaon on Pragat Din weekend without a pre-booked room, they learned the most important lesson about Bhakta Niwas booking the hard way: demand during festival periods is not just high — it is existential. Every available room filled within hours of the announcement, and they spent the night at a guest house 12 kilometres away, making three trips to the temple by auto-rickshaw the next day. Had they booked two weeks earlier, they could have secured a room at Bhakt Niwas itself and woken up inside the temple gates. This guide covers everything you need to know to avoid that situation, from selecting the right room type to handling cancellations and special requirements.

## Before you book: decide your dates

The first step is to decide your dates with an awareness of the Sansthan's festival calendar. Gajanan Maharaj's Pragat Din in February — marking the saint's first appearance in February 1878 — and Rishi Panchami in September — marking the mahasamadhi on 8 September 1910 — are the two busiest periods at Shegaon. Accommodation fills completely two to three weeks before each event. Navaratri and Diwali also draw larger crowds, though the spike is more moderate. For off-peak months, one to two weeks ahead is sufficient. The [shegaon-travel-guide](/blog/shegaon-travel-guide) includes a month-by-month climate overview so you can choose the right season before you choose your dates.

Estimate your group size honestly before looking at room types. Private rooms at Bhakt Niwas are reserved for families with a minimum of three members, and this rule is enforced at check-in. Solo travellers and couples without children should use the Bhandara dormitory accommodation.

## How to book: three official channels

**Option 1: Online booking form.** Visit the [booking page](/booking) and fill in your arrival date, departure date, group size, room preference, and contact number. You will receive a reference number by return message. This option is available 24/7 and works equally well from Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, or overseas.

**Option 2: WhatsApp.** Send a message to the official Sansthan WhatsApp number with the following details in a single message: names of all guests, arrival and departure dates, number of adults and children with ages, preferred room type with one alternative, and ID proof type you will carry. WhatsApp is the fastest channel during festival weeks because the team monitors it continuously.

**Option 3: Phone call.** Call during morning desk hours (8am to 12pm) or evening desk hours (6pm to 9pm). The evening desk is generally more responsive because the morning desk is clearing the overnight backlog. The phone number is listed on the [Sansthan contact page](/contact). Calling during the first 30 minutes of each shift window — 8am or 6pm — gives you the best chance of getting through.

All three channels route to the same back-end team. Do not submit the same request across two channels; the Sansthan office will see both and may mark one as a duplicate.

## What to have ready before you call or fill the form

Gather these details before you initiate contact: ID proof type for every adult guest (Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, or driving licence — the specific type matters because the front desk verifies it at check-in), confirmed arrival and departure dates, the exact number of adults and children with each child's age, your preferred room type with one backup option, any dietary requirements for the Mahaprasad Hall, and any accessibility needs such as ground-floor preference for senior citizens or wheelchair access.

## Room types and exact pricing

Here are the nine room categories at Bhakt Niwas with their current all-inclusive rates. Every rate includes bedding, towels, and morning tea — there are no hidden charges.

The budget dormitory options are the 2-Bed Non-AC at 1,250 rupees per night and the 3-Bed Non-AC at 1,750 rupees. Both are clean, well-ventilated rooms with attached bathrooms and hot water, ideal for devotees who prioritize proximity to the temple over air conditioning.

The mid-range options — the most popular choices for typical family visits — are the 2-Bed AC at 1,650 rupees, the 3-Bed AC at 2,050 rupees, and the 4-Bed AC at 2,550 rupees. The 4-Bed AC at 2,550 rupees is the sweet spot for a family of four: comfortable, air-conditioned, spacious, and reasonably priced. The 4-Bed Non-AC at 2,250 rupees is the natural alternative for families who prefer natural ventilation.

For devotees seeking more comfort, the Deluxe Suite at 3,150 rupees and the Family Room at 3,850 rupees offer premium amenities with larger floor areas. The Family Room accommodates up to six people, making it the right choice for extended family groups travelling together from Mumbai or Pune — both roughly 570 kilometres from Shegaon. The Luxury Suite at 4,150 rupees is the top-end option. All nine room types share the same 24-hour check-in and check-out policy, so your choice of room type does not affect your arrival flexibility.

## The booking confirmation process

After you submit a request, the Sansthan team reviews availability and sends a confirmation within two to four hours during normal periods. During festival weeks, response times may stretch to six to eight hours. Your confirmation includes the reference number, the room type you requested, the dates, and a request ID. Keep this message — take a screenshot and save it to your phone — because you will need to show it at the Bhakt Niwas front desk on arrival.

Your confirmation guarantees the room type and dates, not a specific room number. The exact room is assigned at the check-in counter on arrival based on availability. Arriving before noon gives you a significantly wider choice than arriving after 9pm.

## What to do if your preferred dates are full

This happens more often than first-time visitors expect, especially during February Pragat Din and September Rishi Panchami. Three options exist: the waitlist for last-minute cancellations, Anand Vihar as a quieter alternative with availability, or shifting your dates by one or two days on either side when demand drops sharply. Contact the [Sansthan office](/contact) to explore all three.

## Cancellation and refund policy

The Sansthan's cancellation policy is straightforward. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled check-in time receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours — including same-day cancellations — are not refundable. During festival periods, the policy becomes stricter: festival-week cancellations typically require seven or more days' notice for any refund, and no-shows during Pragat Din or Rishi Panchami are charged the full room rate. Always confirm the specific cancellation terms for your booking dates at the time of confirmation. Submit cancellation requests in writing — WhatsApp or email — with your booking reference number.

## Arrival and check-in process

Check-in at Bhakt Niwas operates 24 hours, which means your room is available when you arrive regardless of the hour. If you arrive after 11pm, call ahead to ensure the duty staff knows you are coming and can let you in through the night gate. Bring two items to the front desk: your booking confirmation — printed or digital on your phone — and a government-issued photo ID for every adult guest. Children under 18 do not need separate ID but must be accompanied by an adult whose ID is on the registration form.

The check-in process takes 10 to 15 minutes for a standard booking. You will sign a simple registration form, receive your room key and a campus map, and get a brief orientation covering the Mahaprasad timings, the temple aarti schedule, and emergency contact numbers. If your room is not ready — common around 11am during the check-out turnover — you can deposit your luggage, take a quick darshan at the Samadhi Mandir, and return for a peaceful check-in once the room is prepared.

## Late arrival and special requirements

If your train or bus is delayed and you expect to arrive after 11pm, call the Bhakt Niwas front desk as soon as you know about the delay. The duty staff can hold your room and arrange access after hours. This is a common occurrence during monsoon season, when train delays of three to six hours are not unusual.

For senior citizens, ground-floor rooms are available on request at no extra charge. If you or a travelling companion uses a wheelchair, inform the Sansthan during booking so that an accessible room can be allocated. Wheelchairs are available at the temple entrance on request from the volunteer desk. For diet-specific meals at the Mahaprasad Hall — Jain food, diabetic-friendly options, or allergy accommodations — provide at least 48 hours' notice during the booking process.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the minimum number of people required to book a private room?** Private rooms require a minimum of three members. Solo travellers and couples without children should use the Bhandara dormitory accommodation instead. This rule is enforced at check-in and cannot be waived.

**What ID proof is required at check-in?** Any government-issued photo ID: Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, PAN card, or driving licence. Every adult guest must present their original ID — the Sansthan maintains a security register, and this is a mandatory requirement.

**What are the exact room rates at Bhakt Niwas?** The nine room types and their all-inclusive per-night rates are: 2-Bed Non-AC at 1,250 rupees, 3-Bed Non-AC at 1,750, 2-Bed AC at 1,650, 3-Bed AC at 2,050, 4-Bed AC at 2,550, 4-Bed Non-AC at 2,250, Deluxe Suite at 3,150, Family Room at 3,850, and Luxury Suite at 4,150. All rates include bedding, towels, and morning tea.

**Can I cancel my booking and get a refund?** Yes, if you cancel more than 24 hours before your scheduled check-in time. Cancellations within 24 hours — including same-day cancellations — receive no refund. Festival-week bookings follow stricter terms, typically requiring seven days' notice for any refund.

**Do I get a specific room number when I book?** No. Your booking confirms the room type, dates, and group details. The specific room number is allocated at the check-in counter on arrival. Arriving before noon gives you the widest selection from the available rooms.

**What should I do if my preferred dates are completely full?** Ask about the waitlist for cancellations, request Anand Vihar as an alternative, or consider shifting your dates by one or two days on either side. Demand drops sharply on days adjacent to festival dates, and shifting by even 24 hours often resolves availability. Contact the [Sansthan office](/contact) to explore all three options.

**Are unmarried couples permitted to stay at Bhakt Niwas?** No. The Sansthan's booking rules specify that accommodation is for families and groups only. Unmarried couples are not permitted, and this policy is consistently enforced across all Sansthan accommodation properties.

## The booking that begins the yatra

The moment your confirmation message arrives — whether by WhatsApp, email, or as a reply to your phone call — is the moment your yatra begins in earnest. The days between confirmation and departure are an opportunity to prepare practically and spiritually: read the [gajanan-maharaj-sansthan-complete-guide](/blog/gajanan-maharaj-sansthan-complete-guide) for context about Shegaon's history and facilities, pack your cloth bag and refillable water bottle following the eco-friendly practices described in the [eco-friendly-pilgrimage-practices](/blog/eco-friendly-pilgrimage-practices) guide, and set a sankalp — a clear inner intention — for what you want this visit to accomplish. The booking is the logistical foundation. The visit is the spiritual experience. Both are necessary, and neither is sufficient without the other.
