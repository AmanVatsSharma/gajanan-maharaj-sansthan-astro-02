# How to Reach Shegaon: Train & Bus Guide from Every Major City

Shegaon sits on the Nagpur–Bhusaval railway line in the Buldhana district of Maharashtra, at a convenient point for road and rail travel from Vidarbha and parts of central India. It is not a large junction, but it receives enough trains that most major cities — Nagpur, Mumbai, Pune, Akola, Amravati, Wardha — can reach it in a single direct journey. This guide covers the distances, train timings, bus options, and practical arrival tips for each origin city.

## Shegaon's location and connectivity

Shegaon is on the Howrah–Nagpur–Mumbai line, which means it sits on one of the busiest rail corridors in central India. The station code is **SEG**. Most trains that run between Nagpur and Bhusaval/Akola stop at Shegaon, which gives you a wide choice of timings without needing to change trains. By road, the town is connected to Khamgaon (12 km), Balapur (18 km), Akola (75 km), and Nagpur (about 110–115 km).

## From Nagpur

**Distance:** ~110–115 km  
**Train time:** 1.5 to 2.5 hours (most trains from Nagpur to Akola or Bhusaval stop at Shegaon)  
**Bus time:** 2.5 to 3 hours via the Nagpur–Khamgaon–Shegaon highway

Nagpur is the most practical starting point for a Shegaon visit. The MSRTC (Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation) runs regular bus services from Nagpur to Khamgaon, and from Khamgaon you can take a local bus or shared rickshaw to Shegaon (12 km). By train, the Nagpur–Bhusaval line has multiple daily services that stop at Shegaon. The journey from Nagpur takes under two hours on most express trains. The Ajni–Khamgaon MEMU is the fastest option.

## From Mumbai

**Distance:** ~500 km  
**Train time:** 6 to 8 hours depending on the train  
**Bus time:** 8 to 10 hours (direct MSRTC sleeper bus services are available from Mumbai to Khamgaon or Akola)

Mumbai to Shegaon is a longer journey but doable in a single day. The trains that go from Mumbai to Nagpur and continue to the east (Vidarbha) stop at Shegaon. The Devagiri Express from Mumbai CST and the Gitanjali Express from Mumbai are commonly used. The journey is overnight or early morning — planning an overnight train and arriving at Shegaon at dawn for the first morning darshan is a popular itinerary.

## From Pune

**Distance:** ~580 km  
**Train time:** 8 to 10 hours  
**Bus time:** 10 to 12 hours (limited direct services; most travelers connect through Nagpur or Aurangabad)

Pune to Shegaon is the longest of the main Maharashtra routes. There are fewer direct trains, so most Pune-based devotees take the Pune–Nagpur express and then a connecting train or bus from Nagpur to Shegaon. The train journey from Pune to Nagpur takes about 6 hours, and Nagpur to Shegaon takes about 2 hours.

## From Akola

**Distance:** ~75 km  
**Train time:** 1.5 to 2 hours  
**Bus time:** 1.5 to 2 hours via the Akola–Khamgaon–Shegaon road

Akola is the nearest major city, and the Akola–Shegaon route is well-connected by both train and road. The MSRTC buses run frequently from Akola to Khamgaon, and Khamgaon to Shegaon is a 12 km shared rickshaw or local bus. By train, several daily services between Akola and Bhusaval stop at Shegaon.

## From Amravati

**Distance:** ~95 km  
**Train time:** 2 to 2.5 hours  
**Bus time:** 2 to 2.5 hours

Amravati to Shegaon is a short and comfortable journey. MSRTC buses run regularly from Amravati to Khamgaon. From Khamgaon, local transport covers the remaining 12 km.

## From Wardha

**Distance:** ~140 km  
**Train time:** 2.5 to 3 hours  
**Bus time:** 3 to 3.5 hours via Wardha–Yavatmal–Khamgaon

Wardha is connected by train on the same Nagpur–Bhusaval line. Trains from Wardha to Akola or Bhusaval stop at Shegaon. Road connectivity is also good through the Wardha–Yavatmal route.

## From Hyderabad

**Distance:** ~420 km  
**Train time:** 6 to 7 hours  
**Bus time:** 7 to 8 hours

Hyderabad has direct trains to Akola that stop at Shegaon. The Nanded–Akola–Shegaon route is also an option for travelers from Telangana and northern Andhra. Several express trains between Secunderabad and Bhusaval or Akola stop at Shegaon.

## Arrival at Shegaon station

Shegaon station (SEG) is a small but functional station. When you arrive:

- **Shared rickshaws:** Available at the station entrance for the short ride to the Sansthan campus (typically ₹20–₹50 per person).
- **Sansthan shuttle:** The Sansthan runs a free shuttle bus between the station and the mandir campus at regular intervals, especially during festivals.
- **Auto-rickshaw:** Local autos are available and will take you to the mandir, the Bhakta Niwas, or the Anand Sagar entrance.

The station to temple distance is about 1.5 km. The walk is short but can be uncomfortable in summer heat; use a rickshaw if arriving during the day in peak summer.

## Travel tips

**Best travel season:** October to February is ideal — comfortable temperatures, dry roads, good train punctuality. The summer (April to June) is extremely hot, and the monsoon (July to September) brings flooding to some rural roads.

**Traveling with family:** Book the AC coach for train travel. For bus travel, the MSRTC Volvo services are the most comfortable. If you are traveling with children or elderly family members, a direct train from Nagpur is the least stressful option.

**Carrying luggage:** The Sansthan has cloakroom facilities near the mandir. You can leave your bags at the cloakroom and collect them after darshan.

**Booking accommodation in advance:** Pragat Din (February 12) and Samadhi Utsav (August–September) require advance booking. See our [Bhakta Niwas tariff guide](/bhakt-niwas-room-tariff-room-types-2026) for rates and [Shegaon accommodation guide](/shegaon-accommodation-guide) for alternatives.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What is the nearest airport to Shegaon?**
A: Nagpur (Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, NAG) is the nearest airport, about 110 km away. From Nagpur, take a taxi or train to Shegaon. The drive from Nagpur takes about 2.5 hours.

**Q: Are there direct buses from Mumbai?**
A: Yes. MSRTC runs overnight sleeper bus services from Mumbai to Khamgaon (near Shegaon). From Khamgaon, it is a 12 km bus or rickshaw to Shegaon. The total journey is about 8 to 10 hours.

**Q: What is the best train from Nagpur?**
A: The Nagpur–Akola MEMU and the Nagpur–Bhusaval Passenger are the most convenient — frequent stops, short journey time, and easy booking. The Ajni–Khamgaon MEMU is the fastest option.

**Q: Can I hire a taxi from Nagpur?**
A: Yes. Taxis are available from Nagpur to Shegaon for approximately ₹2,000–₹3,000 one way. This is a good option if you are traveling in a group or want to combine Shegaon with stops at other sites.

**Q: How do I book train tickets?**
A: Use the IRCTC website or app. The Sansthan does not handle train bookings. Book as far in advance as possible — train berths on the Nagpur–Bhusaval line sell out quickly during Pragat Din and other festival periods.
