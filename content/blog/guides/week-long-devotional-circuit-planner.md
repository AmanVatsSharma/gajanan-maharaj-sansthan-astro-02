# Week-Long Devotional Circuit Planner | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

A well-planned week-long devotional circuit is the single most efficient way to visit multiple Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan locations in a single trip. The standard circuit runs Shegaon to Omkareshwar to Pandharpur and back, covering the Samadhi Mandir, the Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga, and the Pandharpur Vitthal temple — three of the most spiritually significant sites in the Shri Gajanan Maharaj tradition. The total road distance for the full circuit is approximately 800-900 km depending on the exact route, and a comfortable 7-8 day schedule allows you to spend quality time at each location without feeling rushed. This planner is designed for devotees who want to take a single block of annual leave and return with a deeply fulfilling multi-location yatra experience. It assumes you are starting from Mumbai or another major western Maharashtra city, driving your own vehicle or hiring a private taxi for the entire circuit.

## The Complete 7-Day Circuit: Day-by-Day Schedule

**Day 1 — Mumbai to Shegaon (arrive evening):** Depart Mumbai CST by the 12139 Sewagram Express at 8:55 PM on Friday, arriving Shegaon at 8:00 AM on Saturday. Check in to Bhakta Niwas, drop luggage, and head directly to the temple for darshan. The Mangala Aarti at 7:30-8:30 AM is the morning priority. After darshan and breakfast at the Mahaprasad canteen, rest for the morning and plan the afternoon at Anand Sagar. Evening Mangala Aarti at 6:00 PM, dinner at the canteen, and an early night because tomorrow is the long drive to Omkareshwar.

**Day 2 — Shegaon to Omkareshwar (arrive evening):** Wake at 5:00 AM, pack, settle the Bhakta Niwas bill, and do a final darshan at the temple. Depart Shegaon by 7:00 AM. The drive to Omkareshwar is 340 km and 6-7 hours via Khamgaon, Mehkar, and Khandwa. Break for lunch and tea near Khandwa. Arrive Omkareshwar by 2:00 PM, do the afternoon darshan at the Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga, walk around the island, attend the Narmada aarti at sunset, and check into your Omkareshwar accommodation for the night.

**Day 3 — Omkareshwar (full day):** Full day at Omkareshwar. Pre-dawn darshan, morning walk around the Mandhata island, visit the Mamaleshwar Jyotirlinga, explore the Fort of Mandhata for panoramic Narmada valley views, afternoon rest, and the Narmada aarti at sunset. This is the most relaxed day of the circuit and the day to spend the most time on the island.

**Day 4 — Omkareshwar to Pandharpur (arrive evening):** The road distance from Omkareshwar to Pandharpur is approximately 480 km and 9-10 hours via Burhanpur, Maharashtra border, and the Wainganga River road. This is the longest stretch of the circuit, and you should start early — 5:00 AM departure, arrive Pandharpur by 3:00-4:00 PM. The route passes through the Burhanpur-Akola belt and requires one 45-minute break for lunch around Burhanpur. Check into Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas, freshen up, and proceed to the Vitthal temple for evening darshan.

**Day 5 — Pandharpur (full day):** Full day at Pandharpur. The Vitthal temple has a very different atmosphere from Shegaon and Omkareshwar. The morning aarti, the Rukmini temple, the Govardhan puja timing, and the Krishna bhajans are all distinctive. Walk along the Chandrabhaga river ghats, visit the Pundalik samadhi, and spend the afternoon resting at Bhakta Niwas Pandharpur. Evening darshan and dinner.

**Day 6 — Pandharpur to Shegaon (arrive evening):** The return journey from Pandharpur to Shegaon is 320 km and 6-7 hours via Khamgaon and Shegaon's main approach road. This is the reverse of the earlier leg. Arrive Shegaon by evening, check into Bhakta Niwas, and have one last darshan at the temple. Evening Mangala Aarti and dinner.

**Day 7 — Shegaon to Mumbai (depart evening):** Final morning at Shegaon. 6:00 AM darshan, breakfast, and departure by the 12140 Sewagram Express at 5:00 PM, arriving Mumbai CST at 4:30 AM Monday morning, in time for work on Monday.

This 7-day schedule gives you three full days of darshan and rest, three days of driving, and one buffer day. For devotees who want a more relaxed circuit, the 8-day version adds one extra day at each location, giving you more time at the temples and reducing the driving pressure.

## Budget Breakdown

The approximate cost of the 7-day circuit, per person, sharing a car with two other devotees, is as follows:

- **Transport (shared taxi):** INR 8,000-12,000 per person for the entire circuit (shared among 3 devotees, petrol, tolls, and driver stay).
- **Bhakta Niwas accommodation (all locations):** INR 2,500-4,000 per person for 6 nights (sharing rooms).
- **Meals (all locations):** INR 2,500-3,500 per person for 7 days (Mahaprasad canteen meals and Guruprasad restaurants).
- **Darshan and temple donations:** INR 500-1,000 per person (optional).
- **Pocket expenses:** INR 1,000-2,000 per person for parking, auto-rickshaws, small purchases.

**Total approximate cost: INR 14,500-23,000 per person** for a 7-day devotional circuit covering three major locations. This is an excellent value considering the spiritual significance of each site and the logistical complexity of a three-location itinerary.

## How to Book the Circuit

The most efficient approach is to send a single WhatsApp message to the Sansthan office listing all three locations with your preferred dates. The Sansthan office handles multi-location bookings as a unit and confirms all locations in the same response. Book at least 4-6 weeks in advance, especially if your trip overlaps a festival period. The [Bhakta Niwas booking page](/booking) has the contact details, and the [multi-location comparison guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-across-locations-comparison) explains the differences between the Bhakta Niwas properties at each location.

For the private taxi, book in advance through your local taxi operator or through a Shegaon-based operator who can provide the car for the entire circuit. A Shegaon-based driver who knows the route to Omkareshwar and Pandharpur is preferable to one who does not, because the route involves some rural stretches and the driver's familiarity with the roads matters. The [complete transport guide](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) lists taxi operators and their contact details.

## Seasonal Considerations

The best time for the circuit is October to February, when the weather is mild across all three locations. Shegaon is hot from March to May (temperatures above 40 degrees), and the long drive days are uncomfortable in summer. The monsoon months of July to September bring rain across the entire circuit, which affects driving and darshan schedules but does not make the trip impossible. The festival periods of Ashadhi Ekadashi (July) at Shegaon, the Narmada Pushkaram at Omkareshwar (every 12 years), and the Ashadhi Ekadashi at Pandharpur (July-August) are the most spiritually intense times to visit but also the most crowded. The [festival rush booking strategies](/blog/festival-rush-booking-strategies) guide covers how to plan around festivals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I do the circuit by public transport?** It is technically possible but very inconvenient. Trains connect Mumbai to Shegaon, Khandwa to Omkareshwar, and Solapur to Pandharpur, but the inter-city connections between Shegaon, Omkareshwar, and Pandharpur are sparse. A private car or taxi is strongly recommended.

**Can I extend the circuit to Trimbakeshwar?** Yes. Trimbakeshwar adds another 2-3 days to the circuit and approximately 400 km of driving. The [10-day circuit with Trimbakeshwar](/blog/week-long-devotional-circuit-planner) is described in the [senior devotee multi-city itinerary guide](/blog/senior-devotee-multi-city-itinerary).

**What if a location is fully booked?** The Sansthan will offer you the nearest available dates. If Shegaon is full but Omkareshwar and Pandharpur are available, you can start from Omkareshwar and still complete a meaningful circuit.

**Can I do the circuit solo?** Yes. Many solo devotees complete the circuit using shared taxis or overnight buses between cities. Staying at Bhakta Niwas connects you with other devotees, and the devotional community at each location is welcoming to solo travellers.

**How early do I need to book?** 4-6 weeks for regular seasons, 8-10 weeks for festival periods. Book early and book all three locations together.

**Is it safe to drive these routes at night?** No. All driving should be done during daylight hours. The rural stretches between Shegaon and Omkareshwar, and between Omkareshwar and Pandharpur, have poor lighting and are not safe to drive at night. Depart early morning each day to arrive at your destination before sunset.
