# WhatsApp Booking Troubleshooting | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

WhatsApp is the primary booking channel for the Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas, and for most devotees it works smoothly — you send your details, the booking desk confirms within a few hours, and you are set. But there are moments when the WhatsApp booking process does not go as planned: the message goes unanswered, the confirmation is unclear, the payment arrangement is confusing, or the booking status is not what you expected. These situations are almost always resolvable, but they require a specific approach rather than frustration or repeated identical messages. This guide covers the most common WhatsApp booking issues, explains why they happen, and provides the exact steps to resolve each one. The [phone and WhatsApp booking best practices guide](/blog/phone-and-whatsapp-booking-best-practices) covers the optimal booking workflow from the start, and the [emergency contact guide](/blog/emergency-contact-during-pilgrimage) lists alternative contact channels when WhatsApp is not working.

## Issue 1: No response to your WhatsApp message

**Why it happens:** The booking desk receives a high volume of messages, especially during festival seasons. Messages without complete information (missing dates, headcount, room preferences) require a follow-up from the desk before they can be processed, and in a busy period, the desk may prioritise complete messages over incomplete ones.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Check your message for completeness.** Does your message include: exact check-in and check-out dates, total headcount with age breakdown, room type preferences, and your contact number? If any of these are missing, the booking desk cannot process your request and may not have sent a follow-up. Resend the message with all details included. The [phone and WhatsApp booking best practices guide](/blog/phone-and-whatsapp-booking-best-practices) provides the complete checklist.

2. **Check the timing.** If you sent the message during lunch hours (1:00 PM to 3:00 PM), late evening (after 9:00 PM), or early morning (before 8:00 AM), it may not have been processed yet. Wait until the next processing window and send a polite follow-up.

3. **Call instead of WhatsApp.** If you have sent a complete message and not received a response within 4 hours during regular hours, call +91 80531 90691 directly. A phone call gets immediate attention and allows you to explain your situation in real time. This is the most effective escalation step for unanswered messages.

4. **Use the web booking form as a backup.** If WhatsApp is genuinely not working — messages are not being delivered or not being read — use the [booking form](/booking) on this website as an alternative channel. Different staff members monitor different channels, and a web form submission may reach someone who can help.

## Issue 2: Unclear or partial confirmation

**Why it happens:** During busy periods, the booking desk may send a brief confirmation that does not include all the details you need — for example, confirming that a room is available but not specifying which room type, or confirming dates but not the tariff.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Ask for a complete confirmation in writing.** Reply to the booking message with: "Thank you for the confirmation. Can you please confirm the following details: room type, tariff per night, check-in time, and whether meals are included?" A written confirmation with all these details is your booking record.

2. **Do not rely on verbal confirmations.** If the booking desk confirmed your booking over a phone call, ask them to also send the confirmation on WhatsApp for your records. Verbal confirmations can be misunderstood or forgotten, and a written confirmation protects both you and the Sansthan.

3. **If the confirmation is conditional (waitlist, subject to availability), ask for clarity.** Some confirmations are tentative — "we will try to accommodate you." Ask the booking desk to confirm whether your booking is firm or conditional, and what the next steps are if it is conditional.

## Issue 3: Payment confusion

**Why it happens:** The payment process for Bhakta Niwas bookings can be unclear, especially for first-time bookers. The Sansthan accepts different payment methods at different locations, and the payment instructions may not be included in the initial confirmation message.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Ask for payment instructions in your confirmation reply.** When you receive a booking confirmation, reply with: "Please confirm the payment method (cash, UPI, bank transfer) and whether an advance deposit is required." The booking desk will provide the specific payment details for your booking.

2. **Do not make payments to unofficial numbers.** Always verify the payment account details through the [official contact page](/contact) or by calling the booking desk. Never make a payment to a number that was not provided by the Sansthan.

3. **Keep payment receipts.** Whether you pay by cash, UPI, or bank transfer, keep the receipt or transaction reference. You will need this at check-in to verify your payment.

4. **If you have already made a payment and have not received confirmation:** Send the payment receipt to the booking desk on WhatsApp and ask them to confirm the booking with your payment reference. Payments are typically matched to booking requests within 24 hours, but during busy periods, this may take longer.

## Issue 4: Booking does not match your requirements

**Why it happens:** The booking desk may allocate a different room type than the one you requested, or the dates may not match exactly what you specified. This usually happens when the exact room type you requested is unavailable and the desk allocates an alternative without explaining the change.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Review the confirmation carefully.** Compare the confirmed details with your original request. If there are discrepancies, reply immediately with: "I requested [original request details] but the confirmation shows [confirmed details]. Can you please clarify or adjust?"

2. **Request a room change at check-in.** If the allocated room type is not suitable, request a change at the Bhakta Niwas reception at the time of check-in. Room changes on the day of arrival are more manageable than changes requested after you have settled in.

3. **For group bookings, verify room assignments.** If you booked multiple rooms for a group, confirm the room numbers and assignments in the booking confirmation. Ask the booking desk to provide a room allocation list before your arrival so that you can distribute room assignments to your group members in advance.

## Issue 5: Cannot reach the booking desk by phone

**Why it happens:** The booking desk phone line (+91 80531 90691) can be busy during peak hours, especially during festival seasons when dozens of devotees are calling simultaneously.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Try WhatsApp instead of calling.** WhatsApp messages do not tie up the phone line and can be processed between calls. Send a message with your query and your phone number, and the booking desk will call you back when they are free.

2. **Try calling during off-peak hours.** The booking desk is busiest between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM, and between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM. Try calling during the 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM lunch window or after 6:00 PM for better response rates.

3. **Use the web contact form.** The [contact form](/contact) on this website reaches a different staff member and can be used when both WhatsApp and phone are not working. Responses to web form submissions typically come within 24 hours.

## Issue 6: Your booking was cancelled without notice

**Why it happens:** This is rare but can happen during festival periods when the Bhakta Niwas is overbooked and a higher-priority booking replaces yours. It can also happen if the advance payment for your booking was not received within the required timeframe.

**How to resolve it:**

1. **Contact the booking desk immediately.** Call +91 80531 90691 and ask for an explanation and a solution. The booking desk should offer an alternative room (if available) or a full refund of any advance payment made.

2. **Request an alternative accommodation option.** If the Bhakta Niwas is genuinely at capacity, ask the booking desk for recommendations for nearby private hotels. The staff are familiar with the local accommodation options and can provide reliable recommendations.

3. **Document the cancellation.** Keep a record of all communication related to the cancellation, including WhatsApp messages and call notes. If you had to make alternative arrangements at a higher cost, you may be able to negotiate a partial refund or compensation from the Sansthan.

## Preventing booking issues

Most WhatsApp booking problems can be avoided by following a few practices from the start:

- **Send a complete message on your first attempt.** Include all the information the booking desk needs.
- **Follow up once if you do not receive a response within 4 hours.** One polite follow-up is sufficient — multiple messages do not speed up the process.
- **Confirm all details in writing.** Always save the complete confirmation from the Sansthan.
- **Make advance payments promptly.** Delays in payment are the most common cause of booking cancellations.
- **Keep a backup contact channel.** Save the [emergency contact numbers](/blog/emergency-contact-during-pilgrimage) for situations when WhatsApp is not working.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How long should I wait for a response before following up?**

A: During regular days, wait 4 hours before sending a follow-up. During festival seasons, the booking desk may take up to 8 hours to respond due to high message volume. Do not send multiple follow-up messages within a short period — one polite follow-up is sufficient.

**Q: The booking desk asked me to send my booking details again. Is this normal?**

A: Yes. The booking desk may ask you to resend your details if they did not receive them clearly, if the message was forwarded between staff members, or if the original message was part of a long conversation thread that was difficult to follow. Resend your details in a clean, formatted message.

**Q: Can I change my booking after confirmation?**

A: Yes. To modify a confirmed booking, send a message to the same WhatsApp number with your original booking reference and the changes you need (new dates, different room type, additional guests). The booking desk will check availability and confirm the changes. Modifications are easier to process during regular seasons than during festival periods.

**Q: What if the booking desk confirms a room type that is not available at my chosen location?**

A: If the booking desk confirms a room type that you know is not offered at your chosen location (for example, an AC room at Trimbakeshwar where only Non-AC rooms are available), reply immediately to clarify the discrepancy. This is usually a simple misunderstanding that can be resolved in one message.

**Q: My WhatsApp messages are not being delivered to the booking number. What should I do?**

A: First, verify that you are messaging the correct number (+91 80531 90691). If the number is correct but messages are still not being delivered, try calling the number directly or use the [web contact form](/contact) as an alternative channel. WhatsApp delivery issues can sometimes be resolved by clearing the chat history and starting a fresh conversation.

**Q: Is there a way to check my booking status without messaging the desk?**

A: The Sansthan does not have a self-service booking status portal at this time. To check your booking status, message or call the booking desk with your booking reference or the details of your original request. The desk will confirm your booking status, room allocation, and payment status in a single response.
