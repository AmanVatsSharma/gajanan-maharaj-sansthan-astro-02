# Group Booking Essentials | Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan

A group of thirty devotees arriving at Shegaon railway station in the early morning, each carrying a bag and a shared intention, is a different logistical challenge from a solo devotee with a backpack. The group needs a coordinated accommodation plan, a designated point of contact, a shared transport arrangement from the station, and a system for managing the group's movement through the darshan queue, the Mahaprasad Hall, and the daily schedule. The trust at Shegaon has developed procedures for handling groups of this size, and the group booking system at all four Sansthan locations is designed to make the experience smooth for both the group and the trust.

This guide covers the group booking process at all four Sansthan locations — the eligibility criteria, the booking procedure, the room allocation, the group rates, the dedicated coordinator support, and the logistical planning that makes a group pilgrimage successful. It is designed for community organisations, family groups, temple committees, and any group of ten or more devotees who want to visit the Sansthan locations together.

## What Counts as a Group Booking

The trust defines a group booking as any booking of ten or more rooms or dormitory beds at a single Sansthan location for the same dates. The ten-person threshold applies to the total number of accommodation units, not the number of people — a family of four in a private room counts as one unit, not four, and a group of ten people in dormitory beds counts as ten units.

For groups of fewer than ten people, the standard individual booking system is the appropriate channel. You can still request that the trust allocate your rooms in a contiguous block, and the trust will try to accommodate this request, but the dedicated group coordinator and the group rate are available only for bookings of ten or more units.

For groups of ten or more, the group booking process involves a dedicated trust coordinator who manages the entire booking, coordinates the accommodation allocation, arranges the transport from the station if requested, and serves as the single point of contact for the group during the stay. The coordinator is an experienced trust staff member who has handled group bookings before, and they can answer questions, resolve issues, and help the group navigate the temple experience.

## Booking a Group at Shegaon

The group booking process at Shegaon begins with a request to the trust accommodation office. The request should include the group size, the room requirements (dormitory, private, family), the check-in and check-out dates, the number of accompanying vehicles, and any special requirements — ground-floor rooms for elderly devotees, dietary provisions for medical conditions, wheelchair-accessible rooms, or specific timing requirements for darshan.

The trust accommodation office reviews the request and provides a confirmation with the room allocation, the total cost, the group coordinator's name and contact number, and the check-in procedure. The group rate at Shegaon is typically five to ten percent lower than the standard individual rate, and the discount increases for larger groups. A group of thirty people in dormitory rooms at Shegaon during a non-festival period pays approximately the same total amount as twenty-five individual bookings, which means the group rate provides a genuine saving.

The trust also provides a group check-in lane at Bhakta Niwas during peak periods. Instead of joining the individual check-in queue, the group proceeds directly to a designated counter where the coordinator processes the check-in for all group members simultaneously. This reduces the check-in time from approximately thirty minutes per person to approximately fifteen minutes for the entire group.

The [Bhakta Niwas Complete Booking Guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide) covers the group booking procedure in detail, including the request format, the confirmation timeline, the group rate structure, and the modification and cancellation policies for group bookings.

## Group Booking at the Other Locations

The group booking process at Omkareshwar, Pandharpur, and Trimbakeshwar follows the same general procedure as Shegaon, but with location-specific variations.

At Omkareshwar, the trust dharamshala on the island has limited capacity, and the group booking needs to be made well in advance — at least four to six weeks before the planned visit, particularly during festival periods. The trust coordinator at Omkareshwar will help the group arrange transport from Khandwa station to the island, and the group can request that the rooms be allocated in a contiguous block within the dharamshala.

At Pandharpur, the group booking during the wari is the most complex of the four locations, because the trust camp has to accommodate tens of thousands of devotees simultaneously. The group booking request for the wari should be submitted three to four months in advance, and the trust will allocate a section of the camp for the group. The group coordinator at Pandharpur manages the section allocation, the meal coordination, and the darshan queue arrangement for the group. The [Pandharpur Wari Accommodation Tips](/blog/pandharpur-wari-accommodation-tips) guide covers the group booking procedure during the wari in detail.

At Trimbakeshwar, the trust dharamshala is small, and group bookings of more than twenty people may need to be split across multiple dates or supplemented with private hotel rooms. The trust coordinator at Trimbakeshwar will advise on the maximum group size that can be accommodated and suggest alternative arrangements if the group is too large for the dharamshala.

## The Group Coordinator's Role

The group coordinator is the single most important person in the group booking experience. They are the link between the group and the trust, and they are responsible for ensuring that the group's accommodation, transport, darshan, and daily needs are met throughout the stay.

The coordinator's specific responsibilities include: managing the group check-in and check-out, coordinating the transport from the station or bus stand, briefing the group on the darshan timings and the temple procedures, arranging the Mahaprasad timing for the group, responding to any issues or questions during the stay, and coordinating with the temple staff for any special requirements.

The coordinator is not a tour guide — they do not provide sightseeing tours or cultural commentary — but they are deeply knowledgeable about the temple, the local area, and the trust's services, and they can answer most practical questions that the group members have. For questions that are outside the coordinator's scope — local sightseeing, regional transport, medical facilities — the coordinator can direct the group to the appropriate local resources.

## Logistical Planning for Groups

The logistical planning for a group pilgrimage starts with the accommodation booking and extends to transport, meals, darshan timing, and daily schedule coordination.

**Transport from the station.** The trust can arrange a shared vehicle from the railway station or bus stand to the accommodation for the entire group. The cost is typically lower than individual auto-rickshaw fares, and the arrangement is more convenient for a group carrying multiple bags. The [Complete Transport Guide for Devotees](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) covers the group transport options at all four locations.

**Meal coordination.** The trust Mahaprasad Hall can accommodate groups, but the timing needs to be coordinated to avoid the peak periods when the Hall is overcrowded. The group coordinator can arrange a specific meal time for the group, typically before or after the main serving periods. If the group has specific dietary requirements — vegetarian-only, no onion-garlic, specific allergy restrictions — the coordinator can communicate these requirements to the Mahaprasad Hall kitchen in advance.

**Darshan queue coordination.** The darshan queue at Shegaon and the other locations can be long during peak periods, and the group coordinator can arrange for the group to enter the queue together at a specific time. This is particularly useful during festival periods when the queue management is more structured. The [Shegaon Darshan Timing Guide](/blog/shegaon-darshan-timing-guide) and the [Pandharpur Vitthal Darshan Timing Guide](/blog/pandharpur-vitthal-darshan-timing-guide) cover the darshan timing and the queue procedures for groups.

**Daily schedule.** The group coordinator will help the group plan the daily schedule — darshan timing, Mahaprasad timing, Anand Sagan visit, and any other activities. The coordinator knows the local timings and can advise on the best sequence of activities to avoid crowds and heat.

## Multi-Location Group Trips

For groups that are visiting multiple Sansthan locations, the coordination is more complex. The group needs a coherent itinerary that accounts for the transport between locations, the accommodation booking at each location, the darshan timing at each temple, and the overall time budget.

The [Darshan and Stay Priority Planning](/blog/darshan-and-stay-priority-planning) guide covers the sequencing and timing considerations for multi-location trips, and the [Complete Transport Guide for Devotees](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) covers the inter-city transport options between all four locations. For group travel, the recommended approach is to book a private vehicle for the inter-city legs rather than relying on public transport, because a private vehicle provides more flexibility for the group's schedule and can accommodate the group's luggage.

The [Return Journey Planning Tips](/blog/return-journey-planning-tips) guide covers the timing and transport planning for the return journey, including the strategies for managing delays and ensuring that the entire group returns safely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum group size for a group booking?**

The trust defines a group booking as ten or more accommodation units. This includes dormitory beds, private rooms, and family rooms. A group of ten people in dormitory beds qualifies, as does a group of five families in private rooms.

**How far in advance should I book a group accommodation?**

Three to four months in advance for festival periods, and one to two months in advance for non-festival periods. The group booking process takes longer than individual bookings, because the trust needs to allocate rooms, assign a coordinator, and confirm the group's requirements.

**Can the trust arrange transport for the group from the station?**

Yes. The trust can arrange a shared vehicle from the railway station or bus stand to the accommodation for the entire group. The cost is typically lower than individual auto-rickshaw fares. Confirm this arrangement when you submit the group booking request.

**Are there group discounts on accommodation?**

Yes. The trust provides a group discount of five to ten percent on the standard accommodation rates, and the discount increases for larger groups. The group rate is confirmed in the booking confirmation that the trust sends after the booking request is approved.

**What happens if some group members need to cancel their booking?**

The group booking modification and cancellation policies are outlined in the booking confirmation. In general, the trust allows individual cancellations within the group, and the group rate is adjusted accordingly. However, if the number of cancellations reduces the group size below the ten-unit threshold, the group discount may be withdrawn and the individual rates will apply to the remaining members.

**Can the group coordinator help with local sightseeing and transport to other locations?**

The coordinator can provide information and recommendations, but they do not arrange sightseeing tours or private transport to other locations. The [Complete Transport Guide for Devotees](/blog/complete-transport-guide-for-devotees) covers the local transport options and the inter-city transport between all four Sansthan locations, and the group can arrange private vehicles through local operators.
