The anniversary of Shri Gajanan Maharaj's arrival at Shegaon is one of the most significant observances in the annual programme of the Sansthan. It commemorates the day when the saint came to Shegaon and began his public life as a spiritual teacher and healer, and the Sansthan marks this anniversary with a multi-day programme that includes special abhishek ceremonies at the temple, devotional discourses that explore the saint's teachings, and community programmes that celebrate the saint's legacy. The anniversary utsav — the festival of commemoration — is observed with particular devotion at the Sansthan, and the combination of the spiritual programme and the community celebration creates an atmosphere of focused devotional energy that is deeply moving for devotees who have studied the saint's life and teachings. Devotees who attend the anniversary observance often describe it as one of the most spiritually enriching experiences of the year, and many make it a point to be present at Shegaon for this important date in the Sansthan's calendar. The anniversary also draws visitors from outside the immediate devotee community — scholars, researchers, and pilgrims from across India and overseas — who come to witness the observance and to pay their respects at the Samadhi of one of the most revered saints in the Hindu tradition.

## The Historical Significance

Shri Gajanan Maharaj arrived at Shegaon in the late nineteenth century, and the anniversary of his arrival is commemorated annually at the Sansthan. The saint's arrival at Shegaon marked the beginning of his public ministry — the period during which he received devotees, conducted healing, and taught the devotional philosophy that continues to guide the Sansthan today. The anniversary observance therefore is not merely a commemoration of a historical event — it is a celebration of the living tradition that the saint established and that the Sansthan continues to maintain with unwavering commitment across more than a century of continuous spiritual practice. The anniversary observance connects each generation of devotees to the original moment of the saint's arrival, creating a sense of continuity across time that is rare in modern devotional practice.

The historical records of the Sansthan document the saint's arrival at Shegaon and the early years of his ministry, when he conducted his teaching and healing work from the site that is now the main temple complex. The anniversary programme honours this history by including sessions that recount the saint's life story, the circumstances of his arrival at Shegaon, and the spiritual teachings that he imparted to his early devotees. These historical sessions are one of the distinctive features of the anniversary observance, and they provide devotees with a deeper understanding of the spiritual tradition that they are participating in.

## The Abhishek Ceremony

The anniversary observance at the Sansthan includes a series of special abhishek ceremonies that are conducted at the main temple and at the Samadhi. The abhishek at the main temple includes the ceremonial bathing of the deity with traditional offerings including milk, honey, water, and flowers. The ceremony is conducted by the temple priests with Vedic mantras, and the atmosphere during the ceremony is one of focused devotional attention. Many devotees sponsor the abhishek as a devotional offering, and the names of the sponsors are announced during the ceremony. The sponsorship system allows devotees who cannot be physically present to participate in the abhishek through their offering, and many devotees from across India and overseas sponsor the ceremony as part of their anniversary observance.

The abhishek at the Samadhi — the place where Shri Gajanan Maharaj attained mahasamadhi — is particularly well-attended during the anniversary observance. Devotees gather at the Samadhi for the early morning abhishek ceremony, and the atmosphere in the quiet garden surrounding the Samadhi is one of deep devotional reverence. The combination of the garden setting, the early morning hours, and the devotional music creates an experience that many devotees describe as one of the most spiritually significant moments of the anniversary programme. The Samadhi grounds are decorated with flowers for the anniversary abhishek, and the decorations are maintained throughout the programme period.

## The Devotional Discourses

The anniversary programme at the Sansthan includes devotional discourses that explore the teachings of Shri Gajanan Maharaj and their relevance to contemporary devotional practice. The speakers are typically prominent spiritual teachers from across Maharashtra who have studied the saint's life and teachings, and the discourses draw on traditional texts including the saint's documented teachings, the biographical accounts of his ministry, and the devotional literature that has been produced by his devotees over the past century. The discourses are delivered in Marathi with Hindi and English summaries, making them accessible to devotees from across India and overseas.

The afternoon discourses typically include a Q&A session that allows devotees to engage directly with the speakers and to ask questions about the saint's teachings. The Q&A sessions are one of the most popular features of the anniversary programme, and they provide devotees with an opportunity to explore the practical application of the saint's teachings in their own devotional lives. Many devotees describe the discourses as one of the most intellectually and spiritually enriching elements of the anniversary programme, and the combination of the intellectual exploration and the devotional atmosphere creates a learning environment that is both challenging and deeply satisfying.

## The Community Programmes

The community programmes at the Anand Saga grounds during the anniversary observance include devotional singing, community meals, and cultural performances. The programmes are designed to be accessible to devotees of all ages, and they create an atmosphere of communal celebration that complements the more focused devotional programme at the temple. The community programmes are typically conducted in the afternoon and early evening, and they provide a social environment in which devotees can meet and share their devotional experiences with other members of the community. Many devotees who attend the anniversary observance alone find that the community programmes provide an opportunity to connect with other devotees and to build relationships that continue beyond the festival.

## Weather and Preparation

The anniversary falls in February, which is the cool late-winter period at Shegaon. Daytime temperatures range between 22 and 30 degrees Celsius, and the night-time temperatures can drop to between 10 and 15 degrees Celsius.
