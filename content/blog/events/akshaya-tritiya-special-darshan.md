Akshaya Tritiya arrives in the spring, when the Vidarbha plains are in their most generous season. The harvest is complete, the fields stand golden with ripened grain, and the air carries warmth that has returned after the cool clarity of winter. For devotees of Shri Gajanan Maharaj, this is a day that carries a particular devotional weight — it is counted among the most auspicious dates in the Hindu calendar, a day when every act of worship, every offering, and every prayer is believed to yield multiplied spiritual merit. The Sansthan at Shegaon marks Akshaya Tritiya with a special programme that draws devotees from across Maharashtra, and for those who plan well, it provides a devotional experience that is both celebratory and deeply personal.

The word "Akshaya" means "inexhaustible" — never diminishing, never depleted. The festival's core theme is abundance that does not run out: spiritual abundance, the abundance of well-being, and the abundance of divine grace that flows through sincere devotional practice. For devotees who want to mark a new beginning — a commitment to regular temple visits, a significant offering, or a prayer for a specific intention — Akshaya Tritiya at Shegaon offers a devotional moment that is universally auspicious regardless of personal circumstance or astrological timing.

## The Akshaya Tritiya programme at the Sansthan

The Akshaya Tritiya programme begins before most devotees have finished their morning routine. The Mangala Aarti at the temple opens at 4:30 AM, and the special morning puja begins at approximately 7:00 AM. The priests perform an extended abhishek on Maharaj's Samadhi, using sacred substances that carry the Akshaya Tritiya theme of inexhaustible blessing: milk for purity, honey for sweetness, ghee for nourishment, and turmeric for auspiciousness. The abhishek ceremony is accompanied by devotional singing and the recitation of mantras specific to the Akshaya Tritiya programme, and the atmosphere in the temple hall during the puja is one of concentrated, collective devotion.

The Akshaya Tritiya puja at the Sansthan is open to all devotees without restriction. The main temple hall has a seating area for devotees who want to observe the ceremony closely, and the priests accept offerings from those who wish to participate in the abhishek. ## The spiritual significance of Akshaya Tritiya

Akshaya Tritiya occupies a distinctive position in the Hindu calendar because it is considered universally auspicious — its spiritual merit does not depend on an individual's astrological chart or personal circumstances. This universal accessibility makes it particularly meaningful for devotees who want to start a new devotional practice, make a significant offering, or seek blessings for prosperity and well-being for their family. The day does not require elaborate preparation or specific knowledge — the intention of the devotee is the primary factor, and the sincerity of that intention is what activates the day's spiritual energy.

For devotees of Shri Gajanan Maharaj, the Akshaya Tritiya visit is often combined with a specific devotional intention. Many devotees visit Shegaon on this day to seek blessings for material and spiritual prosperity, for the well-being of family members who could not be present, or for a new beginning in their spiritual practice. The practice of making a donation or offering on Akshaya Tritiya is considered especially meritorious, because the donation is believed to carry multiplied spiritual value on a day when every action is believed to yield greater fruit.

## Planning your Akshaya Tritiya visit

The Akshaya Tritiya programme at the Sansthan draws a moderate to substantial crowd — typically 15,000 to 25,000 devotees over the course of the day. The morning puja draws 5,000 to 8,000 devotees, and the evening programme draws the larger share. The crowd is smaller than during Pragat Din or Punyatithi, but the special puja programme means that Bhakta Niwas rooms can book out two to three weeks before the festival. Booking at least two to three weeks in advance is the minimum recommended window, and earlier booking provides flexibility in room category selection. ## Darshan strategy during the festival

The morning darshan hours during Akshaya Tritiya — from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM — offer the most peaceful and devotional experience. The queue during morning hours runs 20 to 30 minutes long, and arriving by 6:30 AM allows you to participate in the Mangala Aarti, complete your darshan before the puja begins, and find a comfortable position for observing the abhishek. The evening programme, which includes aarti and prasad distribution, draws a larger crowd, and the darshan queue during the evening extends to 45 to 60 minutes during peak hours. ## Transport and travel planning

The transport arrangement for an Akshaya Tritiya visit follows the same patterns as any Shegaon visit. State transport buses and trains from Akola connect to Mumbai, Nagpur, and Amravati on a regular schedule. The Akola railway station, approximately 55 kilometres from Shegaon, is the most convenient arrival point for devotees travelling by train. Auto-rickshaws from Akola station to Shegaon cost 200 to 300 rupees and take 45 to 60 minutes. For devotees travelling by private vehicle, the spring weather makes the road journey comfortable, and the countryside around Shegaon is at its most beautiful during the Akshaya Tritiya season. The [shegaon-travel-guide](/blog/shegaon-travel-guide) covers the complete transport options and routing advice.

## Frequently asked questions

**What time does the Akshaya Tritiya puja begin at the Sansthan?**
The special morning puja begins at approximately 7:00 AM. The temple opens at 4:30 AM for Mangala Aarti and early darshan, and arriving by 6:30 AM gives you time to complete darshan and find a position for the puja.

**How many devotees attend the Akshaya Tritiya programme?**
Typically 15,000 to 25,000 devotees over the course of the day. The morning puja draws 5,000 to 8,000 devotees, and the evening programme draws 10,000 to 15,000. Darshan queues during the morning run 20 to 30 minutes.

**Is Akshaya Tritiya particularly auspicious at Shegaon compared to other temples?**
Akshaya Tritiya is considered auspicious at all temples, but the Sansthan's programme adds a specific devotional context that many devotees find meaningful. The combination of the special puja, the collective devotional energy, and the connection to Maharaj's teaching makes the Shegaon experience distinctive.

**When should I book accommodation for Akshaya Tritiya?**
Two to three weeks before the festival. Booking earlier provides more flexibility in room category selection. Call the booking office before 9:00 AM on weekdays when the lines are shortest. Use our [booking page](/booking) for reservations.

**Are there specific offerings I can make during the Akshaya Tritiya puja?**
Yes, the Sansthan accepts offerings for the special puja, including donations for the abhishek and prasad distribution. Contact the temple office before you arrive through our [contact page](/contact) to discuss specific offerings.
