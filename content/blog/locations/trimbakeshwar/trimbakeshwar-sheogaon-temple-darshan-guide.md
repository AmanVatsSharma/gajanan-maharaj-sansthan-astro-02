## About Trimbakeshwar and what makes it distinctive

The Kushavarta kund, a stepped stone tank at the centre of the town, is one of the most sacred bathing sites in Maharashtra and is the focus of a long queue of pilgrims throughout the day. Priests at the kund offer karmakanda rituals including tripindi shraddha, rudra, and narayan-nagbali poojas, and the pool is also the symbolic source of the Godavari, which is ritually represented by a small set of carved channels that leave the kund. The careful maintenance of the kund and its immediate precincts is overseen by the temple trust in consultation with the local Brahmin priest guild.
