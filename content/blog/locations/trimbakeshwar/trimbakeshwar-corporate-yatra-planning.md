## About Trimbakeshwar and what makes it distinctive

The Kushavarta kund, a stepped stone tank at the centre of the town, is one of the most sacred bathing sites in Maharashtra and is the focus of a long queue of pilgrims throughout the day. Priests at the kund offer karmakanda rituals including tripindi shraddha, rudra, and narayan-nagbali poojas, and the pool is also the symbolic source of the Godavari, which is ritually represented by a small set of carved channels that leave the kund. The careful maintenance of the kund and its immediate precincts is overseen by the temple trust in consultation with the local Brahmin priest guild.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 1

1. Locate the nearest ATM and bank branch before you run out of cash.
2. ATMs can be crowded on weekends, so withdraw cash on weekday mornings.
3. Inform your bank about the travel dates to avoid card block issues.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 2

1. Carry a refillable water bottle and refill it at the trust's drinking water points.
2. Avoid single-use plastic bottles as the temple town discourages plastic use.
3. Keep a small hand sanitiser and pack of tissues in your day bag.
4. Carry a small packet of wet wipes for cleaning hands before eating meals.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 3

1. Confirm the dress code for any nearby attraction before planning a visit.
2. Some religious sites require head covering and removal of leather items.
3. Carry an extra scarf in your bag to meet unexpected dress code requirements.
4. Respect the silence rules at meditation halls and reading rooms.
