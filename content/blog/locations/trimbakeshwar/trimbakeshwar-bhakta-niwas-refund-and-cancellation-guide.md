## Trimbakeshwar Bhakta Niwas refund and cancellation guide

The Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas cancellation policy is festival-specific. Mahashivratri has the strictest 21-day cut-off; the monthly Shivaratri has the most relaxed 7-day cut-off. This guide explains the policy in full, the partial-cancellation rule, the UPI refund timing, and how to escalate if you disagree with the desk.

### The cancellation window at a glance

| Booking context | Cancellation window (before check-in) | Refund of advance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mahashivratri | 21 days | 50% |
| Sawan Mondays (any of the four) | 14 days | 75% |
| Navratri Day 1 or Day 9 | 14 days | 75% |
| Kartik Purnima | 14 days | 75% |
| Monthly Shivaratri | 7 days | 75% |
| Normal weekday (non-festival) | 7 days | 90% |
| Walk-in booking (no advance paid) | Anytime before check-in | Not applicable |

The "before check-in" date is the most important number. A 21-day window means the cancellation request must reach the desk 21 full days before your arrival date.

### How to cancel

Three steps:

**Step 1 — Send a WhatsApp message to the booking desk.**
Use the same desk that handled the original booking. Include:
- Booking ID
- Name on the booking
- Original check-in and check-out dates
- Requested cancellation date
- Refund method (UPI ID or bank account)

**Step 2 — Receive the cancellation acknowledgement.**
The desk replies with the cancellation number, the eligible refund amount, and the expected refund date. Allow 24 hours for the acknowledgement.

**Step 3 — Receive the refund.**
The refund is processed within 7–10 working days for UPI and 10–14 working days for bank transfer. The desk sends a confirmation message with the UPI reference number or the bank transfer reference.

### Partial cancellations

The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas policy on partial cancellations is straightforward:

- **Reducing the number of nights:** Allowed up to the cancellation window. Refund is 50–90% of the per-night advance for the cancelled nights, depending on the festival.
- **Reducing the number of guests:** Allowed up to the cancellation window. Refund is 50–90% of the per-guest advance for the cancelled guests, depending on the festival.
- **Changing the room type:** Allowed up to 7 days before check-in, subject to availability. No refund is processed if the new room type is the same price or cheaper — only the price difference is adjusted.

Partial cancellations inside the cancellation window (i.e., too close to check-in) are not processed. The full advance is forfeited.

### Refund timing by payment method

| Payment method | Refund timing | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPI | 7–10 working days | Fastest method, preferred for refunds |
| Bank transfer (NEFT) | 10–14 working days | Used for large refunds above ₹50,000 |
| Bank transfer (RTGS) | 3–5 working days | Used for refunds above ₹2 lakh |
| Card (debit / credit) | 14–21 working days | Slowest method; refund appears on the card statement |

The desk prefers UPI refunds. If you paid by card and your booking was below ₹50,000, ask for a UPI refund to a different account (your UPI ID) — this is faster.

### What is not refundable

Three things are non-refundable:

1. **Donations to the Sansthan.** Any voluntary donation made during booking is non-refundable.
2. **Mahaprasad charges.** If you prepaid for mahaprasad and didn't consume it, the desk does not refund the mahaprasad component.
3. **Pickup and drop charges.** Cab / auto charges paid in advance are non-refundable once the cab has been assigned.

For the broader booking flow see [Bhakta Niwas Booking Process](/blog/trimbakeshwar-bhakta-niwas-booking-process). For the festival-specific open dates see [Festival Advance Booking Window](/blog/trimbakeshwar-festival-advance-booking-window).

### What if I cancel because of a medical emergency?

Medical emergencies are treated case-by-case. Send the WhatsApp cancellation request with:

- A short note explaining the medical situation
- A scanned copy of the hospital admission or doctor letter
- The booking ID

The desk escalates to the Sansthan office, which usually approves a higher refund percentage (75–100% depending on the documentation). Allow 5–7 working days for the escalation.

### What if the festival is cancelled or rescheduled?

If the temple or the Sansthan cancels a festival (rare but possible — it happened with one of the Sawan Mondays in 2023 due to local administrative reasons), the desk offers:

1. **Full refund** of the advance (100%) — most common outcome
2. **Date change** with the same advance applied to a new date — if the pilgrim prefers

The desk proactively contacts all booked pilgrims within 24 hours of the cancellation announcement.

### What if I don't get a refund within the expected timeline?

Three steps:

1. **Send a follow-up message to the desk** with the cancellation number and the original refund commitment date.
2. **Wait 5 additional working days** for the refund to land.
3. **Send an escalation email** to the Sansthan office (see [Contact and Support Guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-contact-and-support-guide)). The office escalates within 3 working days.

For UPI refunds, if the UPI reference is shown as "failed" on your UPI app, the desk re-processes the refund within 48 hours. Always share the screenshot of the failed reference with the desk.

### No-show policy

If you do not arrive on the check-in date and do not send a cancellation request:

- **Same-day no-show (within 24 hours of expected check-in):** Full advance is forfeited.
- **Next-day no-show:** Full advance is forfeited.
- **No contact for 48 hours after the check-in date:** The desk treats the booking as a no-show, cancels it, and the advance is forfeited.

If you are running late on the check-in day, send a WhatsApp message to the desk — the reception holds the room until 22:00 on the check-in day. After 22:00, the desk releases the room to the standby list.

### Disputing a charge

If you believe the desk has applied the wrong cancellation policy or wrong refund percentage:

1. **Send a written complaint** to the Sansthan office via email. Include the booking ID, the cancellation message trail, and the amount in question.
2. **Wait 7 working days** for the office to respond. The office reviews the booking and policy application.
3. **Escalate to the Sansthan Grievance Cell** if the response is unsatisfactory. The grievance cell responds within 14 working days.

The Sansthan Grievance Cell is the final escalation point within the Sansthan. Beyond that, the consumer forum route is available but rarely needed.

### Common mistakes to avoid

- **Waiting until the last day to cancel.** The cancellation window is "before check-in" — the request must reach the desk within the window.
- **Cancelling via phone call without WhatsApp follow-up.** The desk needs a written record. Always follow up a phone cancellation with a WhatsApp message.
- **Assuming the cancellation window is the same for every festival.** Mahashivratri has a 21-day cut-off; the monthly Shivaratri has 7 days. See [Festival Advance Booking Window](/blog/trimbakeshwar-festival-advance-booking-window).
- **Asking for a refund to a different person's account.** The desk refunds to the original payer only. If you need a different account, the original payer's authorisation is required.
- **Forgetting to share the UPI reference or the bank transfer reference.** The desk uses these to track the refund. Always share them.

### Final checklist for a cancellation

- [ ] Cancellation request sent to the desk via WhatsApp
- [ ] Booking ID, name, and dates in the request
- [ ] Cancellation number received
- [ ] Refund amount matches the festival policy
- [ ] Refund method (UPI / bank) confirmed
- [ ] Refund received within 7–14 working days
- [ ] Refund reference number saved for records
