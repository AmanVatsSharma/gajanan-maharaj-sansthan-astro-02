## About Trimbakeshwar and what makes it distinctive

Devotees should observe a clear set of cultural norms in Trimbakeshwar, beginning with a bath at the Kushavarta kund before darshan and continuing through the strict vegetarian food discipline of the temple zone. Mobile phones are restricted inside the core sanctum, footwear and leather items are deposited at the counters near the entrance, and the queue moves through a narrow passage that requires patience. Local etiquette also requires that pilgrims treat the Brahmin priests who perform the shraddha rituals with formal respect, and that the Brahmagiri trek be undertaken with a local guide whenever the path is wet or the visibility is poor.
