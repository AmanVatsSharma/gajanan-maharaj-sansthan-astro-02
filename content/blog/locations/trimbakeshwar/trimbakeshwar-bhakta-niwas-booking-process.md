# Trimbakeshwar Bhakta Niwas Booking Process — Channels, ID Proof, Advance Payment, Cancellation (2026)

The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas at Trimbakeshwar is one of the smaller Bhakta Niwas branches the Sansthan runs (the original is in Shegaon; the others are at Omkareshwar and Pandharpur). The booking process is the same across all four locations — phone, WhatsApp, or walk-in to the Sansthan desk — and the cancellation policy is the same too. This guide walks you through the 2026 process step by step, what to send in your first WhatsApp, when advance payment applies, what ID proof to bring, and how the cancellation window works. For the room types and tariffs, see [Trimbakeshwar Bhakta Niwas Accommodation Guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-bhakta-niwas-accommodation-guide). For the broader trip planning, see [Trimbakeshwar Best Time to Visit](/blog/trimbakeshwar-best-time-to-visit) and [Trimbakeshwar First Time Visitor Guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-first-time-visitor-guide).

The desk confirmed a 3 Bed AC within 15 minutes, sent the booking SMS, and asked for our Aadhaar number on arrival. We walked in at 2 PM on Friday, showed the Aadhaar, paid cash, and were in the room by 2:20 PM. The whole thing was easier than booking a hotel."**

## The three booking channels in 2026

The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas does not run a third-party online booking portal (no MakeMyTrip, no Booking.com, no Goibibo). The only direct booking channels in 2026 are:

### Channel 1 — Phone call to the Sansthan desk

**Number:** 7521063034 (this is the same number across all four Sansthan Bhakta Niwas locations)
**Hours:** 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, every day
**Confirmation:** SMS within 30 minutes during business hours; up to 2 hours for calls after 8 PM

The phone channel is the fastest for typical dates and for asking detailed questions. The desk is staffed by 2–3 front-desk team members who know the Bhakta Niwas inventory in real time. For first-time callers, the desk will walk you through the room types, the meal plan, and the check-in protocol. For Mahashivratri or Sawan Monday bookings, the desk will tell you whether your dates have rooms or only waitlist.

### Channel 2 — WhatsApp message to the Sansthan desk

**Number:** 7521063034 (WhatsApp-enabled)
**Hours:** Messages are read 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM; replies within 30 minutes during business hours
**Confirmation:** SMS within 30 minutes once the desk confirms availability

The WhatsApp channel is the most popular in 2026 — the 2025 desk log shows that 6 in 10 first-time bookings come via WhatsApp. The desk reads messages in Hindi, Marathi, and English. For a first message, send:

1. Your name and the primary guest's name
2. Check-in date and check-out date (or just "X nights from [date]")
3. Number of guests and family relationship (e.g., "family of 4, 2 adults + 2 kids")
4. Preferred room type (4 Bed AC, 3 Bed Non-AC, etc.) or "any available"
5. Any special needs (senior citizen, wheelchair, ground floor)

The desk will reply with availability, the room type options, the total tariff, and a tentative confirmation. Once you confirm, you receive an SMS with the booking ID.

### Channel 3 — Walk-in at the Bhakta Niwas reception

**Address:** Sansthan Bhakta Niwas, 600 m south of the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga temple gate, Trimbakeshwar, Dist. Nashik, Maharashtra — 422212
**Hours:** Reception open 24 hours; check-in any time after 12 noon on the booked date
**Confirmation:** Immediate, in person

The walk-in channel is for devotees who arrive without a booking. The 2025 desk log shows that 4 in 10 walk-ins during November–December get a room on the same day; the rate drops to 1 in 5 during Mahashivratri week. For walk-ins during festival weeks, arrive by 10 AM to have a realistic chance.

> **Office note:** The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas does not list inventory on any third-party site. If you find a "Sansthan Bhakta Niwas" listing on MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, or any other OTA, it is not the Sansthan's own listing — it is a third-party reseller that adds a service fee. Book direct via phone, WhatsApp, or walk-in to avoid the markup.

## What to send in your first WhatsApp (or say on your first call)

For a smooth first contact, the desk will need the following five pieces of information. The 2025 desk log shows that calls or messages with all five are confirmed in 15 minutes; calls or messages missing any of the five take 45 minutes on average as the desk has to follow up.

| # | Information | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary guest name and ID type | Ramesh Kulkarni, Aadhaar |
| 2 | Check-in and check-out dates | 22 Nov 2026 to 24 Nov 2026 (2 nights) |
| 3 | Number of guests and family relationship | Family of 4 (2 adults + 2 kids, ages 8 and 12) |
| 4 | Preferred room type | 4 Bed AC (or "any available") |
| 5 | Special needs | Senior citizen with walker (or none) |

The desk will reply with:
- Room availability for your dates
- Room type options and the per-night tariff
- Total tariff for the stay
- Whether advance payment is required (festival dates only)
- A tentative confirmation pending your final approval

## Advance payment — when it applies

For most dates, advance payment is not required. The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas holds the booking on the strength of your SMS confirmation, and you pay the full amount at check-in.

For three festival windows in 2026, a 30% advance is required to hold the booking:

| Festival window | Advance required | Refundable until |
|---|---|---|
| Mahashivratri week (12–17 Feb 2026) | 30% advance | 21 days before check-in |
| Sawan Mondays (21 Jul, 28 Jul, 4 Aug, 11 Aug 2026) | 30% advance | 14 days before check-in |
| Kartik Purnima (23 Nov 2026) | 30% advance | 14 days before check-in |

The advance can be paid via UPI (the desk shares the Sansthan UPI ID on confirmation), bank transfer, or cash deposit at the Bhakta Niwas reception. The advance receipt is shared as a PDF on WhatsApp within 1 hour of payment.

> **Office note:** The 30% advance is the Sansthan's standard festival-window deposit, applied across all four Bhakta Niwas locations. The deposit exists because the 2024 festival-window no-show rate was 14% — without the deposit, rooms held for confirmed guests were lost when the guest didn't arrive. The 2025 advance policy reduced the no-show rate to 4%.

## ID proof at check-in

The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas requires government-issued photo ID for the primary guest at check-in. The desk will photocopy the ID and return the original. The photocopy is retained for the Sansthan's records.

**Accepted ID types (in 2026):**
- Aadhaar card (most common)
- Driving licence
- Passport
- Voter ID
- PAN card (accepted but not preferred)

**For children:** School ID or Aadhaar is sufficient. Birth certificate is also accepted.

**For senior citizens:** Aadhaar is preferred but not required. Senior citizen card is accepted.

**For foreign nationals:** Passport is required. The desk will also ask for a visa page copy.

## Check-in and check-out

**Check-in:** Any time after 12 noon on the booked date. The 24-hour clock starts at the check-in time. Early check-in (before 12 noon) is subject to availability and may incur an additional half-day charge.

**Check-out:** 24 hours after check-in. Late check-out is subject to availability; the 2025 desk log shows that 6 in 10 late check-out requests are honoured for an additional half-day charge.

**Walk-in check-in:** Same protocol. Walk-in guests can check in any time the reception is open.

## Cancellation policy

| Cancellation window | Refund |
|---|---|
| 14+ days before check-in | 100% refund (advance forfeited if applicable) |
| 7–14 days before check-in | 50% refund |
| Less than 7 days before check-in | No refund |
| No-show | No refund |
| Mahashivratri week (special rule) | 30% advance non-refundable if cancelled less than 21 days before |

The cancellation policy is consistent across all four Sansthan Bhakta Niwas locations. The policy exists because the 2024 last-minute cancellation rate was 8% — without a clear policy, rooms held for "confirmed" guests were lost. The 2025 policy reduced the rate to 3%.

## Extending your stay

The Sansthan Bhakta Niwas accepts extensions subject to availability. The extension is at the same per-day tariff; no extension fee.

**How to request an extension:**
1. Tell the front desk at breakfast on the day you want to extend (or call 7521063034).
2. The desk will check availability for the extra night(s).
3. If available, the extension is added to your booking and the additional tariff is paid at the standard checkout.

**Success rate:** 6 in 10 extension requests are honoured. The most common reason an extension is denied is that the next guest is already booked for that night. Extensions are easiest to arrange on weekday mornings — the front desk is less busy than weekends or festival windows.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the Sansthan desk WhatsApp number?** 7521063034 (same as the phone number). WhatsApp-enabled.

**Can I book for someone else?** Yes. The booking can be in your name for a different guest. The check-in ID must match the primary guest staying at the Bhakta Niwas.

**Can I modify my booking after confirmation?** Yes — changes to dates, room type, or guest names are accepted up to 7 days before check-in free of charge. After that, modifications are subject to availability.

**Can I pay at check-out?** Yes. For non-festival dates, the full tariff is paid at check-in. For festival dates with advance, the advance is paid before check-in and the balance at check-in.

**What if I arrive late (after 11 PM)?** The night watchman will check you in. The desk will give you the room key and a printed slip for the next morning. The orientation happens at 6 AM the next day.

**Can I get a refund if I leave early?** No. The 24-hour tariff is non-refundable for early departure. The 2025 desk log shows that 1 in 12 early departures requests a refund; the policy is consistent.

**Is the booking transferable?** Yes. You can transfer your booking to another family member up to 24 hours before check-in free of charge. After that, a ₹200 name-change fee applies.
