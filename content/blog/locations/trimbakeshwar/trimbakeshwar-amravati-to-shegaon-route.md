## Darshan and visit planning checklist

1. Carry light snacks and water for the journey to Buldhana district.
2. Ensure your vehicle has enough fuel as some routes have fewer stations.
3. Use GPS but also verify directions with locals for the final temple approach.
4. Keep your booking confirmation handy to show at the entry gates.

## Location-specific tips for devotees

For those driving from Mumbai, the Samruddhi Mahamarg has significantly reduced travel time to the Vidarbha region.

Families visiting Trimbakeshwar often benefit from arriving early to avoid peak queues, especially on weekends and festival days. The temple complex and accommodation areas are well-maintained, with clear signage and helpful staff. Keep a copy of your booking confirmation handy, and carry light snacks and water for children.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there an online booking portal for Sansthan accommodation?** Currently, the Sansthan primarily manages bookings through official phone and WhatsApp channels to ensure fair distribution. Be wary of unofficial websites claiming to offer online bookings.

**Are meals provided at the temple accommodation?** While rooms don't usually have room service, the Sansthan operates a massive Mahaprasad Hall and various canteens where healthy, subsidized, or free meals are served.
