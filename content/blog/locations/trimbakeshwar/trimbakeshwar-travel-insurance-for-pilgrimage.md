## About Trimbakeshwar and what makes it distinctive

The town owes its name and its central place in Hindu geography to its status as the home of one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Shiva, with the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga temple rising at the heart of the settlement. The deity is enshrined in a standing form, an unusual representation among the Jyotirlingas, and the shrine is associated with a powerful trinetra symbolism that is reflected in the temple name. Trimbakeshwar also sits at the source of the Godavari, which gives it a weight in Hindu sacred geography that extends well beyond its modest size.
