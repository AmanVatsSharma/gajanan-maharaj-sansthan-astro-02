## Trimbakeshwar festival advance booking window guide

Trimbakeshwar's festival calendar is uneven. The temple is quiet most of the year and then very crowded for a handful of dates. Five festival windows really matter for the booking calendar:

1. Mahashivratri (Feb / Mar — one night)
2. The four Sawan Mondays (Jul / Aug)
3. Navratri (Sep / Oct — nine nights, but only Day 1 and Day 9 are big here)
4. Kartik Purnima (Nov — one day)
5. Monthly Shivaratri (every 13th lunar day — moderate crowd)

For each, the booking window, payment terms, and confirmation timing are different. This guide is the festival-by-festival reference for the Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas booking system.

### Mahashivratri

The single largest event of the year. Bhakta Niwas rooms within 30 km of Trimbakeshwar fill 60–90 days before. The Sansthan's booking pattern:

- **Open date:** Mid-December for the February festival
- **Advance payment:** 100% of stay — partial holds are not the norm for this week
- **Cancellation window:** 21 days before festival — see [Bhakta Niwas Refund and Cancellation Guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-bhakta-niwas-refund-and-cancellation-guide)
- **Confirmation:** WhatsApp message within 48 hours of advance payment landing
- **Lock-in:** Once confirmed, the room is held for the entire stay; no partial cancellations

For the full festival plan see [Mahashivratri Booking Guide](/blog/trimbakeshwar-mahashivratri-booking-guide).

### Sawan Mondays (Shravan Somvar)

Four Mondays, with the third being the heaviest because of Kanwar Yatra overlap. Bhakta Niwas rooms fill progressively earlier as the calendar advances:

- **Sawan Somvar 1** — open 50 days before, advance payment 50%
- **Sawan Somvar 2** — open 55 days before, advance payment 50%
- **Sawan Somvar 3** — open 60 days before (Kanwar peak), advance payment 50%
- **Sawan Somvar 4** — open 55 days before, advance payment 50%

For Sawan Mondays, the Sansthan desk also opens a Sawan-month standby list. Pilgrims who can travel on short notice (5–10 days) sometimes pick up a cancellation slot — ask the desk to add you to the standby list. See [Sawan Monday Booking Strategy](/blog/trimbakeshwar-sawan-monday-booking-strategy) for the full Sawan playbook.

### Navratri

Navratri is a 9-night festival, but Trimbakeshwar's draw is concentrated on Day 1 (Shailaputri) and Day 9 (Siddhidatri). The other days see normal weekday crowds. Bhakta Niwas booking pattern:

- **Day 1** — open 30 days before, advance payment 50%
- **Day 9** — open 30 days before, advance payment 50%
- **Days 2–8** — treated as normal weekdays; advance payment 30% or walk-in subject to availability

If you want to do the full 9-night Trimbakeshwar stay (a rare but powerful option), talk to the desk directly — they will stitch a 9-night reservation with a single advance payment.

### Kartik Purnima

A single-day festival in November. Less crowded than Mahashivratri but the temple sees a noticeable spike. Bhakta Niwas booking pattern:

- **Open date:** 30 days before
- **Advance payment:** 50% of stay
- **Cancellation window:** 14 days before

Kartik Purnima often falls in the same week as the annual Trimbakeshwar temple festival — confirm the exact dates with the desk before booking.

### Monthly Shivaratri

Every 13th lunar day (Trayodashi) is a monthly Shivaratri. Trimbakeshwar sees a modest crowd increase on these dates — queue time doubles from the usual 30 minutes to about 60–90 minutes. Bhakta Niwas booking pattern:

- **Open date:** 14 days before
- **Advance payment:** 50% of stay
- **Cancellation window:** 7 days before

Monthly Shivaratri is a good option for pilgrims who want a quieter festival experience without the Mahashivratri crowd.

### The general Bhakta Niwas booking flow (any festival)

The flow is the same regardless of festival:

**Step 1 — Identify the festival date and the open date.**
Use the table above or check with the Sansthan desk.

**Step 2 — Send the WhatsApp request on or after the open date.**
Use the Sansthan booking template. Include festival name, arrival date, departure date, adults/children/seniors, and preferred room type.

**Step 3 — Receive the quotation.**
The desk shares the room option, total stay cost, and the advance payment amount.

**Step 4 — Pay the advance.**
Bank transfer or UPI. The desk confirms the UPI ID and the beneficiary name before you send the payment.

**Step 5 — Receive the confirmation.**
Within 48 hours of advance payment, the desk sends a confirmation message with your booking ID, room type, check-in and check-out dates, and the remaining balance if any.

**Step 6 — Pay the balance at check-in.**
Balance is collected at the Bhakta Niwas reception during check-in.

For the channel-by-channel flow see [Bhakta Niwas Booking Process](/blog/trimbakeshwar-bhakta-niwas-booking-process). For the message template see [Bhakta Niwas WhatsApp Message Template](/blog/trimbakeshwar-bhakta-niwas-whatsapp-message-template).

### What if the festival you want is fully booked?

Three fallback paths:

1. **Standby list.** The Sansthan desk maintains a standby list for every festival. Cancellations in the 7 days before the festival are passed down the standby list. Add yourself via WhatsApp.
2. **Stay 30–60 km away.** Nashik city (28 km), Igatpuri (60 km), and Sinnar (40 km) all have private hotels and dharamshal properties that fill later than Trimbakeshwar town. See [Route and Transport Options](/blog/trimbakeshwar-route-and-transport-options) for the commute logistics.
3. **Shift the date by one day.** Arriving the day before or staying the day after the festival often unlocks Bhakta Niwas rooms because the desk holds a smaller buffer inventory for the surrounding nights.

### Mistakes to avoid

- **Booking only the festival night and forgetting the day-before and day-after.** The hotel is often fully booked 2 days either side of the festival.
- **Waiting for the temple to publish the festival date.** The Sansthan desk confirms the date earlier than the temple and starts taking bookings earlier.
- **Paying the advance without a WhatsApp confirmation.** Always wait for the desk to share the UPI ID and beneficiary name before transferring.
- **Assuming the cancellation window is the same for every festival.** Mahashivratri has a stricter 21-day cut-off than the monthly Shivaratri 7-day cut-off.
