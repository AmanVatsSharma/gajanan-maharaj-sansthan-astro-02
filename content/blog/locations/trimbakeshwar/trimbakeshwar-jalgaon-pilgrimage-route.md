1. Book your return tickets in advance, especially during holidays.
2. Locate the nearest parking facility if you are using a private vehicle.
3. Plan your arrival to coincide with the check-in timings of your stay.
4. Check for any local traffic diversions during festival processions.

## Location-specific tips for devotees

Local transport within Shegaon is easy to find, but walking is often the fastest way during peak crowd hours.

Families visiting Trimbakeshwar often benefit from arriving early to avoid peak queues, especially on weekends and festival days. The temple complex and accommodation areas are well-maintained, with clear signage and helpful staff. Keep a copy of your booking confirmation handy, and carry light snacks and water for children.
