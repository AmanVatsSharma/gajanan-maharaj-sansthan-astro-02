## About Shegaon and what makes it distinctive

Shegaon is a small temple town in the Buldhana district of Maharashtra, situated in the eastern Vidarbha region on the fertile plains that lie between the Purna and Tapi river basins. The town sits at roughly 20.79 degrees north latitude and 76.69 degrees east longitude, and its surrounding landscape is dominated by cotton and soybean farmland, scattered acacia groves, and gently undulating terrain. Shegaon lies about 550 kilometres northeast of Mumbai by road, around 270 kilometres west of Nagpur, and roughly 100 kilometres south of the district headquarters at Buldhana, placing it firmly within the central Indian heartland rather than the coastal Konkan strip.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 1

1. Set aside a daily budget for meals, local travel, and small donations.
2. Keep small denomination notes for donations at the temple counter.
3. Allocate a separate amount for souvenirs and prasad to bring back home.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 2

1. Keep small bills and coins ready for tipping the dharamshala cleaning staff.
2. Tip the trust shuttle driver if they assist with luggage at the station.
3. Offer a small donation to the temple kitchen if you eat the free Mahaprasad.
4. Tip the local guide if they show you around the temple and explain the rituals.

## Darshan and visit planning checklist — Part 3

1. Note the local police, ambulance, and fire service helpline numbers.
2. Save the trust main office number and the dharamshala reception number.
3. Identify the nearest government hospital and pharmacy before you arrive.
4. Keep your embassy or consulate number saved if you are a foreign traveller.
