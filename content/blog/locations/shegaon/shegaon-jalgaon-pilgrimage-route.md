## Darshan and visit planning checklist

1. Carry light snacks and water for the journey to Buldhana district.
2. Ensure your vehicle has enough fuel as some routes have fewer stations.
3. Use GPS but also verify directions with locals for the final temple approach.
4. Keep your booking confirmation handy to show at the entry gates.

## Location-specific tips for devotees

Local transport within Shegaon is easy to find, but walking is often the fastest way during peak crowd hours.

Families visiting Shegaon often benefit from arriving early to avoid peak queues, especially on weekends and festival days. The temple complex and accommodation areas are well-maintained, with clear signage and helpful staff. Keep a copy of your booking confirmation handy, and carry light snacks and water for children.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there an online booking portal for Sansthan accommodation?** Currently, the Sansthan primarily manages bookings through official phone and WhatsApp channels to ensure fair distribution. Be wary of unofficial websites claiming to offer online bookings.

**Can I book a room for just one person?** Yes, dormitory facilities are often available for solo devotees, while family rooms are reserved for groups. ID proof is mandatory regardless of the group size.
