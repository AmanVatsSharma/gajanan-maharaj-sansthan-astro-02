# Shegaon Bhakta Niwas Check-in and Check-out Timings

Most rooms at Shegaon Bhakta Niwas follow a **24-hour check-out cycle counted from the time of your entry**, not a fixed hotel-style noon deadline. That means a family that checks in at 6 PM on Thursday is expected to check out by 6 PM on Friday, and pilgrims arriving on early-morning trains can usually take their room as soon as it is ready and the booking desk opens. Exact timings for your specific dates should be confirmed with the Sansthan office when your booking is confirmed — start at the [official booking page](/booking) or [contact the Sansthan team](/contact).

This guide walks through how check-in actually works at the Shegaon Bhakta Niwas complex, what documents to keep ready, how the 24-hour cycle plays out for different arrival patterns, and how to plan check-out day so you leave without rushing your last darshan of Shri Gajanan Maharaj's Samadhi Mandir.

## How check-in works at the Shegaon Bhakta Niwas desk — step by step

Devotees reach the Shegaon Bhakta Niwas reception with their booking confirmation — usually a message or reference shared by the Sansthan office after the booking request is processed through the [booking flow](/booking). The sequence below reflects the standard flow; small details can vary on heavy festival days.

1. **Show your confirmation.** Present the booking reference at the reception desk along with photo ID for each adult in the party.
2. **Room allocation.** The desk matches your booked room type — 2-bed, 3-bed, or 4-bed, AC or non-AC — against the day's allocation chart.
3. **Entry time recorded.** Your entry time starts the 24-hour cycle, so it is worth arriving at a time that suits your darshan and train plans rather than rushing at a fixed hour.
4. **Keys and housekeeping notes.** Collect your keys, ask about hot-water timings for your wing, and note the canteen and mahaprasad timings for the day.
5. **Settle any balance payment** as guided by the office, and ask for a printed receipt for every payment you make during the stay.

Full location details for the complex are on the [Shegaon Bhakta Niwas page](/locations/shegaon-bhakt-niwas).

## Early-morning arrivals and the Kakad Aarti advantage

Shegaon is one of the few pilgrimage towns where arriving before sunrise is a strategy, not a hardship. Overnight trains from Mumbai, Pune, and the Nagpur side deliver devotees to the Shegaon-Akola belt in the early hours, and many families go straight for the pre-dawn Kakad Aarti at the Samadhi Mandir before their room is ready. If that is your plan:

- Keep one light bag with pooja items and fresh clothes accessible, and leave heavier luggage with the reception as guided.
- Let the desk know at booking that you expect an early arrival, so your room readiness is planned the previous evening where possible.
- Confirm the next day's aarti and darshan schedule with the temple office, because timings shift across festival periods.

## Documents and details to keep ready

Check-in is quickest when everything is at hand:

1. Booking confirmation message or reference number.
2. Original photo ID for each adult — Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, or passport.
3. The exact headcount, including children, matching your booking.
4. Your expected arrival window, communicated to the office in advance.
5. Any special request — ground-floor room, adjoining rooms for a joint family, wheelchair-friendly access — raised at booking, not at the desk.

## Festival days change the rhythm

On Ekadashi, Gajanan Maharaj's Pragat Din, and major utsav days, the check-in queue moves slower simply because the whole town moves slower. Three practical adjustments:

- **Build a buffer of at least an hour** into any same-day darshan plan attached to your arrival.
- **Expect high occupancy** — festival-window bookings often need advance planning, which is why our [Ekadashi and festival room booking guide for Shegaon](/blog/shegaon-room-booking-during-ekadashi-and-festivals) exists.
- **Keep the office number from your confirmation handy** and call ahead if your train is delayed, so your room is held per the prevailing policy rather than assumed.

## Checking out without the rush

On departure day, devotees often want one last darshan or a morning aarti before leaving. Under the 24-hour cycle, that usually works naturally: check out at the desk, leave luggage as guided, collect it before your train, and leave buffer for the prasad counter and the walk to the station. Ask at reception the previous evening about any crowd expectations for the next morning — the office's guidance on the ground is always more current than any published article, including this one.

## Getting oriented: from Shegaon station to the reception

Shegaon rewards unhurried arrivals. The town is compact and walkable, and most devotees find their bearings within a single visit — the station, the Samadhi Mandir, and the Bhakta Niwas complex all sit within the same small pilgrimage circuit, so the first walk to the reception doubles as an orientation tour. Travellers carrying heavier luggage, senior citizens, and families with small children usually prefer an auto-rickshaw or shared transport for the short hop; these are readily available around the station area, and drivers are well used to devotee traffic towards the Sansthan's accommodation.

Two small preparations make arrival smoother. First, keep your booking confirmation handy — a screenshot or printout you can show at the desk within moments of arriving spares you digging through bags while the queue builds behind you. Second, if you are travelling with elderly devotees or anyone who needs physical assistance, arrange support in advance through the [contact page](/contact) rather than at the last minute; the office can note the requirement against your booking so the reception is prepared when you reach it.

First-time visitors need not worry about the layout. The reception is the natural first stop, and the desk routinely guides newcomers to their wing, the canteen, and the mandir — once you have checked in, every part of your stay begins from that one familiar point.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the check-out policy at Shegaon Bhakta Niwas?

Most accommodations follow a 24-hour check-out cycle counted from the time of entry, which gives families flexibility around train timings rather than a fixed noon deadline.

### Can I check in before my room is ready if I arrive very early?

Early arrivals are routine at Shegaon. The reception typically assists with luggage while you attend the Kakad Aarti or morning darshan, and the room is handed over as soon as it is ready.

### Do I need ID for children as well?

Photo ID is required for adults. For children, carrying a school ID or a copy of an Aadhaar where available speeds up the register, but the desk guides families case by case.

### What if my departure train is many hours after check-out?

Ask at the desk about luggage assistance after check-out. Most families use the gap for a final darshan, mahaprasad, or a short visit to Anand Sagar before collecting bags and leaving.

### Where do I confirm the exact timings for my dates?

Confirm with the Sansthan office when your booking is acknowledged — through the [booking page](/booking) or [contact page](/contact) — since timings can vary across seasons and festival periods.
