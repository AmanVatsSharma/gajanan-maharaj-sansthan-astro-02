## About Shegaon and what makes it distinctive

The history of the temple town is inseparable from the life of Shri Gajanan Maharaj, who is believed to have taken his samadhi at Shegaon in 1910. The original shrine was a modest structure, but the Sansthan has over more than a century built a substantial temple complex, a richly decorated samadhi mandap, and a network of religious and social buildings around the site. The Sansthan also preserves a library, a museum, and an archival office that maintain records of Maharaj's life and the growth of the trust over several generations.
