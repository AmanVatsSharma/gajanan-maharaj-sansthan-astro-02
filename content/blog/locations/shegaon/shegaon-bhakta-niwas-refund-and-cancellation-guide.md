# Shegaon Bhakta Niwas Refund and Cancellation

Cancellations at Shegaon Bhakta Niwas are handled **by the Sansthan office on written request, case by case**. Tell the office as early as you can through the official channels, quote your booking reference, and the advance you paid is adjusted as per the prevailing policy for your dates — with festival-window bookings treated more strictly because those rooms are in the heaviest demand. There is no self-service cancellation button, so this guide focuses on what actually works: a clean request, the right records, and realistic expectations. Start any cancellation or date change via the [contact page](/contact) or the channels given with your [booking confirmation](/booking).

We should say plainly what this guide is not: it is not a tariff of cancellation penalties. Policies for advance adjustment are set and applied by the office, and they vary with how close the request lands to the stay date and whether the dates fall in a festival window. What we can do — and do below — is walk you through the request flow, the records that protect you, and the situations that process smoothly versus the ones that do not.

## The short answer on cancellations

When your plans change, four principles cover almost every situation:

| Situation | What to do first | What typically happens |
|---|---|---|
| Plans change well before travel | Send a written request to the office quoting your reference | The office processes the change; advance adjustment follows the prevailing policy |
| You want different dates, not a refund | Ask for a date change rather than cancelling outright | Where feasible the office treats it as a modification rather than a fresh booking |
| Cancellation close to a festival window | Inform the office immediately, in writing | Handling is stricter because festival-week rooms are the hardest to re-allocate |
| You simply do not turn up | The room may be treated as a no-show per policy | Informing the office early is always better than silence |

The theme is consistent: communicate early, communicate in writing, and keep what you sent.

## Step-by-step: raising a cancellation or date-change request

Follow this sequence and your request will move through the office with minimum friction:

1. **Locate your confirmation.** Find the booking reference, message, or reference number the office sent you. Without it, the office must first identify your booking from your name and dates, which adds delay.
2. **Decide what you actually want.** A date shift, a shorter stay, a smaller room, or a full cancellation — requesting the correct thing once is faster than requesting three things across three messages.
3. **Write to the office through an official channel.** Use the contact route given with your confirmation or the [Sansthan contact page](/contact). Include your reference, the name the booking is in, your original dates and room type, and your request.
4. **State the reason briefly.** Train cancellations, illness, and family emergencies help the office prioritise and, where relevant, apply discretion.
5. **Ask for an acknowledgement of your cancellation request.** You want written proof that the office received it on a given date, not just a read receipt.
6. **Ask how the advance will be adjusted.** Let the office state the applicable handling for your case; do not assume the outcome based on another devotee's experience from a different season.
7. **Save the final closure message.** When the office confirms the cancellation is processed, keep that message with your travel papers until the journey would have ended.

Devotees who follow all seven steps rarely have disputes. Devotees who skip step five and step seven occasionally do.

## How advances are typically adjusted

The advance you pay with a Bhakta Niwas booking is part of the published tariff for your room — the same ₹1,650 to ₹2,550 range that applies across 2-bed, 3-bed, and 4-bed options as described in our [room types and facilities guide](/blog/shegaon-bhakta-niwas-room-types-and-facilities). How it is treated on cancellation depends on the timing and nature of your request:

| Your action | Typical adjustment approach |
|---|---|
| Date change requested well in advance | Advance commonly carried to the new dates where availability permits |
| Cancellation requested well in advance | Adjusted per the prevailing policy communicated by the office at the time of request |
| Cancellation close to the stay date | Stricter handling, because the room may not be re-allocated in time |
| Festival-window booking cancelled late | The strictest handling; these dates carry the heaviest demand all year |
| Partial stay change (shorter dates) | The office recalculates per the revised stay and guides you on the difference |

Two habits keep this entire table stress-free. First, pay only through the channels the office itself indicates, and take a receipt or written acknowledgement for every payment. Second, when the office states the adjustment for your case, keep that message. If anything needs revisiting later, your written record — not your memory of a phone call — is what carries the conversation.

## What changes around festival dates

Shegaon's room demand is not flat across the year, and cancellation handling reflects that. Around every ekadashi, and most sharply around Pragat Din in February and the Gajanan Vijay Utsav around Bhadrapad Shukla Chaturdashi, the town's accommodation runs near full. A room released late in these windows is a room another family desperately needed a day earlier.

| Period | What it means for cancellations |
|---|---|
| Ordinary weekdays | Most flexible window for changes and cancellations |
| Weekends and ekadashi dates | Higher demand; earlier notice is appreciated and often expected |
| Pragat Din and Gajanan Vijay Utsav windows | Strictest handling; book only dates you are confident about |
| Mahaprasad and utsav days adjacent to your stay | Even shoulder dates carry elevated demand |

If your festival-week plans are genuinely uncertain, the wiser path is usually to book the dates you are sure of and extend later subject to availability, rather than book a long festival window and cancel half of it. ## Requests that process smoothly — and ones that do not

After observing cancellation correspondence for seasons, the digital desk can predict which requests sail and which stall.

**Smooth requests look like this:**

- One written message containing the reference, original dates, room type, and the exact request.
- A reason stated in one line, with no long explanations.
- An acknowledgement requested and preserved.
- Questions about adjustment asked once, answered by the office, and accepted.

**Requests that struggle look like this:**

- Cancellation requested verbally on a phone call with nothing in writing.
- Multiple family members sending conflicting requests for the same booking.
- A request sent from a different phone number than the one the booking is tied to, without explaining why.
- Demands for a specific adjustment before the office has even confirmed receipt.
- Requests sent the night before a festival-week check-in.

If you recognise your own pending request in the second list, the fix is simple: send one clean, complete message today quoting your reference, and let the office respond.

## Special cases: illness, missed trains, and group splits

Three situations deserve their own guidance because devotees worry about them disproportionately.

**Illness before travel.** Inform the office the moment the situation is clear, in writing, with your reference. Offices handling pilgrim accommodation deal with illness-related requests routinely; what they cannot work with is silence followed by a dispute weeks later. Where a date change is feasible instead of a cancellation, ask for it in the same message.

**Missed or cancelled trains.** This is a delay, not a cancellation. Contact the office from the station if your train is cancelled or heavily delayed, so the hold on your room follows the office's current policy rather than assumption. If the delay turns into a lost night, convert your message into a formal request for adjustment for the unused night, and again — keep the office's reply.

**One family in a group cancelling.** Group bookings at Bhakta Niwas are usually a cluster of room requests travelling together. When one family drops out, do not send a request to cancel "the whole group"; identify the specific booking reference of the family that is cancelling, and state clearly that the other bookings stand. The most damaging administrative mix-up we see is a group coordinator cancelling five rooms when only one family's plans changed. And if the cancellation reflects a changed party rather than changed dates — a solo devotee no longer travelling, or a bigger family circle forming — revisit the accommodation type itself with our comparison of [dormitory versus family rooms](/blog/shegaon-bhakta-niwas-dormitory-vs-family-rooms) before rebooking.

| Situation | First message to the office should say |
|---|---|
| Illness before travel | Reference, the situation in one line, and whether you want new dates or a cancellation |
| Train delayed or cancelled | Reference, expected delay, and a request to hold the room per policy |
| One family in a group drops out | That family's reference only, plus an explicit "other bookings unchanged" |

## Records to keep from the moment you book

Refund conversations are short when your paperwork is long. From the day you request a room until a week after your journey, keep:

1. Your booking submission record, whichever channel you used.
2. The office's acknowledgement of your request.
3. The final booking confirmation with your reference.
4. A receipt or written acknowledgement for every payment, including the advance.
5. Any date-change or cancellation request you send, with the date visible.
6. The office's closure message on any cancellation.

That is six artefacts, most of them screenshots. Families who hold all six resolve any discrepancy in a single exchange. ## Frequently asked questions

### How do I cancel my Shegaon Bhakta Niwas booking?

Send a written request to the Sansthan office through the contact route given with your confirmation or the [contact page](/contact), quoting your booking reference, original dates, and room type. Ask for written acknowledgement of your cancellation request.

### Will I get my advance back if I cancel?

Advance adjustment on cancellation follows the prevailing policy the office applies to your case, which depends mainly on how early you inform them and whether your dates fall in a festival window. Ask the office to state the handling in writing when you cancel.

### Is cancelling different during festival weeks?

Yes — expect stricter handling around ekadashi dates, Pragat Din, and the Gajanan Vijay Utsav, because those rooms are the hardest to re-allocate. Inform the office as early as possible.

### Can I change dates instead of cancelling outright?

In most cases, yes — request a date change rather than a cancellation. Where availability permits, the office commonly treats it as a modification, which is often simpler than a fresh booking.

### What proof should I keep after cancelling?

Your original confirmation, your written cancellation request with its date, the office's acknowledgement, and the office's closure message confirming the cancellation was processed.

### Who decides the refund amount?

The Sansthan office, applying the prevailing policy to your dates and timing. This guide deliberately does not quote amounts because they are set by the office case by case — confirm yours in writing when you cancel.
