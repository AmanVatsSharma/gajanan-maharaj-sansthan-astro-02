# Shegaon Bhakta Niwas Room Types and Facilities

Shegaon Bhakta Niwas offers **five published room configurations across two bed counts and two comfort levels: 2 Bed AC at ₹1,650, 3 Bed Non-AC at ₹1,750, 3 Bed AC at ₹2,050, 4 Bed Non-AC at ₹2,250, and 4 Bed AC at ₹2,550 per night**. Capacity is counted in beds, every room carries an attached bathroom with hot water, and the Sansthan's published pricing states that tariffs include room accommodation, all meals, applicable taxes, and hot-water facilities — with no hidden charges. You request any of these through the [booking page](/booking), and the office confirms availability for your dates.

This guide is the complete reference: the full tariff table, what each tariff includes, how to decide between AC and non-AC by season, the facilities inside and around the rooms, and how to request the specific room your family needs. For how to choose between the common hall and a private room, see our companion piece on [dormitory versus family rooms](/blog/shegaon-bhakta-niwas-dormitory-vs-family-rooms).

## The full tariff table at a glance

| Room type | Beds | Tariff per night | Comfort level | Natural party |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Bed AC | 2 | ₹1,650 | Air-conditioned | Couples and elderly pairs |
| 3 Bed Non-AC | 3 | ₹1,750 | Natural ventilation | Small families in cooler months |
| 3 Bed AC | 3 | ₹2,050 | Air-conditioned | Small families in warmer months |
| 4 Bed Non-AC | 4 | ₹2,250 | Natural ventilation | Standard family option |
| 4 Bed AC | 4 | ₹2,550 | Air-conditioned | Larger families, April–June travel |

Read the table with two rules in mind. First, capacity equals the bed count — a 3-bed room sleeps three, and the register matches headcount to beds, so state your party size accurately when booking. Second, the gaps between tariffs are modest by design: moving from a 3 Bed Non-AC to a 3 Bed AC costs ₹300, and from 4 Bed Non-AC to 4 Bed AC costs ₹300, so the decision is comfort and season rather than budget strain.

Beyond these five core configurations, the Sansthan also maintains premium options such as suites at select complexes — those are allocated and quoted by the office on request rather than through the standard chart, so ask through the [contact page](/contact) if your family needs one.

## What the tariff includes

Devotees accustomed to hotel pricing often brace for add-ons that never come. The Sansthan's published pricing is explicit:

| Included in every tariff | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Room accommodation | The room and its beds for your party for the 24-hour cycle |
| All meals — breakfast, lunch, dinner | Taken at the complex's canteen and mahaprasad arrangements; no meal billing at stay's end |
| Applicable taxes | The quoted figure already carries them |
| Hot-water facilities | Attached bathroom with hot water, with timings guided by the wing |
| No hidden charges | The final price is exactly as shown — the published promise |

This is why comparing Bhakta Niwas with a budget hotel on room rate alone misleads: the hotel quote excludes dinner and taxes, while the Sansthan tariff is the whole account. Families budgeting a two-night stay can multiply the tariff by two and be done. ## AC or non-AC: a seasonal decision

The ₹300 difference between AC and non-AC versions of the same bed count makes this a seasonal judgement rather than a financial one. Vidarbha's climate sets the calendar:

| Travel window | Recommended choice | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| March to June | AC — 2 Bed AC, 3 Bed AC, or 4 Bed AC | Peak summer heat in the Shegaon belt; afternoons between darshan rounds are for resting |
| July to September (monsoon) | Non-AC comfortable; AC by preference | Humidity varies; ventilation is usually pleasant after rain |
| October to November | Non-AC comfortable | Post-monsoon warmth by day, cooling evenings; festive periods raise footfall |
| December to February | Non-AC | Winter mornings in Buldhana district are genuinely cool; the AC sits idle |
| Festival weeks in any season | Book early rather than shop late | Choice narrows before comfort debates begin |

Two practical notes. Elders resting through a hot afternoon before the evening aarti benefit from AC more than any other travellers — if your yatra falls in April–June with grandparents in the party, take the AC room without agonising. And children sleep through summer nights better in AC rooms, which protects the next morning's early darshan. The seasonal backdrop, including festival timing, is covered in our [best time to visit Shegaon guide](/blog/shegaon-best-time-to-visit).

A word on how a Shegaon summer day unfolds, because it should shape the room as much as the dates do. In April, May, and June the heat builds through the late morning, holds through the afternoon, and yields only as the evening approaches — the hours a family most needs its room are exactly the hours the sun is at its hardest. The practical summer pattern is therefore darshan in the early morning, mahaprasad, a closed and cooled room through the afternoon, and a return to the mandir as the light softens. Book the AC configuration for those months without treating it as a luxury: it is what converts the day's worst hours into the stay's most restful ones. And because every family reasons the same way, AC rooms are the first configurations to thin out in the summer festival windows — request them early rather than negotiating for them late.

## Facilities inside and around the room

Every published room type carries the same facility baseline, maintained by the Sansthan across its network:

- **Clean and well-maintained rooms** — housekeeping runs through the blocks daily.
- **Comfortable bedding and pillows** — one per bed, per the room's capacity.
- **Attached bathroom with hot water** — the single most-appreciated facility in pilgrim feedback, especially in winter.
- **24/7 customer support** — assistance channels around the complex; problems are fixed, not filed.
- **Peaceful spiritual environment** — quiet hours and devotional decorum are maintained.

Around the rooms, the Bhakta Niwas complex adds the amenities that define a pilgrimage stay: **hot water in the mornings**, the **mahaprasad canteen** for the meals your tariff already includes, a **free bus service** for devotees moving between the complex, temple, and Anand Sagar, and **parking** for families arriving by car. Together the baseline and the complex amenities mean a family arrives with clothes and devotion, and needs little else.

## Matching room type to your party

| Your party | Right configuration | Notes for the request |
|---|---|---|
| Married couple | 2 Bed AC | Smallest private room; ideal for elderly pairs too |
| Couple with one child | 3 Bed AC or Non-AC by season | One bed each; no bed-sharing negotiations |
| Two adults and two children | 4 Bed Non-AC / 4 Bed AC | The standard family room |
| Three adults and one child | 4 Bed AC or Non-AC | State the child in the headcount |
| Joint family of five or six | Two rooms — e.g., 4 Bed plus 2 Bed | Request adjoining rooms at booking time |
| Family needing ground floor | Any type, request noted | Raise accessibility at booking, not at the desk |

The joint-family row deserves emphasis: two correctly sized rooms beat one overstuffed room every time — bathrooms, bedding, and the register all run smoothly. Splitting a large party is covered further in our [common booking mistakes guide](/blog/shegaon-bhakta-niwas-common-booking-mistakes), which also explains why understating headcount backfires at allocation.

## If your first choice is unavailable

On busy dates — ekadashi evenings, long weekends, the days around Pragat Din and the Gajanan Vijay Utsav — the office may respond that your exact configuration is taken. Having a fallback ready converts a disappointment into a decision:

| What is full | Sensible fallback | Trade-off you accept |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Bed AC | 4 Bed Non-AC plus ₹300 saved | Ventilation instead of cooling |
| 4 Bed of both kinds | A 3 Bed plus a 2 Bed | Two rooms; request adjoining |
| AC rooms in April–June | Shift dates by a day either side | Darshan on a quieter day |
| Bhakta Niwas entirely | Anand Vihar near Anand Sagar, or the station-side Visawa complex | A longer walk or short ride to the Samadhi Mandir |
| Everything in Shegaon that night | Arrive next morning instead, or check the dormitory if you travel solo | One less night, same darshan |

Note what never appears in the fallback column: overstating your headcount to grab a bigger room, or splitting your family across unverified private lodgings to force the dates. The first fails at the register; the second works against the peace the yatra exists to find. When the office suggests an alternative, they can also tell you what each option genuinely involves — the walking distances, the comfort level, the character of each complex — so treat their reply as advice worth taking.

## How to request a specific room type

The request flow takes minutes when you arrive prepared:

1. **Fix your party size honestly** — adults and children counted, because capacity equals beds.
2. **Choose the configuration by bed count**, then the comfort level by season, using the tables above.
3. **Open the [booking page](/booking)** and enter dates, room type, headcount, and contact details.
4. **Add special requests in the remarks** — adjoining rooms, ground floor, early arrival — where the office will see them with the request itself.
5. **Save the submission record**, then the office's acknowledgement, and finally the confirmation that names your room type.
6. **Carry photo ID for every adult** — Aadhaar, Voter ID, or equivalent — matched to the names in the request.

The same remarks field is where a specific wing or room request belongs. If your family needs a ground-floor room for an elder who cannot manage stairs, adjoining doors for a split party, or quieter placement for light-sleeping children, write the preference into the request itself rather than raising it at the desk. A note entered at booking travels with your file to whoever draws up the day's allocation chart, so the preference is already in view when rooms are assigned; the same request made on arrival day depends entirely on what the chart happens to hold. The office honours such preferences as allocation allows and says so plainly when it cannot — either way, asking early costs nothing and often decides the question.

At check-in the desk matches your confirmation against the day's allocation chart, records your entry time to start the 24-hour cycle, and hands over the keys — the full sequence is walked through in our [check-in and check-out timings guide](/blog/shegaon-bhakta-niwas-check-in-check-out-timings), and the request discipline behind it is part of the wider [complete Bhakta Niwas booking guide](/blog/bhakta-niwas-complete-booking-guide). If your dates fall near ekadashi or a Shegaon utsav, read our [ekadashi and festival booking guide](/blog/shegaon-room-booking-during-ekadashi-and-festivals) before requesting, because advance windows tighten sharply in those weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the room types at Shegaon Bhakta Niwas?

Five published configurations: 2 Bed AC (₹1,650), 3 Bed Non-AC (₹1,750), 3 Bed AC (₹2,050), 4 Bed Non-AC (₹2,250), and 4 Bed AC (₹2,550). Capacity is the bed count, and premium suites at select complexes are quoted by the office on request.

### Do the tariffs include food?

Yes — the Sansthan's published pricing states that tariffs include room accommodation, all meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), applicable taxes, and hot-water facilities, with no hidden charges.

### Is AC necessary at Shegaon?

Between March and June, AC rooms markedly improve afternoon rest and children's sleep. From October to February, non-AC rooms are comfortable and the more natural choice.

### How many people can stay in one room?

Exactly the bed count of the configuration booked — two, three, or four. The register matches headcount to beds, so book two correctly sized rooms for a party of five or six rather than one overstuffed room.

### Are bathrooms attached?

Yes — every room has an attached bathroom with hot water, with timings for hot water guided by your wing at check-in.

### Can I request a ground-floor room?

Yes, note it in the remarks of your booking request through the [booking page](/booking). Requests raised at booking are far easier for the office to honour than requests raised at the desk.
