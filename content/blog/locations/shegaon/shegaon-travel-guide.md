# Complete Travel Guide to Shegaon

Shegaon, the home of Shri Gajanan Maharaj's Samadhi Mandir, is one of the most-visited pilgrimage towns in the Vidarbha region of Maharashtra. Lakhs of devotees arrive every year for darshan, and the town has well-developed rail and road connectivity, with Sansthan-managed accommodation options that make planning straightforward. This guide consolidates routes, trains, best season, and on-ground logistics into a single, planning-friendly reference.

## How to reach Shegaon

Shegaon Junction (station code: **SGO**) is on the Mumbai–Howrah trunk line, which is one of the busiest and most-served rail corridors in India. The town is also connected by state highways and is within easy road reach of Khamgaon, Akola, Buldhana, and Amravati. Most pilgrims arrive by train; the second-most common mode is by private car or hired taxi; the third is by state transport bus.

### By train — most popular option

Long-distance trains that stop at Shegaon Junction include the Vidarbha Express, Sevagram Express, Howrah Mail, Gitanjali Express, and the Maharashtra Express. From Mumbai, the fastest options are the Vidarbha Express and the Sewagram Express, with journey times of 8–10 hours. From Pune, the typical pattern is to board an early-morning train to Mumbai, change at Lokmanya Tilak Terminus or Dadar, and reach Shegaon by evening. From Nagpur, several daily trains cover the 250 km stretch in 4–5 hours. From Hyderabad, a direct overnight train or a connecting service via Nagpur is the typical route.

The station itself is compact, with prepaid taxi and auto-rickshaw stands just outside the main exit. The Sansthan complex is approximately 2 km from the station and is easily reachable by auto, with most drivers familiar with the Bhakta Niwas and the Mandir drop-off points.

### By road

By road, Shegaon is approximately:

- **28 km from Khamgaon** (the nearest major town; well-connected by buses and shared taxis)
- **100 km from Akola** (a major city with a domestic airport; connected by NH53 and state highway)
- **250 km from Nagpur** (the closest major airport with domestic and limited international connections)
- **550 km from Mumbai** (NH53/Mumbai–Nagpur Samruddhi Mahamarg via Nagpur is the fastest; older NH6 route is a longer but more scenic alternative)
- **470 km from Pune** (Mumbai–Pune Expressway + NH65 + NH53; best split into two days for senior citizens)
- **500 km from Hyderabad** (NH44/NH65 via Nanded; an overnight drive is feasible but tiring)
- **270 km from Amravati** (NH53 and state highway; a calm 5–6 hour drive)

For families with senior citizens or young children, splitting the road journey into two days with an overnight halt at Akola, Nagpur, or Aurangabad is strongly recommended. The Sansthan office can advise on mid-journey dharamshalas if you request a multi-day itinerary.

## Best season to visit

Shegaon can be visited year-round, but the most popular and most comfortable season is **October to March**, when the Vidarbha climate is dry and pleasant. The monsoon (July–September) is beautiful but can slow road travel in the ghat sections to the south. The peak summer (April–June) sees temperatures of 40–46°C and is best avoided unless the trip is unavoidable. The most spiritually significant periods are **Pragat Din** (anniversary of Shri Gajanan Maharaj's manifestation) and **Punyatithi Aradhana**, both of which draw lakhs of devotees and require advance accommodation booking.

## What to carry for the yatra

- **ID proof** for all travelers (Aadhaar, PAN, or passport for foreign devotees)
- **Light cotton** for summer, a light woollen layer for winter evenings
- **Modest, traditional clothing** for the Mandir darshan
- **Comfortable walking shoes** with grip
- **Basic medicines** including ORS, paracetamol, and personal prescriptions
- **A small umbrella or raincoat** during monsoon
- **A printed or mobile copy of your booking confirmation**
- **A reusable water bottle** — the Sansthan maintains water stations near the Mandir and the Bhakta Niwas
- **A small cloth bag** for prasad and a separate bag for the Annakshetra meal coupon (distributed on busy days)

## Suggested 3-day Shegaon itinerary

A balanced 3-day plan for a first-time family visit typically looks like:

- **Day 1:** Arrive in the morning, check into Bhakta Niwas, freshen up, attend the evening aarti at the Samadhi Mandir, and have prasad at the Annakshetra.
- **Day 2:** Morning darshan at the Mandir, lunch at the Annakshetra, afternoon visit to Anand Sagar, and the evening Dindi procession (during non-monsoon seasons).
- **Day 3:** Final morning darshan and darshan of Shri Gajanan Maharaj's Paduka, then depart after lunch.

This is a comfortable cadence for most families. If you have a fourth day, add a half-day excursion to nearby Anand Vihar or the local devotional points covered in our [Nearby Places from Shegaon](/blog/nearby-places-from-shegaon) guide.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the fastest train from Mumbai to Shegaon?** The Vidarbha Express and the Sewagram Express are the most reliable options, departing from Mumbai CST in the late evening and arriving in Shegaon the next morning. Journey time is 8–10 hours.

**Is the town walkable?** Yes. The Mandir, Bhakta Niwas, Anand Sagar, and the main market are all within a 2–3 km radius. Most families find the town easy to navigate on foot for short stretches, supplemented by auto-rickshaws for longer hops.

**When is the festival darshan most crowded?** The Pragat Din, Punyatithi Aradhana, Maha Shivaratri, and Ganesh Chaturthi are the most crowded periods. If you are planning to visit during these windows, book accommodation at least 4–6 weeks in advance and arrive a day before the festival to settle in.
