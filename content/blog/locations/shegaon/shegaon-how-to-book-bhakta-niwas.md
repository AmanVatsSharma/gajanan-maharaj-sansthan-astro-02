# Shegaon How to Book Bhakta Niwas

Booking a room at Bhakta Niwas is not complicated, but it follows a process that is specific to the Sansthan's approach to accommodation. The system is designed to serve as many devotees as possible fairly, which means there are no fancy online portals, no dynamic pricing algorithms, and no last-minute discounts. Understanding how the system works — and planning around its rhythms — is the key to securing the room you want, especially during festival season when demand far exceeds supply.

## The three booking channels

The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan accepts accommodation requests through three channels, all of which converge on the same booking desk.

Phone call is the most direct method. The single booking number, +91 80531 90691, connects you to the Sansthan's accommodation desk. During regular months, the desk is typically responsive during business hours. During festival windows — particularly the six weeks before Samadhi Utsav — the volume of calls increases dramatically, and you may need to try multiple times or call very early in the morning to get through.

WhatsApp is the second channel and, for many devotees, the more practical option. The same number (+91 80531 90691) handles WhatsApp messages, and the text-based format gives you a permanent record of your booking confirmation. You can send your group details, preferred dates, and room category in a single message, and the booking desk will reply with availability and pricing. For international devotees or anyone with spotty voice connectivity, WhatsApp is the more reliable option.

The online booking form on this website is the third channel. When you visit the [booking page](/booking), you can fill out a simple form with your travel dates, group size, room preference, and contact details. The form submits directly to the Sansthan booking desk, and you will receive a response within hours. This channel is particularly useful if you are booking from abroad or outside Indian business hours, as the form is processed the next working day.

All three channels feed into the same booking system, so it does not matter which one you use. If you submit a form and also send a WhatsApp message, the desk will see both as one enquiry.

## The booking conversation: what to expect

When you make contact, the booking desk will ask for a handful of details that determine your eligibility and room allocation. Having these ready before you call or message saves time and reduces the back-and-forth:

Number of adults and children in your group. This is the primary determinant of room allocation. The Sansthan enforces a minimum occupancy of three family members for private rooms, which means a couple travelling alone cannot book a private room and will be directed to the dormitory hall.

Preferred room category. If you have a specific room type in mind — say, a 4-bed AC for a family of four during summer — mention it upfront. If that category is unavailable, the desk will suggest alternatives, typically the closest equivalent in a different AC configuration.

Intended check-in and check-out dates. Be specific about dates rather than saying "around the second week of February." The Sansthan operates on exact dates, and approximate windows make it harder for the desk to check availability accurately.

ID proof for every adult. Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, voter ID, passport, or driving licence) is mandatory for every adult guest at check-in. For children below twelve, a school ID or birth certificate is acceptable. Foreign nationals need a passport and valid visa. The booking desk will confirm the specific ID requirements when you book.

## Step-by-step booking flow

Here is how the process works from first contact to check-in:

Step 1 — Enquiry. Call or WhatsApp +91 80531 90691, or submit the form at [/booking](/booking). Provide your group details, dates, and room preference.

Step 2 — Confirmation. The booking desk checks availability and sends you a confirmation message with the room details, tariff, check-in instructions, and any applicable cancellation policy. Read this message carefully and save it.

Step 3 — Token payment (if required). During regular months, the Sansthan does not require advance payment. During festival season — Pragat Din, Samadhi Utsav, Ganesh Chaturthi — a token amount may be requested to hold the room against high demand. If a token is requested, confirm the amount, the UPI or bank transfer details, and the reference format you should use.

Step 4 — Payment confirmation. After transferring any token amount, call or message the booking desk to confirm that the payment has been received and your room is held. Do not skip this step — payment confirmations sometimes get delayed during peak periods, and a follow-up call prevents the unpleasant surprise of arriving to find your room given to another group.

Step 5 — Check-in. Arrive at the Bhakta Niwas reception with your confirmation message, the original ID proofs of all adults, and the token payment receipt if applicable. The front desk will verify your details, allocate the room, and provide the key. The check-in process typically takes 15 to 20 minutes.

## Booking during festival season

The demand for rooms during Pragat Din (February 12) and Samadhi Utsav (August-September) is extraordinary. The Sansthan's entire room inventory — across Bhakta Niwas, Anand Vihar, and Visawa — is typically booked out 30 to 60 days before these festivals. The booking desk receives hundreds of calls and messages every day during this window, and rooms at the dormitory level can also sell out.

Book as early as possible. If you want to attend Pragat Din, call the booking desk in December of the preceding year. For Samadhi Utsav, start enquiring in June. The earlier you call, the better your chances of getting the room type and dates you want.

During festival season, the Sansthan may impose a minimum stay requirement — typically two or three nights — to allow the maximum number of devotees to experience the festival. The booking desk will inform you of any such requirement when you call.

## Understanding the room allocation system

The Sansthan does not use a dynamic pricing or overbooking system. Rooms are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis within each category, and the booking desk maintains a simple ledger of confirmed bookings against room inventory. This approach has its limitations during peak periods — there is no "premium" lane to jump the queue — but it also means that the booking process is transparent and predictable. The person who calls first gets the room, and the tariff is the same for everyone.

The family-only allocation policy is worth understanding before you call. Private rooms (all categories except the dormitory hall) are allocated exclusively to families. The Sansthan defines a family as a group with a minimum of three members, and married couples are welcome. Unmarried couples are not allocated private rooms; this policy is strictly enforced, particularly during festival periods when demand is high. If you are travelling as a couple without children, the dormitory hall is the appropriate booking category.

ID verification at check-in is non-negotiable. Every adult guest must present a valid government-issued photo ID, and the name on the ID must match the name provided during booking. This rule exists to prevent misuse of the room quota and to ensure that accommodation reaches genuine devotees. If you arrive without proper ID, the Sansthan may deny check-in even if you have a confirmed booking.

## Using the online booking form

The booking form on this website is a convenient alternative to phone or WhatsApp, especially if you are planning your trip from abroad or outside Indian business hours. To use it, visit the [booking page](/booking) and fill in your travel dates, group composition, room preference, and contact details. The form is straightforward and takes about two minutes to complete.

After submitting the form, the Sansthan booking desk will review your request and respond via phone or WhatsApp within a few hours (typically within one business day). The confirmation message will include the same details as a phone booking — room type, tariff, check-in instructions, and cancellation policy. There is no difference between a form-based booking and a phone booking in terms of the room allocation or the tariff.

For groups of 20 or more, the form is particularly useful because it allows you to specify the exact room breakdown you need across multiple categories. The Sansthan's group booking team will contact you directly to confirm the allocation and arrange any special requirements. ## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What is the phone number for Bhakta Niwas booking?**
A: The official booking number is +91 80531 90691. This number handles voice calls and WhatsApp messages for all Sansthan accommodation at Shegaon, including Bhakta Niwas, Anand Vihar, and Visawa.

**Q: Can I book a room online without calling?**
A: Yes. The [booking form](/booking) on this website allows you to submit your enquiry online. The Sansthan booking desk will respond via phone or WhatsApp within a few hours with availability and pricing.

**Q: How far in advance must I book during festival season?**
A: Book at least four to six weeks ahead for Pragat Din and Samadhi Utsav. Rooms typically sell out 30 to 60 days before these festivals. For regular weekends, two weeks of advance booking is usually sufficient. The earlier you call, the better your chances of securing your preferred room type.

**Q: Is an advance payment required?**
A: During regular months, no advance payment is typically required. During festival season, the Sansthan may request a token amount to hold the room against high demand. Confirm the payment method and reference number with the booking desk before transferring any amount.

**Q: Can a single person book a private room?**
A: Private rooms are allocated to families with a minimum of three members. Single travellers and solo devotees are directed to the dormitory-style common hall at Bhakta Niwas, which is designed specifically for this purpose.

**Q: What happens if my train arrives late at night?**
A: The 24-hour check-in policy means your room is allocated from the time you check in, not from a fixed morning hour. If your train arrives at 2:00 am, you can check in immediately and the room will be yours until 2:00 am the next day. For late arrivals, call the booking desk in advance to let them know your expected arrival time.
