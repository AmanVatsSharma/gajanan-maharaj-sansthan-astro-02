## About Omkareshwar and what makes it distinctive

Omkareshwar is a pilgrimage town in the Khandwa district of Madhya Pradesh, set on a tear-shaped island called Mandhata or Shivapuri in the middle of the Narmada river. The island is famous for its uncanny resemblance to the Om symbol when viewed from above, with the natural curvature of the hills, ghats, and shoreline tracing the sacred syllable around its perimeter. The town lies roughly 77 kilometres from Indore and approximately 130 kilometres from the district headquarters at Khandwa, embedded deep in the Narmada valley between Vindhya and Satpura foothills.
