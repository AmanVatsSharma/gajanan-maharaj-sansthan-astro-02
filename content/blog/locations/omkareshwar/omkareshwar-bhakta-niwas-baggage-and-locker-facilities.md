## About Omkareshwar and what makes it distinctive

The road approach to Omkareshwar crosses the Indira Sagar reservoir, one of the largest man-made lakes in central India, formed by the Indira Sagar Dam on the Narmada. The dam has reshaped the lower Narmada valley, raising water levels near the island and creating a wide, lake-like expanse that is itself considered sacred by pilgrims. Boating on this reservoir at sunrise is a common part of the Omkareshwar experience, and the calm waters around the island provide some of the most photographed views of the temple towers.
