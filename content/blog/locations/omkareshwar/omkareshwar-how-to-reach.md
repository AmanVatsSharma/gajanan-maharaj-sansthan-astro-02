Omkareshwar is located in the Khandwa district of Madhya Pradesh, on the banks of the Narmada river. The town is accessible by train, bus, and road from most major cities in central and western India. For devotees based in Maharashtra, the most common route is via Indore or Khandwa Junction. For devotees based in Gujarat, the route via Barwaha or the Indore route is most convenient. This guide covers all the major routes to Omkareshwar, the transport options at each stage, and the practical tips that seasoned pilgrims share with newcomers.

## By Train: The Most Popular Route

Khandwa Junction is the nearest major railway station to Omkareshwar, located around 70 kilometres away. Khandwa Junction has direct rail connections from Mumbai, Delhi, Nagpur, Indore, and Bhopal. The journey from Mumbai to Khandwa takes around 10 to 12 hours on the Jan Shatabdi Express, and from Nagpur, it takes around 5 to 6 hours. From Khandwa Junction, devotees can take the local train to Omkareshwar Road station, which is around 2 to 3 kilometres from the ghat. Shared auto-rickshaws and taxis are available at Omkareshwar Road station to take you to the boat ghat.

## By Bus: MSRTC and Private Options

MSRTC operates regular buses from Indore, Khandwa, and other nearby towns to Omkareshwar. The bus stand is located near the boat ghat, making it convenient for pilgrims. The journey from Indore to Omkareshwar takes around 2 to 3 hours, and the bus fare is typically around INR 150 to 250 per person. Private bus operators also operate on the Indore-Omkareshwar route, and these buses are often more comfortable but slightly more expensive.

## By Road: Taxi and Private Vehicle

The road from Indore to Omkareshwar is around 130 kilometres and takes around 2 to 3 hours. The road is in good condition, and the journey passes through scenic countryside. Taxis are available at the Indore airport and railway station, and the fare for a one-way trip from Indore to Omkareshwar is typically around INR 2,000 to 3,500. For devotees arriving from Mumbai, the road journey is around 550 kilometres and takes around 10 to 12 hours.

## From Mumbai and Pune

The most convenient route from Mumbai is the train to Khandwa Junction. The Jan Shatabdi Express departs from Mumbai Central in the morning and reaches Khandwa by evening. From Pune, the best option is the train to Khandwa via Bhusaval. The journey takes around 12 to 14 hours. Alternatively, devotees can fly from Pune to Indore and then take a taxi or bus to Omkareshwar. For detailed train routes, see our [omkareshwar-train-and-bus-arrival-planning].

## From Nagpur and Other Vidarbha Cities

From Nagpur, the most direct route is the train to Khandwa Junction. The journey takes around 5 to 6 hours. Shared taxis are also available from Nagpur to Omkareshwar, but the journey takes around 7 to 8 hours. From Akola, the distance to Omkareshwar is around 180 kilometres, and the journey takes around 4 to 5 hours by taxi.

## From Indore

Indore is the closest major city to Omkareshwar, around 130 kilometres away. MSRTC buses operate regularly from Indore to Omkareshwar, and the journey takes around 2 to 3 hours. Taxis are also available from Indore, and the fare is typically around INR 2,000 to 3,500 for a one-way trip. Many devotees combine their visit to Omkareshwar with a visit to Ujjain, which is around 135 kilometres from Indore.

## The Boat Ride to the Island

Once you reach the boat ghat, the final step to the temple is the boat ride to Mandhata island. The boat tickets are purchased at the ghat, and the ride takes around 10 to 15 minutes. The first boat typically departs around 5:30 AM, and the last boat departs around 8:30 to 9:00 PM. During peak hours, the boat service operates at maximum frequency. For first-time visitors, the boat ride is an integral part of the Omkareshwar experience. For accommodation options near the ghat, see our [omkareshwar-accommodation-guide].

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the nearest airport to Omkareshwar?**
The nearest airport is Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Airport in Indore, around 130 kilometres from Omkareshwar. Taxis and buses are available from the airport to Omkareshwar.

**Q: How far is Omkareshwar from Ujjain?**
Omkareshwar is around 135 kilometres from Ujjain by road, taking around 2.5 to 3 hours by taxi or bus.

**Q: Are there direct trains to Omkareshwar?**
No direct trains terminate at Omkareshwar. The nearest railhead is Omkareshwar Road station, which is around 2 to 3 kilometres from the ghat. Devotees typically travel to Khandwa Junction and then take a connecting train or taxi to Omkareshwar Road.

**Q: How long does the boat ride take?**
The boat ride from the mainland ghat to Mandhata island takes around 10 to 15 minutes.
