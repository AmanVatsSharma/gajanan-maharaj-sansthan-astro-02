## About Omkareshwar and what makes it distinctive

The mythology associated with Omkareshwar is closely tied to the Vindhya range, the sage Narada, and the shape of the island itself. Tradition holds that the Vindhya mountains once challenged the Himalayas and prayed to Shiva on this spot, and that the cleft hills of Mandhata absorbed the sound of the Om mantra in a way that gave the place its name. The Parashurama tradition, the Markandeya Purana, and several local Puranas are cited by priests on the ghats when explaining the island's sacred geography to visiting devotees.
