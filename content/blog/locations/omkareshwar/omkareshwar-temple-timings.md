The Omkareshwar temple follows a daily schedule that includes morning darshan, afternoon rest, and evening darshan with aarti. For devotees planning a visit, understanding the temple timings helps in planning the day around the most spiritually meaningful hours. The temple is open from approximately 5:00 AM to 9:30 PM, with the doors closing for a brief rest period in the afternoon. The Mamleshwar temple on the mainland has similar timings but with slightly shorter hours. This guide covers the complete daily schedule of the Omkareshwar and Mamleshwar temples, the aarti timings, and the practical tips that help you plan your visit around the most spiritually meaningful hours.

## Omkareshwar Temple Daily Schedule

The Omkareshwar temple opens its doors at 5:00 AM for the morning darshan. The first aarti of the day, called Mangal Aarti, takes place around 5:30 AM and is the most sacred and intimate aarti of the day. The temple remains open for darshan throughout the morning, with a brief closure around 12:30 PM to 4:00 PM for rest and rituals. The temple reopens at 4:00 PM for evening darshan, and the famous Narmada aarti takes place around sunset on the ghat across from the temple. The temple closes at approximately 9:30 PM, after the final aarti.

## Mangal Aarti at Sunrise

The Mangal Aarti, performed around 5:30 AM, is the most sacred and intimate aarti at Omkareshwar. The temple is bathed in the soft light of oil lamps, and the priests chant the Rudra mantras with great devotion. Devotees who attend the Mangal Aarti describe it as a deeply personal and transformative experience. The temple doors are open to devotees before the aarti begins, and the queue for Mangal Aarti darshan is typically shorter than during peak hours.

## Midday Rest Period

The temple closes for a brief rest period around 12:30 PM and reopens at 4:00 PM. During this rest period, the priests perform the Madhyahna Puja and other rituals that are not open to devotees. The temple complex and the parikrama path remain accessible during the rest period, so devotees can continue exploring the island.

## Evening Darshan and Aarti

The temple reopens at 4:00 PM for evening darshan. The evening darshan is popular with devotees who are on day trips from nearby towns, and the temple sees a steady stream of visitors throughout the evening. The famous Narmada aarti takes place around sunset on the ghat across from the temple, and the darshan experience during the aarti is particularly moving.

## Closing Time and Final Aarti

The temple closes at approximately 9:30 PM, after the final Shayan Aarti. The Shayan Aarti marks the symbolic rest of the deity, and attending it is a deeply moving experience. After the final aarti, the temple doors close for the night.

## Mamleshwar Temple Timings

The Mamleshwar temple on the mainland has similar timings, opening around 5:30 AM and closing around 10:00 PM. The temple does not have a midday rest period and is open throughout the day. The Mamleshwar temple is less crowded than the Omkareshwar temple, and many devotees visit it before or after their visit to the island.

## Festival Timings

During festivals like Mahashivratri and Karthik Purnima, the temple timings are extended. The temple remains open throughout the night during Mahashivratri, with continuous aarti and puja. During Karthik Purnima, special aartis are scheduled in the evening, and the temple sees large crowds throughout the day. For festival planning, see our [omkareshwar-festival-season-guide].

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the Mangal Aarti time at Omkareshwar?**
The Mangal Aarti takes place around 5:30 AM. Devotees are allowed to enter the temple before the aarti begins.

**Q: Is the temple closed in the afternoon?**
The temple closes for a brief rest period around 12:30 PM and reopens at 4:00 PM. The island parikrama path remains open during this period.

**Q: What time is the evening aarti on the ghat?**
The Narmada aarti on the ghat takes place around sunset, typically around 7:00 to 7:30 PM during summer and 6:00 to 6:30 PM during winter.

**Q: Is the temple open on festivals?**
Yes, the temple is open throughout the day and night during major festivals like Mahashivratri and Karthik Purnima.
