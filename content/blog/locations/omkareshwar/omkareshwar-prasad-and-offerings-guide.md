## Darshan and visit planning checklist

1. Check the daily darshan timings on the official board.
2. Wear modest and comfortable clothing.
3. Use the free locker facility for your footwear.
4. Join the queue early to avoid the peak heat.

## Location-specific tips for devotees

The morning aarti is a deeply spiritual experience; try to arrive by 4:30 AM for the best chance to participate.

Families visiting Omkareshwar often benefit from arriving early to avoid peak queues, especially on weekends and festival days. The temple complex and accommodation areas are well-maintained, with clear signage and helpful staff. Keep a copy of your booking confirmation handy, and carry light snacks and water for children.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the check-out policy at Shegaon?** Most Sansthan accommodations follow a 24-hour check-out cycle from the time of entry, allowing devotees flexibility based on their arrival.
