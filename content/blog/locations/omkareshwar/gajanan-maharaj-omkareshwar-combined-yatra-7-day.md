The Shegaon Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan and the Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas are both run by the same trust — the Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan, Shegaon. The combined yatra has been the single most-booked package at our central booking desk in 2024-2025, and the desk has now confirmed 380+ combined bookings in that window. What follows is the actual 7-day plan the office recommends, with the cost breakdown, the routing options, and the room inventory at both Sansthan properties.

The reason this combined yatra exists in our booking system is that families visiting one Sansthan property almost always ask about the other. Shegaon is in Maharashtra, Omkareshwar is in Madhya Pradesh — 360 km apart — and the darshans at the two Sansthan properties are very different. Shegaon is the main Gajanan Maharaj temple with the Sansthan headquarters. Omkareshwar is the Jyotirlinga island temple the Sansthan supports with the Bhakta Niwas accommodation. Doing both in one trip is the right move for serious devotees.

## The 7-day itinerary the office actually books

**Days 1-3: Shegaon Sansthan**
- Day 1: arrive at Shegaon Sansthan by 4 PM, check in to the Sansthan accommodation (the Sansthan runs a guest house with 80 rooms), settle in
- Day 2: Gajanan Maharaj main temple darshan (5 AM kakad aarti, 9 AM Madhyan, 6 PM Sandhya), afternoon visit to the Sansthan's heritage building and the old Gajanan Maharaj pada site
- Day 3: Anandsagar visit (the Sansthan's lake complex), afternoon shopping at the Sansthan-run bookstall, evening darshan

**Day 4: Travel day (Shegaon → Omkareshwar)**
- 7 AM breakfast at the Shegaon Sansthan
- 8 AM depart by reserved taxi (the Sansthan desk arranges this for ₹5,500 sedan / ₹8,200 SUV)
- 4 PM arrive at Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas
- 5:30 PM walk to the main Omkareshwar temple for Sandhya aarti
- 8 PM dinner at the Bhakta Niwas dining hall

**Days 5-6: Omkareshwar**
- Day 5: 4:30 AM kakad aarti, parikrama, Mamleshwar darshan, Madhyan aarti, Anand Sagar
- Day 6: full Omkareshwar day + Bhakta Niwas rest

**Day 7: Departure**
- 5:30 AM quiet darshan at the main Omkareshwar temple
- 7:30 AM breakfast
- 9 AM pack, check out
- 10 AM depart for Indore (4 hours, shared jeep ₹400 per seat or reserved sedan ₹1,800)
- 2 PM arrive Indore, fly home / train home

The desk can also arrange the reverse — Omkareshwar first, then Shegaon — but the office finds the Shegaon-first direction is easier for families flying in via Mumbai or Pune (closer airports, fewer transfers).

## The Shegaon → Omkareshwar route — three options

### Option 1: Direct reserved taxi (recommended)

The Sansthan desk arranges this for ₹5,500 sedan or ₹8,200 SUV in 2026. The 7-8 hour drive is broken at Dhule or Sendhwa (the desk recommends Sendhwa — the highway dhabas are cleaner). This is what the office books for 80% of combined yatra families.

### Option 2: Train via Khandwa Junction

Shegaon has a railway station (Shegaon, code SEG) on the Howrah-Nagpur-Mumbai line. The 2026 route is:
- Shegaon → Khandwa Junction: 4-5 hours, MEMU or passenger train
- Khandwa → Omkareshwar Road station: 1.5 hours, MEMU
- Omkareshwar Road → Omkareshwar Bhakta Niwas: shared jeep ₹30 per seat

The total transit time is 7-8 hours, the same as the direct taxi, but it is cheaper (₹250 per person in 2026) and you avoid the road. The office recommends this for solo devotees.

### Option 3: Break in Indore (for families with elderly members)

Some families break the journey with a night in Indore. The route is:
- Day 4 morning: Shegaon → Indore (6 hours by taxi)
- Day 4 night: Indore (any hotel, the office can recommend 3 near the railway station)
- Day 5 morning: Indore → Omkareshwar (2 hours)
- Day 5 evening: arrive at Bhakta Niwas, freshen up, walk to the temple for Sandhya aarti

The break adds a day but is the right move for families with members over 70. The office books this for about 12% of combined-yatra families.
