# Pandharpur How to Book Bhakta Niwas

Every pilgrimage has a critical logistics question, and for the warkari journey to Pandharpur, that question is almost always about accommodation. The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan runs a Bhakta Niwas near Sant Kaikadi Maharaj Math specifically to address this need, but the booking process is not as straightforward as selecting dates on a hotel website. It involves a few specific steps, a token payment, and some timing considerations that make the difference between securing a room and arriving without a place to stay.

This guide explains the complete booking process — from the first phone call or WhatsApp message to collecting your room key at the counter — and covers the timing strategies that improve your chances, particularly during Ashadhi Ekadashi and Kartik Ekadashi.

## Before you book: understand the eligibility rules

The Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas is not open to every traveller who calls. There are specific eligibility criteria that govern room allocation:

- **Family-only allocation:** Private rooms are reserved for families. Single travellers or groups of friends are typically directed to the hall dormitory instead.
- **Minimum occupancy:** A minimum of three family members is often required to book a private room. Smaller family units may be offered the hall or a smaller room depending on availability.
- **Valid photo ID:** Every adult guest must present a government-issued photo ID — Aadhaar card, voter ID, PAN card, or passport — at the time of check-in. This is non-negotiable and strictly enforced.
- **Marriage proof for couples:** Married couples must be able to show proof of marriage if requested. Unmarried couples are not permitted in family rooms.

These rules are in place to ensure that the limited accommodation reaches genuine pilgrim families who need it most. If you are travelling solo, the hall dormitory is the appropriate option, and it is a comfortable and affordable choice for many devotees.

## Step 1: Decide your room type

The Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas offers four main categories:

- **2 Bed Non-AC** — around Rs 1,250 per night, suitable for couples or small families during cooler months
- **3 Bed Non-AC** — around Rs 1,750 per night, the most commonly booked category for small families
- **4 Bed Non-AC** — around Rs 2,250 per night, ideal for larger families of four
- **4 Bed AC** — around Rs 2,550 per night, the preferred option during summer when temperatures exceed 40 degrees Celsius

All prices include all meals, taxes, and hot water. There are no hidden charges. For a detailed breakdown of what each room type includes, see the [Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas facilities and amenities guide](/blog/locations/pandharpur/pandharpur-bhakta-niwas-facilities-and-amenities).

If you are travelling with a group larger than four, the hall facility with a capacity of twenty is the right option. The hall is economical for community groups and is commonly booked by dindi organisers during Ashadhi Ekadashi.

## Step 2: Contact the Sansthan

You can reach the Sansthan accommodation desk through three channels:

**Phone:** Call the Shegaon office number during business hours. The same number handles queries for all Sansthan locations, including Pandharpur. Mention that you need accommodation at Pandharpur specifically. Have your group details ready before you call — names, arrival date, number of nights, and room preference. The staff will check availability and call you back with confirmation. The [Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas Sheogaon Contact](/blog/locations/pandharpur/pandharpur-bhakta-niwas-sheogaon-contact) guide has the full contact details.

**WhatsApp:** Send a message with the same information. WhatsApp is increasingly the preferred channel because you can send your request at any time and receive a response when the team is available, without waiting on hold. The [Phone and WhatsApp Booking Best Practices](/blog/guides/phone-and-whatsapp-booking-best-practices) guide explains how to format your message for the fastest response.

**Online booking form:** Submit your request through the [accommodation booking page](/booking) on this website. This creates a written record of your enquiry and is useful if you are planning from abroad or want to track your request over time.

## Step 3: Confirm and pay the token

Once the staff confirm availability, you will need to pay a token amount to hold the room. The token is typically between Rs 200 and Rs 500, depending on the room type. It is not the full payment — the balance is settled at check-in.

Payment is usually accepted via bank transfer or UPI. After transferring the token amount, send a screenshot of the transaction confirmation to the Sansthan via WhatsApp or email. The staff will send you a booking confirmation with your name, room type, dates, and token amount. Keep this confirmation safe — you will need to show it at the counter on arrival.

During Ashadhi Ekadashi and Kartik Ekadashi, the Sansthan may require a larger advance or even full payment at the time of booking. This is because the volume of last-minute cancellations during festival periods is high, and the Sansthan needs to protect against rooms going unoccupied while genuine devotees are waiting.

## Step 4: Arrive and collect your room

On the day of arrival, go directly to the Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas counter near Sant Kaikadi Maharaj Math. Bring:

- The original booking confirmation
- A printed or digital copy of your payment receipt
- Valid photo ID for every adult guest
- The token payment confirmation, if not already acknowledged

The staff will verify your booking, collect the remaining balance, and hand over your room key. You will also receive a brief orientation covering meal timings, hot water slots, and any specific instructions for your stay.

If you arrive outside normal hours, call the Shegaon office in advance so that the night supervisor at the Pandharpur property is aware of your arrival.

## Timing strategies for festival bookings

The single most important factor in securing a room at the Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas is timing. Here is how to approach it:

**For Ashadhi Ekadashi (July):** Book at least four to six weeks before the festival. The Bhakta Niwas typically sells out well in advance. If you are flexible with dates, arriving one or two days before the main festival day gives you a better chance of availability than arriving on the day itself. The [Ashadhi Ekadashi Pandharpur Wari guide](/blog/events/ashadhi-ekadashi-pandharpur-wari-guide) explains the festival schedule and what to expect.

**For Kartik Ekadashi (November):** This is the second-busiest period at Pandharpur. Follow the same four to six week advance booking window. Book early, confirm in writing, and pay the token promptly to avoid losing your allocation.

**For off-peak months:** Booking one to two weeks in advance is usually sufficient, but calling earlier never hurts, especially if you need a specific room type or ground-floor accommodation.

## What to do if rooms are fully booked

If the Pandharpur Bhakta Niwas is fully booked for your dates, you have several options:

- Ask the Sansthan staff to add you to the waitlist. Cancellations do happen, especially a few days before the festival.
- Check if rooms are available at Anand Vihar in Shegaon and plan a day trip to Pandharpur.
- Look for accommodation in nearby towns and commute to the temple daily.
- Contact the [Sansthan support team](/contact) for assistance with alternative arrangements.

## Understanding the token payment system

The token payment is a cornerstone of the Sansthan's booking model, and understanding how it works prevents most of the confusion that pilgrims encounter. The token amount — typically between Rs 200 and Rs 500 — is not a deposit in the conventional sense. It is a commitment from your side that you intend to honour the booking, and it allows the Sansthan to hold the room for you rather than releasing it to someone on the waitlist.

The token is deducted from the final bill at check-in. It is not an additional charge. What this means in practice is that the total amount you pay over the course of your stay is simply the room tariff multiplied by the number of nights, minus the token you have already paid. There is no separate "cancellation fee" beyond the forfeiture of the token if you cancel within the notice period.

If you need to cancel your booking, call the Shegaon office and quote your booking reference. The staff will process the cancellation and confirm whether your token is refundable. For regular bookings, cancellations made more than seven days before arrival typically qualify for a full token refund. During festival periods, the token is usually non-refundable because the room could not be reallocated in time.

## Booking for different travel scenarios

**Solo warkari on a padyatra:** If you are walking to Pandharpur as part of a dindi, the hall dormitory is your accommodation option. Book it through the same channels — phone, WhatsApp, or the online form — and mention that you are a solo traveller so that the staff can place you in the hall rather than attempting to allocate a private room. The [Pandharpur solo traveler guide](/blog/locations/pandharpur/pandharpur-solo-traveler-guide) has detailed advice for warkaris making the journey on foot.

**Multi-generational family group:** If you are travelling with grandparents, parents, and children, consider booking multiple adjacent rooms or the hall facility. The hall is particularly suitable for large families because it keeps everyone together in a single shared space with access to the same dining and bathroom facilities. Request ground-floor rooms if any member of your group has mobility difficulties.

**Group or dindi organisers:** If you are coordinating accommodation for a group of ten or more, call the Shegaon office directly and speak to the group booking coordinator. They can reserve a block of rooms or the hall facility and provide a consolidated tariff. Group bookings during Ashadhi Ekadashi require significantly more advance notice — aim for at least eight to ten weeks before the festival.

## After check-in: settling into your room

Once you have your room key, take a few minutes to orient yourself. Note the location of the dining hall, the hot water timing board, the emergency exit, and the night supervisor's contact number. If you notice any issues with the room — a leaky tap, a broken fan, a bedding problem — report it to the accommodation desk immediately rather than waiting until the next morning. The staff are generally responsive and will try to fix the issue or move you to another room if needed.

Your room is available until 10:00 am on your departure day. If you need to store your luggage after checking out, the accommodation desk can hold it for you until you are ready to leave for the bus stand or railway station. This is particularly useful on the day of your departure, when you may want to visit the temple one final time before leaving Pandharpur.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What is the minimum number of people required to book a private room?**
A: A minimum of three family members is typically required for a private room. Solo travellers and smaller groups are accommodated in the hall dormitory.

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
A: For Ashadhi Ekadashi and Kartik Ekadashi, book at least four to six weeks in advance. For regular days, one to two weeks is usually sufficient.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted for the token amount?**
A: Bank transfer and UPI are the most common methods. After paying, send a screenshot of the transaction confirmation to the Sansthan team via WhatsApp.

**Q: Can I modify my booking after confirmation?**
A: Yes. Contact the Shegaon office by phone or WhatsApp with your booking reference number, and the staff will assist with changes to your dates or room type, subject to availability.

**Q: Is there a cancellation fee?**
A: Cancellations more than seven days before arrival usually qualify for a full refund of the token amount. During festival periods, the token is typically non-refundable. Confirm the exact terms at the time of booking.

**Q: What if I lose my booking confirmation?**
A: Call or WhatsApp the Shegahan office with your name and the approximate date of your booking. The staff can look up your reservation in their system using your name and ID details.
