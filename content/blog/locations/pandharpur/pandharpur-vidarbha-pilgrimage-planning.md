## About Pandharpur and what makes it distinctive

Pandharpur is a major temple town in the Solapur district of Maharashtra, perched on the south bank of the Bhima river, which is known in its lower stretch as the Chandrabhaga. The town lies on the Deccan plateau, surrounded by a flat to gently rolling landscape of jowar, sugarcane, and bajra fields, and it sits roughly 375 kilometres southeast of Mumbai and 210 kilometres east of Pune by road. The Chandrabhaga at Pandharpur flows from west to east and is wide enough in the dry months to expose long sand banks that are used for the morning bath before darshan.
